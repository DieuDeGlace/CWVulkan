# Ordinary Steam World-step phase order

This is a bounded original-Steam-only behavioral contract for RVA 0x2DA6C0 and the directly inspected ordinary host and viewed callers. The supported x64 image is binary/cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe under C:/Users/DieuDeGlace/Documents/decompiled cube world steam, image base 0x140000000, timestamp 0x5D93AB0D, SHA256 7AF18CB28C8BF848F355287428C6390B2EA2A682CA466190F5EB9859C5D6F244. The image hash was checked for this audit. All addresses below are original image RVAs.

Evidence was selected by filename before content inspection. The principal originals are decompiled/functions/1402da6c0.c, 1402e2e80.c, 1402adfe0.c, 14005ef10.c, 1402e3510.c, 1402e17d0.c, 1402a56c0.c, 1402d2eb0.c, 1402c34c0.c, 1402dba40.c and 1402e1750.c, with scoped checks in the original decompiled/assembly.txt. Outer-boundary evidence is 140083e60.c, 1400f5520.c and decompiled/retry-180-phase1/functions/1400a5a50.c. Direct support routines inspected are 1402abb70.c, 1402b9890.c, 1402e30b0.c, 1402b68e0.c, 140083640.c, 14003ac90.c, 14003b420.c and 1400a1680.c; selected portions of 140115df0.c and 140131620.c establish the final optional-owner call boundary without a general audit of their gameplay effects. Numerical constants were checked in the original image. These files are original decompiler/listing evidence, not replacement code.

One invocation of 0x2DA6C0 receives a World pointer and a single-precision elapsed value in milliseconds. It preserves that value at 0x2DA721 and passes the same value to the reached elapsed-taking phases. The inspected World-step body contains no elapsed subdivision, upper cap, lower clamp or zero-elapsed early return. Its order is therefore the order of one invocation, including a final remainder invocation made by an outer caller. The ordinary nonnegative finite cases are described below; arbitrary invalid floating inputs and arithmetic overflow are not characterized.

Time/day processing comes first, through 0x2E2E80 at call site 0x2DA728. That routine first scans the current live-resident list at World+0x40. A resident with byte+0x60 zero, byte+0x68 equal to 0x58, and float+0x180 strictly positive selects the faster time rate. If such a resident is the pointer at World+0x448, the routine also changes that resident's float+0x180 through calls to 0x5FAC0 and an upper comparison against that helper's result. This establishes an early actor-state mutation; the helper's wider meaning is not needed here.

The time routine then adds elapsed milliseconds to the float at World+0x440. It advances the float time-of-day at World+0xC by ten units per elapsed millisecond ordinarily, or one hundred when the qualifying resident scan succeeded. Those factors are original floats at 0x42E598 and 0x42E5BC. If pointer World+0x210 is null, it replaces time-of-day with 32,400,000 after the addition. It then repeatedly advances the 32-bit day counter at World+0x8 and subtracts 86,400,000 while time-of-day is strictly greater than that threshold; exact equality does not advance the day in this routine. The threshold is the original float at 0x438028.

At each day wrap, World byte+0x358 is tested separately. When it is zero, 0x2E2E80 calls 0x2D2EB0, then 0x2C34C0, and appends an integer-zero record to World+0x160. The reset calls are at 0x2E300D and 0x2E3015. In the directly inspected reset bodies, 0x2D2EB0 deletes residents whose byte+0x60 is nonzero from World+0x40, rebuilds identity lookup World+0x450 from the survivors, and clears selected-pointer list World+0x50. Routine 0x2C34C0 clears World+0x20 membership and the retained-state map at World+0x10, with additional optional-owner collection changes. Thus these day-wrap effects finish before the main step rebuilds its selected pointers, prepares source summaries, or admits new residents. This is not physical loaded-source required-set removal.

The next call, 0x2ADFE0 at 0x2DA730, clears the nodes and count of World+0x50, scans World+0x40 in forward list order, and appends raw resident pointers for which 0x5EF10 succeeds. The directly inspected predicate requires bit 0x800000 in the resident's 32-bit field+0x80 and a strictly positive float+0x180. It excludes the combination of byte+0x60 equal to 8, 32-bit code+0x64 in the inclusive range 244 through 247, and nonzero byte+0x19C. The predicate supplies no independently proved business name for this list. Rebuilding it here is separate from later live-resident admission and removal; the main step does not treat World+0x50 as its traversal list for the later all-resident update.

Routine 0x2E3510 is then called at 0x2DA73C with the preserved elapsed float. It includes optional-owner work and actor/source preparation as well as the selected-source route. That route collects existing nonnull loaded source pointers from inclusive seven-by-seven zone neighborhoods around the inspected byte+0x60-zero residents and deduplicates source pointers. Its summary pass clears and rebuilds each selected source's keyed counters at source+0x4D8 and 32-bit count at source+0x490 from the reached template and identity collections, with World+0x20 membership tests. Those selected-source summaries are rebuilt before the later passes over selected source object arrays at source+0x30 and source+0x60. Some reached object mutations are themselves gated by zero World+0x358 and can mark source membership in World+0x1A0. These are template/identity summaries, not a census of the live residents newly admitted later in this step. This audit does not claim that the seven-by-seven route exhausts every operation or source query inside 0x2E3510.

After 0x2E3510 returns, a first optional-owner phase runs only when World+0x448 is nonnull. It follows owner+0x1528 without a separate null test in this reached block, subtracts the elapsed float from stored floats at tree-node+0x24 in the tree rooted at secondary+0xE8, conditionally builds a temporary tree from entries with positive updated values, and replaces the original tree from that temporary collection. The decrement and positive test are at 0x2DA796 through 0x2DA7A9; replacement calls are at 0x2DA84E and 0x2DA861. This phase precedes resident admission. It is not gated by World+0x210 or by the broader owner-mode admission below.

The first main-body test of World byte+0x358 occurs at 0x2DA8CE. Zero admits an ordered group: 0x2E17D0 at 0x2DA8DF, then 0x2A56C0 at 0x2DA8E7, then 0x2A45E0 at 0x2DA8F3. The first routine contains its own zero-byte admission and constructs/registers admitted live residents from reached loaded-source/template data; the registration call is 0x29D0D0, reached at 0x2E283A. The second routine removes qualifying nonzero-byte+0x60 residents outside every admitted byte+0x60-zero centre's inclusive horizontal 320-block disk. It updates World+0x40 and identity map World+0x450 and destroys selected residents. There is no vertical-distance read in that retention decision. Its centres come from World+0x40, rather than requiring the optional owner pointer. Consequently admission precedes this live-resident distance retention and both precede the later per-resident update passes. The role of 0x2A45E0 is left operationally unnamed here. The single main-body byte test admits the whole three-call group; it is not a fresh test between each call.

Routine 0x2E0DB0 follows at 0x2DA8FB regardless of that main-body zero-byte admission. If World+0x448 is nonnull, 0x66850 is then called on the owner at 0x2DA90C. These call positions are established, but their wider roles are not assigned by this audit.

The broader optional-owner block follows. It requires a nonnull World+0x448, nonnull World+0x210, and an owner byte+0x68 other than 0x98, 0x9C or 0x9D. In particular 0x99 is admitted. These are the concrete tests at 0x2DA911 through 0x2DA93D, not inferred global pause or role labels. Within this block, owner-secondary collections and value records are updated from coordinate and loaded-source queries. The reached code resets records at secondary+0x148 and secondary+0x1E8 and the value at secondary+0x288 before later conditional replacement; it also subtracts elapsed from secondary float+0x28C, clamps nonpositive results to zero, and can clear that float on its reached position test. These operations finish before the following complete resident flag pass. Their gameplay meanings and the full subsidiary action/lookup routes are deliberately unresolved. Failure of this block's admission skips this block, not the rest of the World step.

The resident portion has two distinct forward traversals of World+0x40. The first, at 0x2DB649 through 0x2DB672, clears bit 0x10 in every visited resident's 16-bit field+0x128. The complete clearing traversal ends before the next traversal starts. This is in addition to earlier flag operations inside 0x2E3510, whose inspected actor pass clears bits 0x0002 and 0x2000 in the same 16-bit field. Neither operation establishes a business label for those bits.

For each resident visited by the second traversal, a strictly positive float+0x180 admits, in order, 0x2BB8E0 at 0x2DB69C, 0x2C5250 at 0x2DB6AB and 0x2C3710 at 0x2DB6BA. All receive World, that resident, and the preserved elapsed float. This positive test is made once for the three-call sequence; the caller does not retest the field between those calls. Routine 0x2DE4C0 then runs for that resident at 0x2DB6C9 regardless of the positive test. The four-call ordering is per resident: this is not four whole-list passes. Their finer AI, movement, combat or animation roles have not been independently established in this bounded audit and remain unnamed. The caller follows the current linked list, not a copied resident snapshot; child-call mutation and alternate ownership guarantees remain open.

A second main-body test of World byte+0x358 follows the resident traversal at 0x2DB6D6. When zero, the step scans World+0x160 in forward order. The first record with a 32-bit payload equal to zero causes 0x2D2EB0 and then 0x2C34C0 at 0x2DB703 and 0x2DB70B, and the scan stops. Thus a zero record appended by an earlier day wrap can reach this later reset point after source work, admission/retention and resident updates. The scan itself neither erases that record nor pops all matching records. If an integer-zero record remains present on a later step, this same scan can trigger again; this statement is conditional and is not an absence-of-other-writers claim.

After that scan, a nonnull optional owner admits coordinate-pair work. The step reaches 0x2ADA20 at 0x2DB80B and writes the returned pair into owner+0x150 and owner+0x154. Only after these writes do owner modes 0x9C and 0x9D skip the subsequent pair-list operation at secondary+0x118. The list route can remove a previously matching pair before calling 0x2D0610 at 0x2DB927. This owner phase has no World+0x210 test at its entry. Its existence does not prove a general travel, discovery or UI interpretation.

The step next calls 0x2DBA40 with elapsed at 0x2DB933. The directly inspected portions establish a separate traversal of World+0x60 records, updates to their elapsed-dependent fields, resident identity lookups through World+0x450, and branch-dependent additions to outgoing collections, including World+0xC0. Selected World+0x60 nodes are collected temporarily and removed after that traversal, with its count updated and node cleanup performed. This establishes a later list-processing phase and a deferred-removal boundary within that routine; a universal gameplay name and the full branch semantics of every record type are left unresolved.

After that routine returns, the main step scans the current World+0xC0 edit records in forward order. For each record it derives a zone from the signed 32-bit horizontal block coordinates using floor division by 64, looks up World+0x460 at 0x2DB9AD, and admits only an existing nonnull source. It appends the record to that source's history at source+0xD8 through 0xA1680 at 0x2DB9D4. The inspected append helper increases the list count and links a new tail node; this route supplies no deduplication test. The scan does not erase its World+0xC0 input records. Therefore an edit still present in that collection can be appended again on another step before an outer clearing boundary; no claim is made that every child or alternate caller preserves it.

The final main-step call is 0x2E1750 at 0x2DB9E8. Its body admits work only when World+0x448 is nonnull, creates a temporary value through 0x115DF0 using World and owner+0x150, passes it to 0x131620 with World, and destroys the temporary through 0x82450. The inspected entry of 0x131620 skips its body for owner mode 0x9C. Its wider source/resident effects are not assigned a business label here. In particular this final call is not identified as the outer outgoing-aggregate clear.

The ordinary host caller is 0xF5520, reached by the clock loop at 0x83E60 using elapsed differences from the millisecond timer. It treats its input as signed 32-bit elapsed milliseconds and caps the upper value at 1000; the inspected comparison does not impose a lower bound. After entering World+0x3C8 and applying the reached incoming aggregate through 0x29D980 at 0xF68DD, it runs as many 16-millisecond World steps as needed while leaving a final remainder no greater than 16 for ordinary nonnegative inputs. Full steps call 0x2DA6C0 at 0xF6917. A final remainder call at 0xF692D always occurs on this reached route, even when the remainder is zero. A positive exact multiple of 16 ends with a 16-millisecond final call. Thus 32 milliseconds gives two complete World steps, and the upper-capped 1000 milliseconds gives sixty-two 16-millisecond steps followed by one 8-millisecond step. These are arithmetic consequences of the original caller, not measured scheduling or thread-rate guarantees.

Only after those host substeps does 0xF5520 call 0x2B68E0 at 0xF693E with the total capped elapsed value, then 0x2E30B0 at 0xF6947. The former remains an operationally identified outer routine here. The latter publishes selected source-array changes: World+0x190 members have source byte+0xF9 set before source+0x30 records are copied into World+0xE0; World+0x1A0 members have source byte+0xFA set before source+0x60 records are copied into World+0xF0. After its reached processing, 0x2E30B0 clears both membership sets. This is a separate outer publication boundary, rather than a call made after each 0x2DA6C0 invocation.

The host then reaches its reply-building call 0xF33B0 at 0xF69B4. It replaces the World outgoing aggregate beginning at World+0x90 with a newly empty aggregate through 0x83640 at 0xF69E3 and destroys the displaced temporary aggregate before leaving the World guard at 0xF69F8. The inspected aggregate constructor, exchange and destructor at 0x3AC90, 0x83640 and 0x3B420 establish this empty replacement and collection cleanup. The aggregate includes the World+0xC0 edit list and World+0x160 integer-record list. These outer calls establish in-memory ordering; they do not establish transport success or disk completion.

The ordinary viewed frame at 0xA5A50 is distinct from this host loop and from a single World step. Its double elapsed input is upper-capped at 1000. Its reached step admission requires the 32-bit Game+0x206C value to equal World+0x220 and the result of 0x6DD20 on Game+0x2070 and World+0x200 to be nonzero. The wider meaning and all producers of those admission fields are outside this audit. On admission, it likewise calls 16-millisecond float steps at 0xA7328 while double remaining time exceeds 16, and narrows the final double remainder to float for 0xA734C. If admission fails, the World-step sequence is skipped while the frame continues.

Later in that viewed frame, 0x2B68E0 is called at 0xA7E80, selected received edit/coordinate/source-array application occurs in the reached outer path, and 0x2E30B0 is called at 0xA8498. The World outgoing aggregate is replaced with an empty aggregate at 0xADB0E before the World guard is left at 0xADB23. These are frame-level boundaries around other reached frame work, not additional phases inside 0x2DA6C0. This audit establishes their relative call positions without re-auditing the full viewed input/application or reply pipeline.

For the inspected ordinary host route, World is embedded at Host+0x8; the host holds Host+0x5F8 and enters World+0x3C8 through 0x2ABB70 at 0xF68CD before incoming application, all substeps and the listed publication/clearing calls. It leaves World through 0x2B9890 at 0xF69F8 before leaving the host guard. For the inspected ordinary viewed route, Game+0x8 supplies the World pointer; entry through 0x2ABB70 at 0xA6464 precedes its step admission and the listed frame boundaries, with the matching leave at 0xADB23. The wrappers at 0x2ABB70 and 0x2B9890 directly operate on World+0x3C8. The inspected 0x2DA6C0 body does not acquire that guard itself. These facts prove those ordinary owner scopes; they do not establish protection for every callee, alternate World-step caller, indirect writer or worker.

World+0x358 is preserved here as an exact zero/nonzero admission byte. Its value does not gate the entire World step, and no remote-only, host-only or global-pause meaning is inferred from the selected branches. World+0x448 is the optional owner pointer used by the stated blocks, not a prerequisite for all source selection, resident phases or collection processing. World+0x210 separately affects time replacement and one broad owner block. No constructor defaults or universal writer sets for those fields are established.
