# Steam Creature appearance record

This bounded native-only note supports cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe, 5,757,952 bytes, SHA-256 7AF18CB28C8BF848F355287428C6390B2EA2A682CA466190F5EB9859C5D6F244, using preferred image base 0x140000000. All routine and instruction addresses below are RVAs. Evidence consists of the original binary, original functions and retry-180-phase1 functions, assembly.txt, calls.csv, and function_ranges.csv. No other project prose, replacement code, SDK, tests, probes, owner outputs, or runtime execution supplied facts for this note. It describes layout and observed consumption, with unsupported meanings left unresolved.

The inline appearance record starts at Creature+0x78 and occupies 0xb0 bytes through Creature+0x127. Constructor RVA 0x4ce80 calls RVA 0x4d600 on Creature+0x10; that nested initializer calls RVA 0x4d1f0 on its own argument+0x68. Consequently the appearance initializer receives Creature+0x78. Offsets in the remainder of this note are Creature-relative unless explicitly described as record-relative. The initializer's last writes cover +0x120 through +0x127. The next two bytes at +0x128 are initialized separately by RVA 0x4d600 and are subsequently read and modified as a 16-bit state bitfield by physical routine RVA 0x2bb8e0. They are adjacent to, rather than part of, the 0xb0-byte appearance initializer's record.

At +0x78 and +0x79, initialization establishes a two-byte zero value, but its semantic purpose and signedness are unresolved. Bytes +0x7a, +0x7b, and +0x7c form a color triplet initialized to 255, 255, 255. Pose routine RVA 0x17ab0 reads these bytes as unsigned quantities, divides each by the original float constant 255.0 at image RVA 0x42e7d8, constructs three ordered floating color components, and combines them with the color argument on the rendering route for the model selected at +0x92. This establishes normalized color components in stored order; an independent proof of the final red/green/blue channel labels or a general color-space convention is outside this audit. The initializer does not establish values for +0x7d through +0x7f. Those three bytes have no proved semantic role here and must not be interpreted as another color component merely because of adjacency.

The 32-bit field at +0x80 is a flag word, initialized to zero. Look rebuild RVA 0x53e80 sets masks in it, frequently by preserving existing bits and adding selected masks. The pose and physical routines consult the word, including masks above the low byte. Thus observing a byte test at +0x80 does not reduce the field's width to one byte. Among bounded examples, mask 0x1 selects different paired placement calculations in the pose routine; mask 0x2 participates in alternate pose and physical paths; mask 0x400 affects a rendering parameter on the color-triplet route; mask 0x400000 selects another rendering parameter; mask 0x1000000 changes an equipment-derived rendering parameter; and mask 0x2000000 excludes the actor from a particular physical overlap path. These are branch effects, not asserted universal flag names. No complete flag taxonomy or statement that every unused bit is reserved is established.

Three consecutive 32-bit floats at +0x84, +0x88, and +0x8c are body-dimension or spatial-extent inputs, initialized individually to 1.0. They are not the three components of a model-transform scale. Physical RVA 0x2bb8e0 pairs +0x84 with the position coordinate at +0x10, +0x88 with +0x18, and +0x8c with +0x20 in spatial comparisons between creatures. It also derives physical quantities using these values, including inverse dependence on +0x84. Pose RVA 0x17ab0 uses +0x8c for vertical placement and uses differences and ratios involving these dimensions in additional pose calculations. Those roles establish physical dimensions, while the exact universal convention of full dimension versus half extent or radius, and a single units label covering every consumer, is not proved here.

RVA 0x53e80 receives an address of the creature's 32-bit category field and a separate appearance-record address, rather than requiring the Creature base address as its appearance argument. One concrete caller, RVA 0x7ea80 at callsite 0x7f642, passes Creature+0x64 and Creature+0x78. The rebuild routine begins by assigning the three record-relative floats at +0xc, +0x10, and +0x14, which correspond to Creature+0x84, +0x88, and +0x8c. Category branches then change dimensions and other record fields. Thus a record-relative offset in this routine must gain 0x78 before comparison with Creature-relative pose or physical accesses.

Eight consecutive signed 16-bit model identifiers occupy +0x90, +0x92, +0x94, +0x96, +0x98, +0x9a, +0x9c, and +0x9e. Every one is initialized to the bit pattern 0xffff. Pose RVA 0x17ab0 sign-extends each identifier, rejects negative values and values outside the available model sequence, and otherwise obtains a model pointer by indexing that sequence of eight-byte pointers. Therefore these fields are identifiers, not inline pointers or 32-bit identifiers; -1 is a supported absent value. Several slots serve as fallback models after an equipment-derived model lookup, so the identifiers alone do not determine every rendered part. A slot can also be used more than once in the resulting pose, as the route using +0x9c demonstrates. Anatomical labels for all eight slots, and a guarantee that every category interprets a slot identically, are not established by this bounded audit.

Eight consecutive 32-bit floats at +0xa0, +0xa4, +0xa8, +0xac, +0xb0, +0xb4, +0xb8, and +0xbc are separately loaded by the pose routine and participate in model-transform scaling. Their initializer values are respectively the float bit patterns 0x3f8147ae, 0x3f800000, 0x3f800000, 0x3f7ae148, 0x3f800000, 0x3f733333, 0x3f4ccccd, and 0x3f800000, approximately 1.01, 1, 1, 0.98, 1, 0.95, 0.8, and 1. Original instructions at pose RVA 0x18320 onward establish their independent loads even where the decompiled text omits floating call arguments. For example, the value from +0xa0 reaches all three axis arguments of scaling helper RVA 0x2b580 at pose callsite 0x27bf2, and +0xb0 likewise reaches all three axes at 0x284d3. The helper scales three matrix basis groups independently. This is an observed uniform per-part scaling use, distinct from the body extents at +0x84 through +0x8c; other pose branches can combine the stored values with animation-dependent terms. A universal one-to-one anatomical naming of the eight scalars is not claimed.

The following 32-bit float at +0xc0 is initialized to 1.0 and changed by some category branches of RVA 0x53e80. Its complete consumption and exact purpose remain unresolved here; adjacency to the preceding eight values does not prove another universally applied model scale. The float at +0xc4 is initialized to zero and has an established angular role: pose callsite 0x26235 passes it, combined with current pose state, to rotation helper RVA 0x2b360, and other model paths subtract it before rotation. That helper multiplies its angle argument by the original constant approximately 0.017453292 at image RVA 0x42e520 before sine and cosine. This establishes degrees for that rotation argument. The exact anatomical axis name for the appearance control is not assigned.

Six further 32-bit floats occupy +0xc8, +0xcc, +0xd0, +0xd4, +0xd8, and +0xdc, initially all zero. The first three are copied into paired pose-control triples in a particular pose state, with the latter two components negated for the paired triple. Rebuild branches assign values such as -45 and -90 among these controls. The float at +0xd4 is directly added to an angle passed to the arbitrary-axis rotation helper RVA 0x2b160 on the route using the +0x96 model slot. These observations support angular or pose-control participation for specific fields and paths, but do not prove a single six-value Euler-angle layout, fixed anatomical names, or complete consumption of +0xd8 and +0xdc. The latter two remain floats with observed initialization and rebuild writes whose complete role was not resolved.

The final 0x48 bytes comprise six consecutive triples of 32-bit floats. The triple +0xe0/+0xe4/+0xe8 starts at 0, 0, -5; +0xec/+0xf0/+0xf4 starts at 0, 0.5, 5; +0xf8/+0xfc/+0x100 starts at 6, 0, 0; +0x104/+0x108/+0x10c starts at 3, 1, -10.5; +0x110/+0x114/+0x118 starts at 0, -8, 2; and +0x11c/+0x120/+0x124 starts at 0, 0, 0. These defaults come directly from RVA 0x4d1f0, including wide stores covering adjacent float zeros. They do not imply that the triples are all positions in the same space.

Pose consumption establishes translation or placement use for the first five triples on particular paths. The +0xe0 triple is copied into a vector passed to translation helper RVA 0x2b780 for the model route whose fallback identifier is +0x98. The +0xec triple contributes directly to translation arguments for the +0x90 and +0x92 model routes. The +0xf8 triple participates in paired placement calculations with a sign change on the first component. The +0x104 triple likewise contributes to paired offsets, and the +0x110 triple reaches translation on the route using the +0x9a model slot. Helpers RVAs 0x2b780 and 0x2b7a0 establish translation by updating the matrix translation group from a supplied three-component vector. Pose state and category branches modify these values before use. Rebuild RVA 0x53e80 also writes the last triple at +0x11c through +0x124, but its complete consumer and semantic name were not established. No claim that the last triple is unused follows from the selected pose reads.

Appearance is inline scalar state, not an allocation-owning collection: the inspected initializer and consumers treat the covered bytes as values and model identifiers, while model pointer resolution occurs through a separate model collection. This does not prove that a Creature or resolved model remains alive for any caller-supplied interval. Look rebuild mutates existing appearance storage; it does not first reset the complete record through RVA 0x4d1f0. Some branches preserve or combine previous values, and some call imported rand. Its optional customization input can also update the category field. Consequently constructor defaults, a category value, and a rebuilt appearance are different lifecycle states; rebuild is not established as a pure decoder from category alone.

The inspected appearance initializer, look rebuild, pose, and physical routine bodies provide no direct acquisition of the World critical-section helpers or imported critical-section operations. This is a bounded direct-body observation, not a guarantee about every descendant, allocator, renderer, caller, or thread. Pose state, model availability, equipment, actor state outside this record, and physical state at +0x128 influence observed results. The full writer set, caller-held protection, concurrent publication, ownership of model resources, and exceptional paths are not resolved here. No capture policy, replacement representation, transitive lock-freedom, or universal quiescence requirement follows from this note.
