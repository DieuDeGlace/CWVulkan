# Steam zone history retention

Evidence is the original x64 Steam image `cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe`, SHA-256 `7AF18CB28C8BF848F355287428C6390B2EA2A682CA466190F5EB9859C5D6F244`, and its original instruction and routine exports. Addresses below are RVAs relative to `0x140000000`.

## The day on an edit and the daily reset

The four bytes at offset 24 of each 28-byte terrain edit record the world day when the edit was created. The resident-to-saved-zone copy, version-44 state writer, matching reader and fresh-zone replay all retain and process entries of any recorded day. These operations have no elapsed-day threshold, distance-from-player cutoff for individual edits, or limit that keeps only the newest edits. They preserve forward list order and duplicate positions. Evidence includes `0x2A4B85`, `0x2D3E20`, `0x299950`, `0x14AB50`, `0x148E90` and `0x2AE0C0`.

The day changes the *collection of saved zones*. In the clock update at `0x2E2E80`, a time of day strictly greater than 86,400,000 game milliseconds advances the day by one and subtracts 86,400,000. Equality alone leaves the day unchanged. The check repeats if needed. On the authoritative world branch, each crossing runs the creature reset at `0x2D2EB0`, the saved-state reset at `0x2C34C0`, then queues world event zero. Later in the same world update, a zero event runs those two resets again, once, before the pending terrain edits are routed to resident zones (`0x2DA6C0`, calls at `0x2DB703`, `0x2DB70B` and `0x2DB9D4`).

`0x2C34C0` clears the saved-zone map at world offset `+0x10`. The clear at `0x8A830` destroys every zone entry and its complete block history (`0x82970`), including entries with today's edits. It does not iterate the resident-zone map or undo live terrain. The day's reset alone therefore removes an unloaded zone's only in-memory saved history. A still-resident zone keeps its live edits and its ordered history at `+0xD8`. Storing that resident zone later can recreate the saved entry with older edits intact. Timing matters even for an edit made just before midnight: storing its zone before the clear and storing it after the clear can yield different next saves.

Innkeeper rest also advances the day. The conversation at `0x89CB0` accepts rest with at least ten coins at or before 06:00 or at or after 18:00. The corresponding interaction at `0x2C3710` checks the target's role `0x84` (Innkeeper, `0xB0680`) and the same inclusive time boundaries. It queues event zero, increments the day, and sets time to 07:00 (`0x2C4100`). The authoritative event pass above performs the clear. On receipt of a type-8 day/time update at `0x3E450`, a changed day invokes `0x2C34C0` before adopting the received day and time. An unchanged day in that message skips the clear.

## Travel, zone unload and replay

`0x2D3E20` stores a selected resident zone under its signed zone X/Y key. A nonempty block history suffices to create a saved entry. When an entry already exists, its entire saved history is replaced by the resident list, even if the new list is empty. Two changed-collection flags, a further nonempty collection or a nonzero zone counter can also cause a zone entry to exist. Emptying just the history of an existing entry does not remove the zone key. An entirely unmodified zone with no existing key need not create one.

Ordinary travel unloads resident zones and stores them. The single-zone selector at `0x2D2410` selects a zone outside the supplied radius in zone coordinates, stores it, removes it from the resident map, and destroys the resident object. It requires a score strictly above the radius squared. The score is squared distance, with four times the radius squared substituted when either coordinate differs by more than twice the radius. The worker at `0x842D0` makes eight selection attempts per such pass, with radius one greater than the larger of three and 30 percent of its integer distance setting rounded down. These are residency thresholds.

The host worker at `0xF2340` constructs a set of wanted zones around its active focus positions. Its search spans 270 blocks on either side of each focus in X and Y and accepts zone offsets with squared distance at most 25. `0x2D2670` selects residents absent from that set, removes each selected resident from the map, stores its state, then destroys the object. The all-zones unload at `0x2D2930` detaches the resident collection and stores each zone before destroying it. None of these storage paths discards a record because the player traveled a particular distance or the record was created on an earlier day.

`0x2AE0C0` returns an existing resident directly. For a newly generated zone on the local saved-state branch, an available saved entry restores its complete history to the resident and replays post-edit values in order. With the key absent after a daily clear, generated terrain has no saved block edits to replay. Leaving an area and returning during the same day does not by itself clear its saved history.

## Saving, loading and explicit resident undo

The host persistence worker at `0xF2340` takes a world-state snapshot under the world lock and writes after releasing the lock. Its timer becomes eligible when at least 60,001 milliseconds have elapsed since the preceding timer baseline. Its exit path also takes a snapshot and attempts a write. The writer at `0x2D38E0` requires a nonempty world identifier and writes the snapshot under the database key `state`. Its source is the saved-zone map at snapshot time. Taking this snapshot does not first gather every resident's history. A resident-only edit or a zone key cleared before the snapshot can consequently be absent. The daily clear itself does not immediately rewrite the database.

World initialization at `0x2B5F20` replaces the previous in-memory state with empty collections (`0x29BE90`) and initializes day 1, time 10:00. The local branch with a nonempty world identifier opens `Save/world.db` and attempts to load `state` (`0x2B98B0`). A version-44 state restores its saved day, time and zone entries. The reader rebuilds the zone map from the supplied state and retains every stored day on the block records. Ordinary loading of a valid state does not invoke the daily reset merely because the stored day differs from the initialization default.

The session transition at `0x3D820` has a separate undo operation on the connected game world. It resets its creature state (`0x2D2C00`), then `0x2DA090` visits resident zones. For each nonempty history it reapplies the old block values in reverse order, clears the resident list and marks the zone for refresh. It performs no edit-day comparison. This operation differs from the daily clear, which removes saved zone entries. The source paths above establish their distinct effects. They do not establish that a specific user's last live edit reached disk before a particular exit or interruption.
