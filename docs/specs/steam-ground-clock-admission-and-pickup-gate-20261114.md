# Steam ground clock admission and pickup gates

FINAL / STABLE / HELD · Original Steam Windows x64 · 2026-11-14

The installed Steam executable and archived original each contain 5,939,072 bytes and have SHA-256 `a1efbbd41eeb8df08395c4d6511194d416cb70a14054b2a2000b9f5cdbcfa65b`. The original unpacked image contains 5,757,952 bytes and has SHA-256 `7af18cb28c8bf848f355287428c6390b2ea2a682ca466190f5eb9859c5d6f244`. RVAs below are relative to its preferred image base `0x140000000`. W denotes the World being stepped. G denotes Game, whose World pointer is at G + `0x08`.

## Which ground records the World step visits

The step at `0x2DA6C0` passes its binary32 step duration in milliseconds to `0x2E3510` at `0x2DA73C`, before the per-creature interaction call at `0x2DB6BA`. This ground pass is reached on both the host and joined Game World step paths. Host update `0xF5520` steps its own World at `0xF6917` and `0xF692D`. On the admitted Game update path, `0xA7328` and `0xA734C` step the Game World. That caller requires the 32-bit values at W + `0x220` and G + `0x206C` to match and the strings at W + `0x200` and G + `0x2070` to compare equal at `0xA72F8`. Each caller splits positive elapsed time above 16 milliseconds into 16 ms steps and a last step of at most 16 ms. The host caps its input elapsed value at 1,000 milliseconds before using that split. The zone set is built anew on each step, and the timer math below uses that step’s duration.

1. The candidate sweep starts at the front of the World’s creature list at W + `0x40` and follows list order. A creature is a player candidate when its **one-byte field at creature + `0x60` is zero**. Each qualifying creature is also appended to a temporary player list in that order at `0x2E3C48`. Selection uses the whole creature list and the zero role byte. It includes eligible remote player creatures and zero-role creatures whose health at + `0x180` is zero. The Game’s distinguished local creature pointer at W + `0x448` is used elsewhere, including a separate earlier local search in this routine. That search does not populate the ground-clock zone set. The sweep uses the creature list independently of the filtered list at W + `0x50`.
2. For each candidate, signed 64-bit X and Y at creature + `0x10` and + `0x18` are converted to signed 32-bit block coordinates by flooring the value divided by 65,536. The zone coordinate is the floor of that block coordinate divided by 64, including across negative boundaries. Z is immaterial. The search covers both offsets −3 through +3, inclusive, around the resulting zone coordinates, for 49 candidate coordinates per player in ordinary coordinate range. X is the outer increasing coordinate and Y is the inner increasing coordinate. There is no Euclidean or height test on this neighborhood.
3. Each pair is looked up in the World’s loaded-zone table at W + `0x460` through `0x12700`, with X in the low 32 bits and Y in the high 32 bits. A present entry supplies its 64-bit Zone pointer at entry + `0x18`. Only a present, non-null pointer enters the ground-zone set. Its key is the **Zone pointer value**, compared as unsigned 64-bit, at the insertion call `0x2E3DBA` through `0x73F40`. Overlapping player neighborhoods revisit lookups and coalesce identical pointers into one visit. The later traversal starts at the least key and walks in ascending unsigned pointer order. The X/Y lookup order and player-list order therefore do not assign the ground-zone processing order. The key is step-local, independent of player ID or distance.
4. For each selected Zone, a host-only prepass may mark creatures referenced by the zone’s separate list at Zone + `0x498`. At `0x2E4721–0x2E4765` this prepass requires W + `0x358` equal to zero. It resolves each listed actor ID and sets mask `0x0100` in the actor’s 16-bit field at + `0x128` when the actor exists and its + `0x60` byte differs from 8. Both World roles then visit the ground array beginning at Zone + `0x30` and ending at its one-past pointer at + `0x38`. The visit goes forward by `0xE0` bytes per slot, in array index order. An empty array has no visits. Every present slot reaches its clock checks, including a slot whose category is zero, quantity is zero, item is reserved, or item flags are set. There is no per-record range or ownership filter here. The only category check before the clock math selects the wall-snap behavior below. Evidence is `0x2E4710–0x2E4ACB`. This routine also processes a separate zone-object array at Zone + `0x60` through + `0x68` after that Zone’s ground array and before advancing to the next Zone. Its other object activity can consume CRT draws between different zones, including at `0x2E5D32`.

## Wall snap, timer, and sound order inside a slot

The wall-snap path runs only for ground category byte `0x11`. It probes one block away at the slot’s current Z in the order −Y, +Y, −X, +X using `0xEAB0` at `0x2E47C4`, `0x2E482C`, `0x2E4897), and `0x2E48FF`. The probed block qualifies when its byte at + `0x04` has any bit in mask `0xFD`. Each probe sees all preceding position changes. A qualifying probe snaps the tested axis within the block obtained by truncating its current signed fixed-point position toward zero in block units. The four snaps leave Z as stored.

| Qualifying probe | New coordinate on that axis | New binary32 field at ground + `0xC0` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| −Y | Block start plus 13,107 fixed-point units | 180.0 |
| +Y | Block end minus 13,107 fixed-point units | 0.0 |
| −X | Block start plus 13,107 fixed-point units | 90.0 |
| +X | Block end minus 13,107 fixed-point units | 0.0 |

A block contains 65,536 fixed-point units. Here the block end is the next block’s start. The offset is the truncation of the binary32 constant at `0x4725AC`. Multiple probes can qualify. The last successful probe writes the final + `0xC0` value. Ground X, Y, and Z are signed 64-bit fields at + `0xA0`, + `0xA8`, and + `0xB0`. Both World roles perform the wall-snap path. The table describes this pass’s coordinate and + `0xC0` field writes before sound placement.

After wall snap where applicable, the per-slot clock order is as follows.

1. If the signed 32-bit timer at ground + `0xCC` is positive, convert it to binary32, subtract the current binary32 step, truncate to signed 32-bit toward zero, clamp the result to a minimum of zero, and store it. An incoming zero or negative value skips that timer update. Evidence is `0x2E4942–0x2E4966`.
2. The binary32 landing delay at ground + `0xC8` is tested and aged independently. A strictly positive incoming value is reduced by the same binary32 step and the result is stored. A result at or below zero is then set to positive zero and runs the landing sound checks. An incoming zero or negative value skips the landing transition. Both roles perform these updates. The integer timer expires independently and is immaterial to the landing transition. The pass reads neither a wall-clock value nor the World’s calendar or accumulated time to decide whether a selected slot ages. An elapsed zero step can visit slots and do wall snap. Evidence is `0x2E4968–0x2E4996`. The ordinary drop creation path at `0x2A5B70` initially sets the landing delay to an integer value from 500 through 799 represented as binary32, using a CRT draw at `0x2A5CB3`. That draw is separate from sound.
3. At a landing crossing, an ordinary sound is appended only when the byte at W + `0x358` is zero. The constructor `0x29A130` initializes zero, the joined Game World receives one at `0x3DB2D`, and the host has a separate World embedded at host + `0x08` via `0xF17A0`. The host in the same process also uses its distinct World. The ordinary sound uses slot XYZ after wall snap, owner/exclusion zero, binary32 gain 1.0, and rate 1.0 plus a single CRT random draw scaled by 0.1 and divided by 32,767, with those operations in that order. Its code is `0x3D` (`drop-coin.wav`) for category `0x0C` and `0x3E` (`drop-item.wav`) for all others, including mana cube. The draw is at `0x2E49DE`, and the append to W + `0xA0` is at `0x2E4A25). Draw order across multiple ordinary landings in a step follows ascending Zone pointer, then increasing array index, restricted to crossings that pass the sound gate.
4. An additional equipment landing sound follows when the category is `0x03` through `0x09` or `0x1A`, W has a local creature, and the slot’s 64-bit reservation value at + `0xD8` equals that creature’s 64-bit + `0x968` value. It can run on either World role. It uses code `0x5D` (`craft-proc.wav`) at slot XYZ, rate and gain 1.0, and owner/exclusion zero. It consumes no CRT draw. Its append site is `0x2E4AB6`.

## Ordinary pickup request, kind 7

The later part of `0x2DA6C0` goes through W + `0x40` in list order. It runs the per-creature interaction at `0x2DB6BA` for actors with binary32 health at + `0x180` strictly above zero, after that actor’s earlier movement-related calls in the same step. The interaction routine at `0x2C3710` returns if the World role byte at + `0x358` is nonzero. With the host role, it reads the current creature’s request list at creature + `0xAB8` in list order. Request kind is the byte at entry + `0xD4`, including list links. The ordinary ground pickup kind is 7. Its full host admission before sound and receipt is as follows.

1. The request’s two 32-bit zone coordinates at entry + `0xB0` and + `0xB4` must resolve to a present, non-null loaded Zone through `0x16E00` at `0x2C3C68`. The signed 32-bit index at entry + `0xB8` must lie from zero through one less than the Zone’s current ground array length. The lookup uses the addressed slot in that current array. It does not require the Zone to have been selected by the earlier ground-clock pass.
2. The slot must be within 4 blocks of the acting creature’s current XYZ, inclusive. The three signed 64-bit coordinate differences are converted to binary32 and multiplied by 1/65,536. The sum of squared binary32 differences in X, Y, Z must be at most 16.0. It uses the current slot position and the actor’s current position after the preceding World phases. No occlusion query precedes this host acceptance.
3. The current slot contents must match the item identity included in the request. The predicate at `0x2C3D2F` through `0x4DF60` compares the category byte, the 32-bit fields at item + `0x04`, + `0x08`, + `0x0C`, + `0x10`, and + `0x18`, the bytes at + `0x14`, + `0x1C`, and + `0x9D`, and the active four-byte item detail entries starting at item + `0x1D` as counted by the + `0x9D` byte. That predicate covers item identity fields only. It omits the ground landing delay at + `0xC8`, the integer timer at + `0xCC`, the ground flags, owner value, and stack quantity. The containing host branch adds no nonempty-category, nonzero-quantity, ground-owner, inventory-capacity, or timer test. Zero, positive, and negative values of either ground timer leave the result of these host checks unchanged.

Immediately after admission, one CRT draw at `0x2C3D4E` gives the pickup sound its rate, with the same 0.1 scale and 32,767 divisor used for the ordinary landing rate. Gain is 1.0 and owner/exclusion is zero. The sound is normally `0x30` (`pickup.wav`) at the actor’s current position, including for ordinary coin and mana cube. The item with category `0x0B` and 32-bit subtype `0x25` uses code `0x2D` (`watersplash-hit.wav`) and doubles the rate. The sound goes to W + `0xA0` at `0x2C3DC4` before the receipt goes to W + `0x110` at `0x2C3EBC`. The receipt carries the actor ID, the item, the unsigned 16-bit quantity read at ground + `0xD4`, the slot XYZ, and flags from ground + `0xC4`. When flag mask `0x02` is clear, the host marks the zone for a ground update at `0x2C3F21) and clears the selected slot, including its category and both timers, by `0x2C3FB6` through `0x837E0`. With mask `0x02` set, it emits sound and receipt but leaves the slot in place. This flag test occurs after both events.

## Client selection and an early manual pickup

- The Game’s ground selection and automatic pickup pass is `0x8AE50`, reached at `0xA9B2C`. The nearby slot must first pass `0x2B8140` at `0x8D4DE`. That helper requires a nonzero category and a nonzero 32-bit field at ground + `0xD4`. With a local creature it also checks that the 64-bit reservation value at + `0xD8` is zero or equals local creature + `0x968`, and applies special-category eligibility to `0x17`, `0x18`, and `0x1E`. This helper has no landing or integer timer test. The outer distance threshold here is again at most 4 blocks in three dimensions.
- A slot of category `0x0C` or `0x0E` with flag mask `0x02` clear takes the automatic branch at `0x8D58E–0x8D5BF`. It queues kind 7 only when the local copy’s binary32 landing delay at + `0xC8` compares exactly equal to zero. Positive and negative nonzero values both fail that comparison. Those unflagged coin and mana-cube records stay on the automatic branch as they wait. The + `0xCC` timer is immaterial. Before the append at `0x8D701`, the automatic producer checks existing requests for identical kind, the same signed 32-bit zone X, zone Y, and slot index, and the same item identity. The duplicate check at `0x8D6D5` uses the same item predicate as the host.
- The same scan sends other eligible categories and records whose flag mask `0x02` is set to the manual selection branch. For a valid selected ground target, `0x2B8FD0` resolves the Zone and index again. The ordinary interaction action checks reservation and the local carry and inventory conditions. Upon the action’s input edge and with those checks passed, the Game copies the selected item identity and its zone X, zone Y, and index into a kind-7 request and appends it at `0x8E775`. Aim and proximity affect target selection. The manual scan, selected-target lookup, and enqueue have no condition on either ground timer.
- Consequently, a valid manual pickup can emit the ordinary pickup sound and receipt while the **host’s** landing delay is still positive, or while its integer timer is still positive. If the host clears that ordinary slot before its landing delay crosses zero, that recorded drop has no later landing crossing. If the host’s ground pass brings the delay to zero in the same step as pickup, the landing chime, where admitted, is appended before the pickup sound for that slot. The two sounds come from different events and use distinct draws. An ordinary unflagged coin or mana cube first waits for the client’s zero comparison on its automatic path. The host then applies its own kind-7 checks, which accept either timer state.

The contract covers the reached World ground pass, normal client selection, and the host’s kind-7 interaction. Zone pointer order is relative to the running original process. Allocation addresses, the phase of a particular client’s copy relative to the host, the precise focus ranking of manual candidates, other interaction kinds, and final receipt presentation are outside this trace.
