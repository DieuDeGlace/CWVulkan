# Steam ordinary hit amount and observable HP change

FINAL / STABLE / HELD. Original-side specification, 2026-11-18. The supported offline Steam x64 image is cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe, 5,757,952 bytes, SHA-256 7af18cb28c8bf848f355287428c6390b2ea2a682ca466190f5eb9859c5d6f244. Addresses are relative to that image. This contract follows finite gameplay values through hit production, same-call HP settlement and subsequent network receipt. All examples are deductions from the original native evidence.

## Calculating the ordinary amount

At RVA 0x29EB60 the caller supplies a floating-point damage amount, an attack category, a separate critical indicator, and source and target creatures. The amount at entry can already include an attack roll, charge or ability multiplier and critical damage. For example, the Water Bubble caller at 0x2A5274 doubles the bubble power on its critical draw and multiplies it by the bubble's remaining factor before entering the resolver. The ordinary resolver retains the critical indicator on its ordinary hit row. It does not apply a second universal critical multiplier. A displayed character stat alone does not establish the caller's rolled amount.

Use the following calculation once a non-null source and living target have reached the ordinary defense branch. Take the amount entering that branch, after any local-attacker bonus or positive shield overflow described below. Read the **attacker's signed 32-bit combo at creature offset 0x70**. Convert it and its positive threshold to single-precision floats, divide combo by threshold and cap the quotient above at 1. A negative quotient retains its value. Subtract the capped quotient from 1, multiply by the target's selected defense, and subtract the product from the entering amount. Floor the result at zero. These are separate single-precision operations in that order. The floor permits zero and fractional damage. There is no minimum one-damage rule. The resolver samples the combo before any post-hit changes by its caller. The signed conversion and upper cap are at 0x29F2F4–0x29F32C, the selection and deduction at 0x29F4EA–0x29F51F.

Attack category zero selects **physical armor** at 0x4F080. Every nonzero category entering this branch selects **magic resistance** at 0x64D90. The attack category used for defense is separate from the ordinary outcome byte zero at offset 0x45 in the eventual hit row. An ordinary physical hit and an ordinary magical hit can both have this outcome byte. The combo threshold comes from the source's equipment at 0x5F9E0. With category 3 in the source slot at 0x6D8, subtypes 3, 4 and 10 yield 50, subtype 6 yields 60, subtype 8 yields 80, subtypes 11 and 12 yield 20, subtypes 15, 16 and 17 yield 15, and the other subtypes yield 30. Without category 3 in that slot, the threshold is 15 if creature flag 0x10 at 0x80 is set, otherwise 30.

Both defense readers begin at zero and inspect in order the target's slots at 0x3B8, 0x458, 0x4F8 and 0x598, requiring item categories 4, 6, 5 and 7 respectively. Affiliation-0 targets also require class compatibility at 0x1094D0. Each included slot contributes its actual per-item physical or magic value, calculated by 0x108D50 or 0x10A1E0 respectively. If either mask 0x200 or 0x20000 at creature offset 0x80 is set, multiply the sum by 1.2 once. If 0x40000 is set, double the result. Presence of target status 0x0E then quarters it once. The first matching status 0x18 multiplies physical armor by that entry's potency, or the first matching status 0x19 multiplies magic resistance by its potency. Use the current float returned by the matching reader. A rounded UI value, unmodified equipment sum or value from the opposite category can give a different result.

## When the produced hit differs from that calculation

1. **Entry and defense classification.** Dead targets, affiliation 8 and target species 0x114 are rejected. A guest-side producer requires the local source and a nonlocal target. Source actions 0x4B and 0x64 require target flag 1 at 0x5C. A qualifying local attacker against a target whose species classifies as 6 at 0x65750 first multiplies the supplied amount by one plus the attacker's inventory-derived value from 0x510F0. The special outcome-3 check and enabled guard precede shield and ordinary projection. An active type-6 shield sum above zero queues a kind-5 hit of the full pre-armor amount. Only the excess over that pre-spending sum reaches the ordinary branch, and only if strictly positive. An absent source follows a separate path into row construction without either defense reader or the combo division. These paths are visible in 0x29EB60, including 0x29EC05–0x29ECC4, 0x29EFFB and 0x29F2C0–0x29F4EA.
2. **Source equipment after the floor.** The native code reads the raw signed combo/threshold ratio again. With a positive ratio and the hit's equipment-effect flag enabled, qualifying elements on the weapons returned by 0x4FD40 add to the target amount *after* the zero floor. The increment uses the uncapped ratio even when the defense deduction used a capped value. For each element, form 0.1 divided by the number of returned weapons. Element byte 0x80 adds this share times the ratio. Byte 0x81 adds half that product and accumulates twice the product as source healing. Bytes 0x82 and 0x83 each add one quarter of the share times the ratio and update separate timers. These contributions are accumulated over each matching element on each returned weapon. A zero from the defense deduction can therefore become positive. The visual-effect threshold strictly above 0.25 is separate from the strictly positive ratio used for these damage additions. See 0x29F607–0x29F975 and 0x2A4230.
3. **Target multipliers and separate source rows.** At ordinary row construction every target status of type 1 multiplies the working target amount by its own potency, in list order. There is no second ordinary defense subtraction or positive minimum after these multiplications. Independently, category 7 with a positive defense result can credit the source with 10 percent of that result. Weapon element 0x81 can credit its accumulated source healing, and source status 2 can credit half the post-element working amount, capped against max HP on that source-status path. Source heals have their own source-to-self event rows. They are distinct from target damage, and a list can contain them before the ordinary target hit. Action argument 0x12 can also install a timed type-4 status capable of generating later damage ticks. The initial ordinary hit and any later tick have separate amounts. See 0x29F52C onward, 0x29F975 onward, 0x29FCF6 through 0x2A0287 and 0x2DE4C0.

## From the hit row to HP

The ordinary producer queues a 72-byte hit with source and target IDs at offsets 0 and 8, the final signed float amount at 0x10, critical byte at 0x14 and kind at 0x45. The authority calls the common hit applicator at 0x2A0287 after queuing. A guest producer does not call that applicator there. Neither the return indicating that an ordinary hit was produced nor an animation, sound or queued row proves that this creature's HP changed in the producing call.

RVA 0x29DCF0 resolves the **target ID in the world's actor index** and requires a living target. A missing target or an affiliation-0 target in a world without a local-player pointer returns before HP settlement. Special hit kinds take other branches. A set 0x2000 flag at target offset 0x80 requires a positive target threat entry for a resolvable affiliation-0 actor and a resolved source of affiliation 0 or 6 for settlement to continue. That check follows ordinary nonnegative-hit combat bookkeeping. When the authority flag at world offset 0x358 is zero, or the target is the world's local creature, a positive ordinary hit debits HP only if 0x50520 accepts the target and also accepts the source when it resolved. It accepts affiliations 0, 1, 3 and 6, and affiliation 7 except species 0x8C, 0x8D and 0x8E. A missing source at this application gate does not by itself forbid the positive debit. A source present during *production* and a source resolved by ID at *application* are separate facts.

For an eligible positive ordinary hit, subtract the row's float amount from HP at target offset 0x180, round as single precision and store zero if the result is nonpositive. Thus overkill retains the full produced number in the row while the greatest observable loss is the target's initial HP. Rounding in the HP subtraction itself can also make the difference of two stored HP values slightly different from the record amount. Authority death processing runs on exact zero as well as underflow. It can also set a separately linked actor's HP to zero. Zero damage causes no ordinary HP debit despite the valid hit row. A negative ordinary row instead heals a living target, capped at maximum HP from 0x5FAC0. The positive damage floor described above belongs to ordinary defense production. An independently generated negative healing row enters through the signed event application path. See 0x29DCF0, in particular 0x29E0C7–0x29E5CD.

The separate Steam server world is created with a null local-player pointer (0xF17A0 and 0x29A130). Its ordinary record can target a remote affiliation-0 player even though that server's call does not debit the player's mirror. Source/origin and target ownership must be recorded for an HP comparison. When the local guest receives an ordinary kind-0 event in message 7, its guest frame at 0xA5A50 finds the target by ID. A living **local** target consumes the signed amount, floors negative resulting HP to zero and caps at maximum HP. The guest does not rerun combo, armor, or the authority's affiliation gates. Its local overkill sound branch tests strictly below zero. Identical ordinary rows execute in arrival-list order; later ordinary rows stop debiting after HP reaches zero. A **third-party** target on this guest can show a reaction without debiting its mirror's HP in that hit handler. Each incoming batch's status records are processed before its hit rows. Special kinds 1 and 5 perform local guard and shield settlement, while kind 6 skips ordinary local HP handling. Guest kind 3 can enter the signed-HP path despite authority kind 3 skipping ordinary settlement. See 0xA8445 and 0xA8B05 onward.

Actor-state message 3 supplies another route to a changed HP sample. For an incoming server-to-guest update, 0x3E450 excludes the guest's own actor ID and applies the actor fields to a remote actor. In the opposite direction, 0xF4490 accepts the state for the connected peer's own actor ID. The field decoder 0x138E00 copies four bytes to actor HP when update bit 29 is present. That is a replacement value. It does not identify the hit that produced it. World stepping, status ticks, healing, additional damage records, death and successive state messages can all occur between two samples on different frames or peers. These later writes cannot be used to explain a mismatch between two immediate same-call samples without evidence that they occurred inside the sampled interval.

## Numerical discriminators

Use distinct source and target actors with eligible affiliations, no guard, shield, special flags, bonuses, elemental additions or status multipliers. Take a settled authority target at 20 HP, rolled input 12, physical armor 8, magic resistance 4 and source threshold 20. At combo **-10**, the factor multiplying defense is 1.5. Physical yields a valid zero-amount ordinary row and HP stays 20. Magic yields amount 6 and HP 14. Clamping the negative combo to zero would incorrectly give 4 and 8. At combo **10**, physical yields 8 and magic yields 10. At combo **30**, each yields 12 because the upper cap is 1. Starting that last target at 5 HP yields a row of 12, HP zero, and an observed loss of 5. If the same target is an affiliation-0 guest player in the headless authority world, an ordinary row of 12 can coexist with server-mirror HP still at 5 in that call, followed by the owning guest going from 5 to zero on receipt.

A separate test distinguishes a correct defense calculation from a correct final row. Use physical input 12, armor 8, combo 10 and threshold 20 again. With one target type-1 status of potency 0.5, the defense result is 8 and the produced ordinary row is 4. From HP 20 an eligible authority target ends at 16. Alternatively, with no such status, input 1, armor 8, combo 10, threshold 20, the equipment-effect flag enabled and one returned weapon carrying one 0x80 element, the defense floor gives zero and the post-defense addition produces approximately 0.05 damage.

The input damage, attack category, combo, threshold and current defense determine the ordinary defense result when damage is measured at that branch, the category selects the supplied current defense, and the signed combo and threshold belong to the same source at that instant. They determine the **final target amount** when every later amount modifier is absent or already known and accounted for. They determine the **immediate target HP** when the identified record settles exactly once on that actor under the ownership and eligibility gates, with the stated starting HP and no other change in the interval. Equipment-derived defense inputs, intervening modifiers, record identity, ownership, sampling boundaries and independently arriving actor states cannot be inferred from the two HP readings alone.
