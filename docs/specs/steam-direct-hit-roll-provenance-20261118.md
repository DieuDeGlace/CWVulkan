# Steam direct-hit amount provenance

## Finding

An ordinary stock creature swing passes its active move number to the hit resolver. Its physical hits can carry a freshly rolled amount, but its move number is positive. On the supported dispatch paths, a resolver entry with **ability 0, damage category 0, a resolved attacking creature and no projectile context** has no established stock producer. If projectile context is present, a physical projectile can produce the two zeros, including one launched by a creature that has since returned to idle. With neither an attacking creature nor projectile context, two environmental hazards produce the two zeros. A resolved hit received from a peer is a different observation point.

Here, ability and category mean the two arguments supplied to the ordinary resolver at RVA 0x29EB60. The source creature's active move is a separate byte at creature offset 0x68. An ordinary outcome kind in a hit record is a third identifier. A missing value in an observation establishes none of these as zero.

## Stock entries with both arguments zero

**Projectile contact and area impact.** RVA 0x2DBA40 supplies ability 0 at both the contact call, 0x2DD0DF, and the impact-area call, 0x2DE162. Both supply the projectile payload as context, even when its owner fails to resolve. The first eight bytes of that payload identify its owner. Its float at 0x50 is the stored amount, its integer at 0x68 is the projectile kind, and its byte at 0x6C supplies the damage category. A zero category therefore survives either call. Contact can affect the initial victim. The area pass can affect a different eligible creature around the impact and skips the saved initial victim when present. Collision and ownership gates still apply.

For an owner that resolves, each of these impact paths can draw against that owner's critical chance for the particular target. A successful critical doubles the stored amount. With no owner the critical flag is clear. The contact path floors the forwarded amount at zero after this step. The area path forwards the resulting value directly. Neither collision calculation draws a new spread multiplier for its stored damage. Contact can make other random draws for resource gain, effects and sounds.

Several stock launches explain why a stored amount may nevertheless contain a roll. The action family dispatched at 0x2CEB05 includes 0x17, 0x18, 0x1B, 0x6C and 0xA0. It constructs a payload at the action time and queues it at 0x2CF06B. The ordinary 0x17 projectile retains the constructor's zero category. At launch, the action multiplier and effective physical attack are scaled using stored charge and an amplitude draw spanning 0.75 to 1.25. The charge coefficient and additive term vary within the family. For 0x17 with zero stored charge and zero positive cost, the additive term is one. For 0x18 it is zero. Action 0x1B makes kind 5, which has a timed contact path. Action 0xA0 explicitly sets category 6 and so falls outside the physical case. The same physical amplitude interval also appears in the separate release path queuing at 0x2CF5D9.

The release family beginning at 0x2CF5FA offers another example. Action 0x2F builds a kind-4 projectile with a launch-time amplitude draw, the action multiplier, effective physical attack and a further factor of five. The constructor starts its category at zero. Creature class byte 3 or 0x98 assigns category 1 or 2 according to specialization. For a release retaining category zero, kind 4 can run the area-impact scan. Stock payloads also exist with a deterministically scaled amount, for example the kind-6 field made by actions 0x62 and 0x63 from twice the action multiplier times the magic attack statistic. Those two fields have category 1 or 2. Stored damage by itself does not establish which release calculation produced it. Original payload defaults are at RVA 0x29A0B0.

**World-object damage.** The authority's object update at RVA 0x2E3510 calls the resolver at 0x2E5CF5 with no attacking creature, no projectile, ability 0 and critical false. Active type 0x12 supplies category 0 and one tenth of the target's effective maximum HP on crossing a 200 ms world-time bucket. Active type 0x13 supplies category 0 and multiplies that one-tenth amount by five, at the zero-time activation check. Both require a nearby eligible target. Type 0x11 shares the call but supplies category 1. These amounts are deterministic for the same type and effective maximum HP. An absent source takes a separate path through the resolver that bypasses attacker-combo armor deduction. The resolver can still consume random numbers for purposes other than damage spread.

## Direct creature attacks

The action dispatcher at RVA 0x2C5250 reads the active move before entering an attack worker. Its unsigned range check at 0x2C5309 and its two tables at 0x2D00DC and 0x2D0044 send actions 0 and 1 past the attack workers. Positive actions including 2, 3 and 4 enter the ordinary swing worker at 0x2C5CF0. A living, eligible hostile target in the strike window and within reach can reach 0x2C84FA. That call uses the source's current move as its ability, a null projectile context and category 0 for these three moves. Moves 0x1F and 0x20 instead set category 1, and 0x21 and 0x22 set category 2.

For an ordinary strike of move 2, 3 or 4, the per-target critical decision comes first. A set forced-critical byte at source offset 0xBB8 succeeds without the critical draw. Otherwise a random integer divided by 32767 must fall strictly below the current critical chance from RVA 0x50D30. The amplitude uses a separate integer from 0 through 32767. An integer of zero gives 1.25, an integer of 32767 gives 0.75, and the intervening values decrease linearly. The source multiplies this amplitude by the action multiplier from RVA 0x50F20, the effective physical attack from RVA 0x4FA70, and one plus five times the square of the stored charge at 0xBA8. A critical doubles the resulting amount before the call. The source performs single-precision arithmetic. The charge here is the value in use for the strike after action-specific preparation.

Some moves in this same worker use a different base. For 0x0C, 0x3D and 0x3F the additive charge term is zero. Move 0x16 applies another move-specific multiplier. Moves 0x60 and 0x61 retain physical category 0 while using the alternate attack statistic at RVA 0x65C70 and the action multiplier, with the same spread and no squared-charge term. The separate targeted move 0x8C at 0x2C8D14 delivers category 0 and ability 0x8C on its qualifying time-bucket crossing. It rolls the amplitude against its action multiplier and effective physical attack first, then rolls critical and doubles on success. Thus even a periodic physical creature attack can carry a fresh upstream roll and a nonzero ability.

## Other callers and peer delivery

The aimed attack at 0x2CC48D always supplies ability 0. The stock dispatch table admits 0x68 through 0x6B to its worker at 0x2CB299. The first two supply category 1 and the last two category 2, overridden to 7 for specialization byte 2. The worker contains a default category zero and a separate conditional calculation for active move 0x74. The stock table instead sends 0x74 to the ordinary swing worker. Likewise it sends 0x1D to its own handler. The default assignment inside the aimed worker provides no stock entry with both arguments zero on these dispatches. The separate attack at 0x2C9DB7 uses active move 0x88, ability argument 0, category 1 and a time-scaled, rolled magic amount.

The death burst at 0x2A5274 supplies ability 0 with category 2 for the hostile owned Water Bubble, species 0x11D. Its other damage branch supplies category 1. The status update at 0x2DF972 supplies ability 0, category 6 and the stored positive potency of status type 4 on a qualifying 400 ms crossing. None is a physical swing with ability zero.

An authority receiving message 10 at RVA 0xF4490 queues a 72-byte resolved hit. RVA 0x29D980 passes its stored row to the hit applier at 0x29DCF0. Guest message 7 likewise carries resolved hit rows, decoded at RVA 0x3E450 and consumed at RVA 0xA5A50. The hit row conveys source and target IDs, amount, critical and outcome information. It omits the producing move, damage-category argument, projectile context, pre-roll base and random draw. Applying that row does not rerun the producer, draw a new melee amplitude or repeat ordinary armor deduction. Message 13 is separately decoded as a projectile payload, with its stored launch amount and category. Observing a projectile received from a peer and observing a received resolved hit are different events.

## Numerical and observational distinctions

Consider a living target at 100 HP with effective maximum HP 200, zero defense and no special effects. Type 0x12 on its qualifying tick supplies 20. A physical projectile storing 20 also supplies 20 on an eligible noncritical contact. Both entries have ability 0 and category 0. The former has null projectile context. The latter has a projectile payload, whether or not its owner resolves. A physical move-2 strike with effective attack 16, action multiplier 1, stored charge 0, amplitude integer 0 and critical false also supplies 20, but its ability argument is 2. With amplitude integer 32767 the same strike supplies 12. In the ordinary immediate authority application with no other modifiers all three 20-point cases can leave 80 HP. Equal damage and HP observations cannot identify their producer.

At resolver entry the caller RVA distinguishes the verified families. The simultaneous presence of a resolved attacker and of projectile context, considered separately, distinguishes a creature strike, owned or ownerless impact, and source-less hazard when paired with the actual ability and category arguments. Projectile kind, category and stored amount tie the impact to its payload. Object type and effective maximum HP distinguish the two hazards. A source actor's current move can change during projectile flight or before peer delivery. Only the release-time move, attack statistic, action multiplier, applicable charge terms, amplitude draw and critical decision establish the numerical provenance of an upstream roll. A scalar amount and critical flag can check a proposed calculation given the other inputs. Combo, threshold, defense and HP address later parts of the transaction and cannot identify the release.

## Evidence boundary

This contract covers the original unpacked Steam image cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe, 5,757,952 bytes, SHA-256 7AF18CB28C8BF848F355287428C6390B2EA2A682CA466190F5EB9859C5D6F244. All addresses are image-relative. The executable section contains the nine direct call sites identified above and no direct tail jump to this resolver. The stock action-table assignments were checked against the original image. The conclusion concerns supported stock dispatch with consistent actor state during the observed action. It makes no attribution to any individual play session or to an unidentified observation boundary. The numerical cases are deductions from offline evidence.
