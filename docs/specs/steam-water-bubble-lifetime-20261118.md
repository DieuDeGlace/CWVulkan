# Steam Water Bubbles lifetime and first area

FINAL / STABLE / HELD

## Result and supported image

A fresh, undisturbed type 0x11D bubble expires after approximately **9 seconds of authoritative simulation**. The first area runs in the next authoritative world substep. With a constant 16 ms step, counting the creation substep as visit 1, visit 563 expires the bubble and visit 564 processes its area. The exact visit for other step sizes depends on binary32 rounding and on where each substep ends.

Evidence is the original Windows x64 Steam unpacked executable, 5,757,952 bytes, SHA-256 7AF18CB28C8BF848F355287428C6390B2EA2A682CA466190F5EB9859C5D6F244, image base 0x140000000. All routine addresses below are RVAs. The duration starts at creation of the creature. Player input, cast animation and presentation latency are outside that interval.

## One authoritative substep

1. World update 0x2DA6C0 runs the dead-creature pass 0x2A45E0 early, only when world byte +0x358 is zero. Later it iterates the live creature list in forward order. For a positive-HP caster this includes the creation routine 0x2C3710. Action 12 initializes bubble HP at +0x180 and lifetime at +0x188 to 1.0, sets role +0x60 to 7 and registers the bubble at the tail through 0x29D0D0. The iterator follows the current node's next link after its work, so it reaches the new bubble in this same substep. Constructors 0x04CE80 and 0x04D600 leave the bubble with action 0, class 0, no equipped weapons, an empty effect list and +0x18C equal to zero.
2. During that creature's upkeep at 0x2DE4C0, positive HP, type 0x11D and world byte +0x358 equal to zero enable the bubble-specific decrement. Multiply elapsed milliseconds by binary32 0.00005, subtract this from lifetime and store it. Test this intermediate result immediately. A value strictly below binary32 0.1 sets HP and clock +0x6C to zero and queues sound 0x2D at the bubble. Equality survives. These checks and writes are at 0x2DF4D7 and 0x2DF51E through 0x2DF5A0.
3. Later in the same upkeep, the ordinary resource adjustment also writes +0x188. The predicate at 0x05EDD0 is false for the stock bubble, and its neutral action 0 selects the second subtraction at 0x2DFDBC through 0x2DFDD2. Subtract the same elapsed-ms product again and then clamp to the interval from 0 to 1. This adjustment has no bubble-expiry test. Crossing 0.1 in the second subtraction therefore leaves the bubble alive until a later bubble-specific check. This ordinary adjustment also runs after the expiry check when HP has just become zero.
4. A final addition at 0x2E01DD through 0x2E021F uses elapsed milliseconds multiplied by 0.00025, then the current +0x18C, then the factor returned by 0x05F8D0. It adds that product to +0x188 and caps the result at 1. On the fresh neutral bubble +0x18C stays zero, so this adds zero. Its ordinary idle update with bit 10 of the word at +0x128 clear subtracts elapsed milliseconds times 0.0005 from +0x18C, flooring it at zero. If that bit is set in this branch, +0x18C instead increases by elapsed milliseconds times 0.00005 up to 1. Effect type 3 forces +0x18C to 1. The 0x05F8D0 factor is 1 without effect type 0x22, otherwise 1 minus 0.1 times the first matching magnitude.
5. The living action-clock update at 0x2DFA99 through 0x2DFAC7 requires positive HP. On the expiry visit it leaves the just-reset clock at zero. The earlier dead pass has already finished that visit. At the next authoritative dead pass, nonpositive HP, a nonzero role, type 0x11D and a clock exactly equal to zero select the bubble's first area. The pass processes the area before increasing +0x6C by that substep's milliseconds at 0x2A5308. A positive step therefore consumes the first-area condition. Once the accumulated dead clock reaches 1,000 ms, the role-7 bubble is scheduled for removal in that pass.

## Timing and edge conditions

The two separate binary32 subtractions drain approximately 0.0001 per simulated millisecond. With 16 ms visits the opening lifetime of visit 563 is about 0.10078757 and its first subtraction produces about 0.09998757. The 563 visits have been supplied 9,008 ms in total, including the 16 ms supplied to the creation visit. As a second check, with 10 ms visits the second subtraction of visit 900 leaves approximately 0.09998143. Visit 901 expires it and visit 902 processes the area. These are deductions from the image's arithmetic, not measured frame timings.

The local driver at 0x0A5A50 caps its incoming double millisecond interval at 1,000. When its world checks permit advancement it processes 16 ms chunks while more than 16 ms remain, then passes the remainder as a float. The authority driver at 0x0F5520 applies the same upper cap to integer milliseconds and passes full 16 ms chunks followed by its integer remainder converted to float. A positive exact multiple of 16 ends with a 16 ms visit. Multiple world substeps can run during one outer update. A zero-length visit can still test an already-low lifetime or a zero death clock. If the first dead pass receives zero, its area runs and the clock stays zero, so a subsequent pass can run the area again.

With a nonzero world byte +0x358 the generic resource upkeep can still adjust +0x188, but the bubble-specific decrement, expiry sound and dead-area pass require authority. A replica's progress alone does not establish an expiry time. An earlier external death, altered action, equipment/class state, nonzero +0x18C or supplied effects can change the timing. For the ordinary nonmagic branch the second subtraction is skipped for action IDs 9, 25, 26, 28, 60, 64 and 65 (decimal). Action 0x92 has a distinct 0.0002-per-ms drain. No such state is inherited from the caster by stock creation.

Source exposure was limited to the isolated author policy, filenames, original Steam executable evidence and individually scoped original Steam exports. The two elapsed-time rates, threshold, frame limits and removal delay were checked in the unpacked image. The contract covers unmodified neutral Water Bubbles and the listed timing conditions.
