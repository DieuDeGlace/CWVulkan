# Steam World selected-pointer lifetime

This bounded contract concerns the raw-pointer collection at World+0x50, its rebuild through RVA 0x2ADFE0 and predicate 0x5EF10, the directly inspected movement and actor-mode consumers, and its relation to later ordinary resident registration/removal. It applies to the original x64 Steam image binary/cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe under C:/Users/DieuDeGlace/Documents/decompiled cube world steam: base 0x140000000, timestamp 0x5D93AB0D, SHA256 7AF18CB28C8BF848F355287428C6390B2EA2A682CA466190F5EB9859C5D6F244. The original image hash was checked for this audit. Addresses below are original image RVAs.

Reference filenames were identified before scoped content inspection. Principal original decompiler evidence is decompiled/functions/14029a130.c, 1402adfe0.c, 14005ef10.c, 14029d0d0.c, 1402992e0.c, 140036c20.c, 14003c0b0.c, 1402da6c0.c, 1402e17d0.c, 1402a56c0.c, 1402d2eb0.c, 1402bb8e0.c and 1402c3710.c, with selected original instruction checks in decompiled/assembly.txt. The actor-mode routine uses the successful original decompiled/retry-180-phase2/functions/1402c5250.c; the ordinary file for that address reports a decompiler timeout and is not used as a substitute body. Direct helper checks, including 1402b5d90.c, 1402d1d20.c, 1402da1e0.c and 1402b77c0.c, bound the particular reached queries without establishing a complete consumer census.

World+0x40 is the live-resident pointer list, with a 64-bit count at World+0x48. World+0x450 is an identity lookup mapping a resident's 64-bit field+0x8 to a pointer. World+0x50 is a separate pointer list, with a 64-bit count at World+0x58. The World constructor 0x29A130 creates distinct empty sentinels for the two lists. The selected-list initialization is directly visible at 0x29A1AC through 0x29A1C9. Its list-node allocator at 0x3C0B0 allocates 0x18 bytes, initializes the two links, and forms a self-linked sentinel when given null neighbors. The pointer-copy helper at 0x36C20 stores an eight-byte payload at node+0x10. Consequently this collection retains resident addresses, not copies of resident positions, dimensions or other state.

At entry, 0x2ADFE0 disconnects and frees all previous World+0x50 nodes, restores the empty sentinel links and sets World+0x58 to zero. The reset starts at 0x2ADFE8, with count clearing at 0x2AE000. It then scans World+0x40 in forward list order. For each visited resident, it calls 0x5EF10 at 0x2AE049 and, on success, appends that same resident address to the tail of World+0x50, increasing the count. The pointer append is reached at 0x2AE064. This is a replacement rebuild, not an accumulation across rebuilds. It frees list nodes without destroying the pointed residents.

The exact inspected admission requires bit 0x800000 in the resident's 32-bit field+0x80 and a strictly positive float at resident+0x180. There is one exclusion combination: byte+0x60 equal to 8, 32-bit code+0x64 within the inclusive range 244 through 247, and nonzero byte+0x19C. The native range comparison in 0x5EF10 is unsigned after narrowing to 32 bits; the positive float test is at 0x5EF38 through 0x5EF46. The predicate does not require byte+0x60 to be zero generally, does not use World+0x448, and does not itself establish a collision, hostility or targetability label. The rebuild does not sort the selected list or perform a separate pointer-deduplication search: it appends in the source list's encounter order.

In the ordinary World step 0x2DA6C0, the rebuild is called at 0x2DA730, after the initial time/day routine and before source-summary/object work at 0x2DA73C. Later, zero World byte+0x358 admits resident registration work through 0x2E17D0 at 0x2DA8DF, distance retention/removal through 0x2A56C0 at 0x2DA8E7, and then 0x2A45E0. The caller subsequently reaches the per-resident movement and actor-mode call sequence. There is no second explicit 0x2ADFE0 call in that interval in the inspected step body. This is an ordering fact for the inspected caller, not proof that no indirect or alternate writer exists.

The inspected registration routine 0x29D0D0 first appends the resident pointer to World+0x40, increments World+0x48 and links the new tail node. It then obtains or inserts the resident identity entry in World+0x450 through 0x2992E0 and writes the pointer into that entry. The tail-link/count operations are at 0x29D134 through 0x29D148; the identity-entry pointer write is at 0x29D15E. The complete registration body has no World+0x50 append, selected-predicate call or selected-list rebuild. Its inspected list and identity-map helpers do not supply an automatic selected-list publication step.

The source-template admission path reaches that registration routine at 0x2E283A, after the selected-list rebuild in the ordinary step. Other directly inspected actor-mode construction branches in 0x2C3710 also reach 0x29D0D0, including calls at 0x2C44A1, 0x2C4AD9 and 0x2C4CDF. Registration through this routine therefore does not automatically put a newly registered pointer into World+0x50 before the next rebuild, even if its eventual fields could satisfy 0x5EF10. No particular generated resident's eligibility is presumed here. Subject to untraced writers, its inclusion by the proved producer occurs when a later rebuild visits it and the predicate succeeds.

This distinction does not stop the new resident from being present in World+0x40 or addressable through World+0x450 immediately after registration. The World-step resident traversal follows the actual linked list and reads the next link after its child calls at 0x2DB6CE. A newly appended tail node can consequently be encountered later in that traversal if the relevant links remain present; the traversal is not a copied resident snapshot. That possibility is separate from selected-list membership and does not prove that every constructed resident receives every phase in the same step.

The directly proved movement consumer is 0x2BB8E0. The ordinary caller invokes it at 0x2DB69C when the current resident's float+0x180 is strictly positive, before 0x2C5250 and 0x2C3710 for that resident. This caller test concerns the resident being updated, not each pointer read from World+0x50. Three direct selected-list reads occur at 0x2BE17D, 0x2BE74D and 0x2C05F5. Their loops use sentinel termination and forward links; the count at World+0x58 is not their iteration admission.

The first movement scan reads each selected pointer's current fixed-position components at resident+0x10, +0x18 and +0x20 and its current float dimensions at +0x84, +0x88 and +0x8C. It compares the candidate and moving resident's bounds and builds a temporary pointer set of initial overlaps. This temporary set does not replace World+0x50. Its membership is used later in this movement invocation to distinguish an overlap already present at the initial scan from one encountered during the movement tests.

The second scan is reached during the component-wise movement checks. It again walks World+0x50 and reads current pointed fields. Candidate code+0x64 equal to 0xFB has a specific skip rule: the candidate is skipped when the moving resident has a nonnull result from 0x5E3A0 for key 0x13, or has bit 0x100000 in its own 32-bit field+0x80. The original key and tests are visible at 0x2BE76F through 0x2BE78C. No business meaning for key 0x13 is assigned. Other candidates proceed to the reached movement/bounds tests. Candidate code 0x12E has its tested vertical extent and position adjusted using its float+0x17C before those tests.

For an admitted candidate, this movement route considers the attempted axis direction and swept bounds. A newly encountered overlap that is absent from the temporary initial-overlap set can trigger movement blocking and position/contact handling. A candidate already in that initial-overlap set is exempted from that particular new-blocking result. These directly reached position effects justify describing this as a movement-collision use of selected pointers. They do not justify making World+0x50 the game's universal collision or target collection.

The third selected scan handles a more specific case: candidate code+0x64 equal to 0x12E and candidate float+0x17C greater than 1.0. With its reached horizontal and vertical admissions, it sets bits 0x1104 in the moving resident's 32-bit field+0x5C and writes contact-direction values. The bit write is at 0x2C0733 and the later direction write at 0x2C076F. Other selected candidates are simply advanced over in this scan. The full numerical movement solver and meanings of that code and those bits are outside this contract.

Within these three inspected scans, the pointed resident is used directly: there is no new call to 0x5EF10, no lookup of that pointer's identity in World+0x450, and no per-candidate recheck of the rebuild's positive-float admission before the stated field reads. There is also no direct null-payload admission before the dimensions/position dereferences. Thus selection at rebuild time is distinct from the later field values observed during movement. A change to a surviving resident's position, dimensions or eligibility fields does not by itself recalculate list membership in these scans. The initial-overlap set is local to that movement call and is destroyed at its end; that local cleanup is not a selected-list reset.

The inspected ability-side comparison is the main reached candidate branch inside actor-mode routine 0x2C5250. It explicitly obtains its candidates from World+0x40, not World+0x50. In that branch the current actor's float+0x6C must lie in the reached action window; each encountered candidate must have a strictly positive float+0x180 and must not already have its identity in the current actor's collection at +0x9B8. Further reached geometry, 0x2B5D90 query and 0x50550 predicate admissions precede later effects. Their broader business meanings and the whole mode-switch census are not established here. The source of candidates and these direct filters prove that this branch is not limited by selected-list membership. A newly registered live resident can be considered by a reached World+0x40-based branch without first being published to World+0x50, subject to that branch's other admissions.

This particular ability branch, its terrain-query helpers, and the three movement scans must remain distinct. The inspected 0x2B5D90 route reaches the loaded-source terrain stepping query at 0x2D1D20; the inspected 0x2B77C0 route reached from movement and selected actor modes tests voxel/block bounds. Those helper bodies do not establish a replacement World+0x50 actor-target search. This contract makes no assertion that all ability modes or all indirect gameplay helpers avoid or use selected pointers.

The later distance-removal routine 0x2A56C0 builds its candidate decisions from World+0x40 and uses byte+0x60-zero live residents as centres. For a selected removal, it finds and unlinks the first matching World+0x40 node, decreases World+0x48, erases the identity key from World+0x450 at 0x2A5949, and invokes the resident's deleting virtual entry at 0x2A5959. Its complete inspected direct body contains no corresponding World+0x50 node erasure or rebuild. This establishes a difference between the direct live-list/map removal and selected-list maintenance. The possible side effects of every concrete deleting virtual body are not resolved here, and no guarantee that every selected pointer remains alive until the next rebuild follows from the inspected caller.

A separate reset boundary is proved in 0x2D2EB0. It first gathers World+0x40 nodes whose resident byte+0x60 is nonzero. For each gathered node it invokes the resident's deleting virtual entry before unlinking/freeing that live-list node and decreasing the live count; the deleting call is at 0x2D2FA5. It then clears and rebuilds World+0x450 from the remaining World+0x40 residents. Only after that identity-map rebuild does it empty World+0x50, zero World+0x58 and free all selected-list nodes, at 0x2D30E1 through 0x2D3111. This final selected-list clear drops surviving selected pointers as well as pointers associated with removed residents. It does not rebuild selected membership from the survivors.

The ordinary World-step routes place this reset at two different boundaries. A day-wrap route in the initial 0x2E2E80 call reaches 0x2D2EB0 at 0x2E300D, before the main step's 0x2ADFE0 rebuild. Later, with World byte+0x358 zero, the main step scans World+0x160 and reaches 0x2D2EB0 at 0x2DB703 on its first integer-zero record, after the per-resident movement/actor-mode sequence. The early clear is followed by the normal rebuild; the later clear has no immediate selected rebuild in the inspected remaining step body. The main step's exact role gates do not establish universal reset callers.

The proved selected maintenance boundaries are constructor initialization, replacement rebuild and the stated wholesale reset. No independent per-resident selected erasure has been established in the scoped registration, distance-removal and movement-list operations. This is a bounded finding, not a complete writer census. Indirect pointer stores, all resident destructors, alternate registration/removal callers, exceptional exits, World-object destruction and synchronization across arbitrary workers remain unresolved. Raw-pointer selection does not itself prove reference counting, lifetime extension or general race safety.

No replacement implementation, implementation history, tests, owner captures, reconstruction, SDK/dependency source, package or implementation output was used. All earlier held contracts, including the World-step contract, remain unchanged.
