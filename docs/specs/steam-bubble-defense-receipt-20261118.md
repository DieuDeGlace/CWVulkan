# Steam Water Bubble burst, guard and shield receipt

FINAL / STABLE / HELD. Original-side behavioral contract, 2026-11-18. The supported image is the offline x64 Steam cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe, 5,757,952 bytes, SHA-256 7af18cb28c8bf848f355287428c6390b2ea2a682ca466190f5eb9859c5d6f244. All addresses below are image-relative. The original image and its own offline native listings supplied the evidence. All numerical outcomes below are deductions for the stated inputs. No game was launched.

## Which hit enters defense

RVA 0x2A45E0 processes species 0x11D's first eligible death pass. For an eligible living candidate inside the burst, the burst makes its one-in-four critical draw and records the candidate ID in the bubble's hit set before the owner-versus-target decision. A previously recorded ID does not enter defense a second time through that bubble. With a resolved hostile owner, positive bubble power at creature offset 0x184 is doubled on the critical draw and multiplied by the bubble's current factor at 0x188. The hit helper receives the owner as source, the target, this signed floating-point product, the critical flag, enabled hit and guard flags, initial stun chance zero, damage category 2 and action argument zero. The entering vector points horizontally from the bubble toward the target, scaled by 10, with vertical component 2.5. Horizontal displacement is normalized only when its squared world length exceeds 0.01. For the examples below use displacement (1,0) in world units, giving (10,0,2.5). The position in the hit is the target's position. The helper can modify the entering amount for a qualifying local attacker before defenses. The amounts below assume that modifier is absent. See the call at RVA 0x2A5274 in 0x2A45E0, the relationship test at 0x50550 and the hit resolver at 0x29EB60.

A nonhostile owner takes a different path for a living target whose affiliation byte is neither 7 nor 8. It queues a negative ordinary hit, preserves the critical flag and uses zero impulse. The authority appends two identical heal records but calls its hit application once. On the guest generation path the second append and local application require owner ID and target both to refer to the local player. Guard, shield selection and shield spending are bypassed for this heal. A guest receiving two such records for its own living character applies both in order up to maximum HP. For example, with 7 missing HP and two records of -5, the first restores 5 and the second restores 2. This producer duplication is distinct from the hostile burst's per-candidate hit set.

## Defense precedence and hit contents

RVA 0x29EB60 rejects a dead target, affiliation 8 or species 0x114 at entry. In a guest world the ordinary resolver requires a local source and a nonlocal target. Authority-produced hits against the guest's own player use the later guest receipt path. Source actions 0x64 and 0x4B require target flag bit 0 at offset 0x5C to proceed. After that gate, the burst's first special outcome is kind 3 if target flag 0x80 at 0x128 or flag 0x20 at 0x5C is set, or if target action 0x7D has elapsed time below 5000. That outcome takes priority over guard and shielding. It queues sound 0x18 and a hit of the full entering amount, full incoming impulse, clear critical byte and kind 3. Authority application can perform positive-hit bookkeeping before it skips the normal movement, HP and shield settlement for kind 3. The guest's ordinary local-receipt branch excludes kind 6 and handles kind 3 as signed HP damage. Consequently an authority kind-3 record of +4 delivered to a living local recipient at 12 HP leaves 8 HP even if a shield is present.

Guard is tested next, before any type-6 shield sum. The ordinary guard predicate at RVA 0x5ED30 requires the float at 0x134 to be nonpositive. It accepts action 9 or 0x6E, action 0x5F with elapsed time strictly below 20000, or action 0x3C or 0x40 with class and specialization bytes 0x14C and 0x14D both 1. For this route the guard resource at 0x184 must be strictly positive before the hit. Independently, actions 0x48, 0x49 and 0x82 can guard during a timed window, even with exhausted resource or a positive timer at 0x134. The window includes its start and excludes its end. For 0x48 and 0x49 it is 200 through strictly below 3200. For 0x82 its start is the integer truncation of 400 divided by the actor's action-speed factor and it lasts 3000. Timing RVAs 0x4EAD0, 0x660D0 and 0x4F220.

Guard cost is the entering amount divided by the defender-specific guard strength from RVA 0x50370. The unmodified, unequipped strength is 5. Eligible weapons, class and creature flags can change it. Actions 0x48, 0x49 and 0x82 force cost to zero. Action 0x5F halves cost. Cost is subtracted from 0x184 with a lower bound of -1. A guard that exhausts its resource still resolves the whole hit as guarded. There is no overflow to shielding or HP for that hit.

On authority the guard also computes half of one minus cost, floored at zero before multiplying by the regeneration factor. RVA 0x5F8D0 supplies 1 when no type-0x22 status exists. With one present it uses 1 minus 0.1 times the potency of the first such status. While the ordinary guard predicate is true, add this scaled gain to 0x188 and also to 0x158 except for action 0x5F. Cap 0x188 at 1 and limit 0x158 to the resulting 0x188. A timed guard alone receives zero gain because the ordinary predicate is false. The cost debit still applies. All listed values are single-precision.

A successful guard produces one kind-1 hit carrying the full entering amount, half the entering vector and the target position. Critical, ordinary hit flag and timers are zero, even if the burst draw doubled the amount. It queues sound 0x18 at the target. The common authority hit-application routine returns on kind 1 before positive-hit bookkeeping. Resource changes belong to guard resolution. No type-6 update is emitted. For a qualifying local guest target, receipt instead recomputes cost from the record amount, its current local strength and current action, and recomputes gain from current local statuses. It uses the current ordinary guard predicate for crediting gain and has no separate requirement to requalify the hit to debit cost. It copies the record vector to movement at 0x34, sets the reaction float 0x994 to 1 for a positive hit, and leaves HP alone. RVAs 0x29EFFB onward, 0x29DCF0, 0xA8B55–0xA8D36.

If neither earlier outcome applies, the resolver sums the current potencies of every type-6 entry on the target in list order. It does not filter by status owner or duration at this point. A nonpositive total goes directly to ordinary resolution. A positive total causes one kind-5 hit with the full entering amount, half the input vector, clear critical and ordinary hit flags, zero timers, original owner and target IDs and target position. Sound 0x5E follows it. Both guard sound 0x18 and shield sound 0x5E have volume 1 and pitch 1 plus 0.3 times a fresh random draw divided by 32767. The classification amount is before magic resistance, ordinary target type-1 multipliers or ordinary on-hit effects. The critical draw, if any, already affected that amount.

The authority calls its hit applicator on the kind-5 hit before computing an ordinary residual. It subtracts the pre-hit shield sum once from the entering amount; only a strictly positive remainder enters ordinary category-2 resolution. Exact equality stops after the shield record. In ordinary resolution the remainder loses target magic resistance multiplied by one minus the capped owner hit-count fraction, floored at zero, before further equipment/status modifiers. It runs the ordinary impact path even if resistance reduces that positive remainder to zero. The ordinary hit restores the original critical flag and full ordinary vector (subject to the ordinary stun path), and uses hit flag 1, kind 0. Positive residual can thus yield two distinct hit records. Category-2 ordinary impact sound is 0x2D. See RVAs 0x29F346, 0x29F4D3, 0x29F4D8, 0x29F4F9 and 0x2A0287 in 0x29EB60, and the stat helpers at 0x64D90 and 0x5F9E0.

## Shield spending and ownership

Authority application at RVA 0x29DCF0 first requires a resolved, living target. A world with no local player pointer at 0x448 rejects affiliation-0 targets before applying the hit. This condition matters for a guest player in the separate Steam server world. The server-world constructor at 0xF17A0 uses 0x29A130, which initializes that pointer to zero. The player-facing scene assigns its local creature at 0x7F657. Classifying and enqueuing a kind-5 record in the server world does not depend on successfully spending a pool in this applicator.

Where authority application proceeds, positive hits reduce the float at 0x18C by 0.25, floored at zero. Target action 0x58 is canceled and sets 0x130 to 3000. A resolved source permits combat-target and threat bookkeeping. The target's flag 0x2000 at 0x80 can further gate settlement. The ordinary examples assume it is clear. For kind 5, the applicator can process impulse before shield spending, then deducts the *full record amount separately from every type-6 entry*. A positive remaining potency keeps the existing duration. A nonpositive result sets both potency and duration to exactly zero. The hit application retains the entry and emits one status record per visited entry, including those just zeroed or previously zero. The outer IDs are the original hit source and target. The inner 24-byte status retains its type and status source ID and carries the updated potency and duration. The final update flag is zero. There is no kind-5 HP debit in this branch. The ordinary residual was based on the total before spending, even though independent per-entry deductions may remove more than that arithmetic suggests for the current hit. A later creature status tick removes expired type-6 entries and clears the entire status list on a dead creature. RVAs 0x29DE00–0x29E350 within 0x29DCF0 and 0x2DE4C0.

## Broadcast, guest order and duplicates

RVA 0xF5520 drains prior guest input through 0xF3FD0 and 0x29D980 before world stepping. The inbound authority pass applies hits in list order, then statuses in list order. It forwards status records even when target lookup fails. World-generated burst hits and any resulting status records enter separate authority lists. RVA 0xF33B0 copies hits and statuses into each peer's pending lists preserving multiplicity and order within each list. For these two lists there is no distance or origin filter. Sounds use a separate 256-world-unit spherical distance gate and suppress a sound whose exclusion ID equals the peer actor ID. Authority fanout comes after this step's simulation. The per-peer send phase precedes that fanout in the same host tick, so new records are eligible at a subsequent send phase.

For event message 7 the inner body is compressed. The hit list is the second counted section and the status list is the thirteenth. The counts are 32-bit, then complete rows in list order. Hit rows are 72 bytes. Relative to the row, signed 64-bit source and target IDs are at 0x00 and 0x08, float amount at 0x10, critical byte at 0x14, two integer timers at 0x18 and 0x1C, three signed 64-bit positions at 0x20, and three floats of impulse at 0x38. The ordinary hit flag, kind and extra action flag occupy bytes 0x44 through 0x46. Status rows are 48 bytes with two outer IDs at 0x00 and 0x08, type at 0x10, float potency at 0x14, float duration at 0x18, signed 64-bit status source at 0x20 and update flag at 0x28. Padding bytes have no specified value. Serialization RVAs 0xF0E70 and 0xF0C90, decoder RVAs 0x3E450, 0x39970 and 0x39570.

The guest's application order differs from the wire order. RVA 0xA5A50 first visits the entire received status list (call at 0xA8445), then the received hit list at 0xA8B05 onward. A status with both outer IDs equal to the local actor ID is skipped on this guest. Otherwise it requires a resolvable target and uses the row's update flag. RVA 0x65390 replaces the first existing status matching both type and inner status-source ID when the flag is set, or when the type is one of 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11. The entire 24-byte status is replaced in place. If there is no match it appends. Unflagged types outside that set append even if a matching entry exists. A repeated type-6 report from the same shield giver therefore overwrites the first match; distinct shield givers retain separate entries. If duplicates with the same key already exist, updates leave later copies in place. A zero report can append a zero-duration row when its key is missing.

The local guest's kind-5 receipt at RVA 0xA8D39 onward loops over every current type-6 entry and deducts the *entire record amount* from each. It zeroes potency and duration on nonpositive remainder, queues one resulting status report per matching entry, and takes no HP from kind 5. The outer source and target of **these new reports are both the local player**, regardless of who hit or granted the shield. The original inner shield source is preserved and the flag is zero. Missing shield entries yield no report and no possibly compensating HP damage. This guest branch does not rerun guard selection, resistance, the shield sum or the authority's residual calculation. It does not assign the kind-5 impulse to the local player's movement. The local receipt has a separate branch for kind 1. For an ordinary residual the local guest accepts the already-resolved signed amount while alive, floors HP at zero and caps healing at maximum HP. A strictly negative post-debit HP value additionally queues local death sound 0x1B; exact zero skips that specific sound branch. A later ordinary hit sees HP zero and skips its HP debit. Local kind-1 and kind-5 cases precede this ordinary living-HP gate. A third-party target outside the local-player branch does not spend the observing guest's shields or HP.

The guest collects locally sourced status reports into network outbound state before the world event lists are cleared (RVAs 0xADADB and 0x3CC40). The sender writes message 0x0C at 0x3FF48 in 0x3F4F0. The server accepts a connected peer's 48-byte status record under that message (0xF4490), applies it in the status phase of 0x29D980 and requeues it for broadcast. An originating player's self/self echo thus has the local skip described above. A remote observer can install it by inner shield source. An authority-generated report with different outer source and target takes the ordinary guest status path, including when the target is the local player. Neither hit ingestion nor local hit application collapses identical hit rows. The incoming event lists are cleared after use, so one consumed batch is not a new hit next frame. The conclusions here concern actual rows delivered, without any assumption about transport retransmission.

## Numerical checks

Use a resolved hostile owner with action 0, affiliation 1, no relevant equipment procs, status or local-attacker bonus, and a living target with no extra defense flags unless stated. The bubble has affiliation 7. Set bubble factor to 1 and power equal to the amount specified. Select a noncritical draw unless stated. The owner hit count, target magic resistance and initial stun chance are zero; no extra action overrides fire. Sound pitch is 1 for an illustrative sound draw of zero. All listed results are at the stated phase before a further status tick or independent actor snapshot.

1. **Guard wins with shields present.** Use power 2.5, target action 9, timer 0x134 zero, strength 5, HP 12, guard resource 0.75, resources 0x188 and 0x158 initially 0.25 and 0.125, and a shield of 7 for 1000 ms. Cost is 0.5. Without type 0x22, authority adds 0.25 to both credited resources, yielding 0.5 and 0.375, with guard resource 0.25. It emits one kind-1 hit of 2.5, impulse (5,0,1.25), and sound 0x18. HP stays 12 and the shield stays 7. For a guest starting at those original resource values, deliver that hit with a separate flagged type-0x22 status from a remote source, potency 5. The guest installs the status first, credits 0.125, and ends at 0.375 and 0.25, guard resource 0.25, HP 12, shield 7. Its movement becomes (5,0,1.25). A second identical hit in the same guest hit list debits again to -0.25 and credits again to 0.5 and 0.375. This effect depends on received row count, not unique attacker/target pairs.
2. **Window and exhaustion.** Action 0x48 with elapsed 200, guard resource -0.5, positive timer 0x134 and a shield of 7 guards power 8 at zero cost. Neither credited resource gains. At 3199 the same holds. At 3200 it falls through to shield selection, emits kind 5 of 8 and an ordinary remainder of 1. Action 9 with resource 0.125 and strength 5 can instead guard a hit of 20: cost 4 drives the resource to the -1 floor without spilling any damage. The next new hit, with the same action and the depleted resource, reaches the shield.
3. **Two independent shields.** At HP 20 take power 6 with two type-6 nodes in order, inner sources 101 and 202, potencies 3 and 5, durations 800 and 1200. Total 8 yields one kind-5 hit of 6, no residual, and sound 0x5E. Where authority settlement is allowed, both nodes immediately become (0,0) and generate two flag-zero reports in the original order. HP remains 20. This discards the apparent unused total of 2. If the authority has no local player pointer and the target is affiliation 0, immediate authority application skips those mutations and reports. A local guest receiving only the kind-5 row from that server, starting with those same shields, zeroes both and emits the two self/self reports itself.
4. **Same-batch shield reports run before hits.** Local actor 42 starts at HP 20 with inner-source 101's shield at 10 for 800 ms. Deliver a kind-5 row of 4 from attacker 17 to 42 and a type-6 report from 17 to 42 with inner source 101, potency 6 and duration 800, all in one message 7. The guest replaces 10 with 6, then spends 4, ending at 2 for 800 ms and reporting outer IDs 42/42, inner source 101 and potency 2. Two copies of the same incoming status and one hit still end at 2. One such status and *two* hit copies end at zero potency and zero duration and emit reports of 2 then 0. If the sole incoming status instead has outer IDs 42/42, the guest skips that status, spends 4 from 10, and reports 6. A type-6 report cannot be assumed to accompany every kind-5 hit against a remote player.
5. **Shield overflow versus HP overkill.** Use power 6 and a critical draw, shield potencies 4 and 3, HP 3 and zero magic resistance. The entering amount is 12 and the shield sum is 7. The queued hits are kind 5 for 12 with critical byte zero, then kind 0 for 5 with critical byte one. Their impulse vectors are (5,0,1.25) and (10,0,2.5), respectively. The impact sounds start with 0x5E then 0x2D, subject to any ordinary species-critical sound. A local recipient with these two shield entries spends 12 from each, zeroes both and reports both. It then takes 5 from 3 HP, ends at zero and enters the strictly-negative local death-sound branch. Starting instead at 5 HP reaches zero without that specific local sound. With shield total exactly 12 there is only kind 5, and HP stays 3. With total 7 and magic resistance 5, hit-count fraction zero, the ordinary row still exists with amount zero and HP stays 3.

## Evidence bounds

The ordering and arithmetic are established for this one Steam image and complete valid event records. The interleaving example explicitly supplies a status and hit in the same consumed batch. It does not assume the headless server generated an attacker-sourced shield update for an affiliation-0 target, or predict when unrelated remote input will share a batch. The examples use no unrelated actor-state refresh, time advance, equipment procs, threat-based settlement restrictions or sound deduplication outside the named routines. Exact UI names for the auxiliary resource fields, padded bytes, network timing and audible presentation are outside this contract.
