At call site 0x29C739, growth reinitializes the bucket table with the requested new count and mask, without clearing the linked entry list. The reached table helpers copy or fill bucket-pointer storage; they do not impose a coordinate order on the entry list. Growth retains the old list’s last entry as its completion marker at 0x29C751, and re-admits the entries in their prior forward traversal order through calls at 0x29C776 until that marker is processed. Rehashing can therefore change which existing node retirement visits first among ties. It does not merely enlarge a table while guaranteeing the old traversal sequence. The inspected distinct-key growth route relinks existing entry nodes; it does not reconstruct their zone payloads.
