This order determines the bounded handling of marks during a batch. Selection does not clear the byte. A mark before a selected mesher's final clear can be overwritten by the unconditional zero store at 0xECB1B; the byte alone carries no preserved generation for that interval. A mark after that clear survives the inspected worker preparation and publication, because those paths do not clear it. If the new cost is nonzero, that surviving mark can satisfy a later scan's invalidation test; if cost is zero, eligibility already comes from the cost-zero path. A mark on a zone omitted from this batch is not cleared by this worker's omitted-item path.
