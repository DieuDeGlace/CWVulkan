# Steam Water Bubbles at first death

**FINAL / STABLE / HELD** · 2026-11-18

Evidence is the original x64 Steam image cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe, SHA-256 7AF18CB28C8BF848F355287428C6390B2EA2A682CA466190F5EB9859C5D6F244, and its offline native exports. Addresses below are RVAs. Actor and hit offsets are byte offsets. The numerical example is derived from the image’s binary32 operations.

## What the burst reads

The actor created by event 12 at 0x2C3710 has model 0x11D, affiliation 7, the caster’s ID at +0x1A0, one HP at +0x180 and a resource of 1 at +0x188. Its separate strength at +0x184 is the caster’s current 0x65C70 result with selector 1, multiplied by 0.75 plus half the creation roll divided by 32767. The creation roll is drawn before that stat query. This selector begins at 5, includes the qualifying category-3 equipment in both hand slots, and applies caster stat modifiers. The burst later reads the bubble’s own two floats. It does not repeat the caster stat query. Affiliation 7 skips both the common +0x184 recharge and its cap at one. A spawn strength above one survives ordinary neutral ticking.

World step 0x2DA6C0 calls 0x2A45E0 only in the authoritative world, where byte +0x358 is zero. It does so before the per-actor update at 0x2DE4C0. A living bubble there first loses a binary32 product of elapsed milliseconds and 0.00005 from +0x188. The strictly-below-0.1 test uses this intermediate stored value. On crossing, HP and action time +0x6C become zero and a sound 0x2D is queued at the bubble. Equality alone does not expire it.

Later in the same update the ordinary neutral, unmodified bubble loses the same product a second time from +0x188, including on the crossing update. This second subtraction alone does not run the expiry test. It belongs to the ordinary resource path for action zero and the false result of 0x5EDD0. Its result is clamped into [0,1]. The later resource contribution depends on +0x18C and is zero for a neutral bubble with that field zero, posture bit 0x400 clear and no overriding status. The two subtractions are individually rounded. The first-death pass of the following world step sees the final residual. Other action, status, posture or external killing conditions can give different residuals. The first-death pass itself requires HP at most zero and action time exactly zero.

## Candidate order and attribution

The first-death pass queues the bubble’s burst particle event before walking the live world actor list in its existing order. Per candidate, it excludes the bubble itself, requires the bubble-to-candidate combat relation at 0x50550, positive candidate HP, candidate float +0x12C equal to zero and absence of the candidate ID in this bubble’s set at +0x9B8. Horizontal distance in world units must be at most 8 plus 0.7 times candidate width +0x84. Vertical separation must be strictly less than 8 plus bubble height +0x8C plus half the candidate height. Original coordinate units are 65536 per world unit. Rejected candidates take no target roll.

For each survivor there is one CRT random draw. Divisibility by four makes the hit critical. The pass prepares the outward horizontal vector from bubble to candidate. It normalizes the horizontal vector only when squared length exceeds 0.01, multiplies both components by 10, and supplies a vertical component of 2.5. A coincident candidate has horizontal components zero. It reads bubble strength, doubles it by addition on a critical roll, and inserts the candidate ID into the bubble’s set. Processing of that candidate is then as follows.

1. Affiliation 8 ends the candidate’s processing after the roll and insertion. For other affiliations, multiply the possibly doubled strength by the bubble’s current +0x188. Only a strictly positive product continues. Resolve the caster ID in the current world at 0xE720. Failure ends the candidate’s processing. There is no caster HP test in this lookup.
2. Re-evaluate 0x50550 with the resolved **caster** and the candidate. A positive relation sends the candidate through the ordinary damage resolver 0x29EB60. A negative relation heals a candidate whose affiliation is other than 7 and whose HP is still positive. Affiliation 7 on this latter branch ends processing without a hit. The caster itself qualifies for the healing branch if it passed the initial gates. The bubble’s affiliation makes the initial relation wider than the caster’s relation.
3. Every generated hit credits the caster ID, not the bubble ID. Each candidate finishes its own resolution before the next candidate. A candidate skipped after insertion still counts as visited. Any downstream random draws from that candidate happen before the next qualifying candidate’s target roll.

The relation at 0x50550 rejects identity and flag bit 22 at actor +0x80 on either party. After those exclusions, affiliation 7 on either party satisfies the relation immediately. For other pairs, affiliation 8, class byte 0xA1 or model 0x12F on either party rejects it. Otherwise affiliation 1 on exactly one party, affiliation 1 on both with differing +0x80 bit 15, or +0x128 bit 5 on either party satisfies it. A passed relation does not itself guarantee a change in HP.

## Healing record and HP

The healing branch negates the full positive product and appends a hit to world +0x90. On the authoritative path it appends a second hit with identical defined fields to the same list, then invokes the hit application routine 0x29DCF0 **once**. These are consecutive list entries. Neither amount is reduced by the recipient’s missing HP. The append sites are 0x2A5146 and 0x2A51C1, followed by application at 0x2A5202. There is no separate healing sound or impulse on this branch.

| Hit offset | Native healing payload |
| --- | --- |
| +0x00, +0x08 | 64-bit caster ID, candidate ID |
| +0x10, +0x14 | Binary32 negative amount, one-byte critical flag |
| +0x18, +0x1C | Two 32-bit zero durations |
| +0x20, +0x28, +0x30 | Candidate’s current three signed 64-bit world coordinates |
| +0x38, +0x3C, +0x40 | Three binary32 zero impulse components |
| +0x44, +0x45, +0x46 | Three zero bytes |

The native hit copy at 0x38B80 assigns these fields. The network list writer 0xF0E70 copies 0x48 bytes per record after a 32-bit count. The field copier gives no value guarantee for padding +0x15 through +0x17 or +0x47.

0x29DCF0 resolves the target again and requires positive HP. A world with no local actor pointer skips application to affiliation 0. For an ordinary healing recipient, one application adds the full magnitude and caps HP at the recipient’s **current** maximum from 0x5FAC0. It can emit a full-sized heal when HP is already at maximum. A negative amount does not perform the positive-damage affiliation test. The shared protection on target +0x80 bit 13 can still suppress application. For that protected target the routine requires a positive threat entry referring to an affiliation-0 actor, together with a resolved source of affiliation 0 or 6. On the host, healing also updates existing eligible opponents’ threat toward the healer using the submitted magnitude. These are application-side effects and do not change the two queued amounts.

## Damage record and HP

The positive branch supplies caster as attacker, candidate as defender, product as input damage, the target roll’s critical flag, byte flag 1, initial knockdown chance zero, bubble position, the prepared impulse, attack kind 2, action override zero, auxiliary value zero and enabled defensive processing. The resolver uses the actors’ **current** state. It rejects dead defenders, affiliation 8 and defender model 0x114. Caster action 0x64 or 0x4B also requires target +0x5C bit 0 set. A local caster against defender model category 6 can have the input scaled by one plus the caster’s 0x510F0 value.

On a normal unblocked hit, the kind-2 defense deduction uses target stat 0x64D90 multiplied by one minus the caster’s +0x70 count divided by its 0x5F9E0 threshold, with the ratio capped above at one. Damage after the deduction is floored at zero. Equipment effects and attacker statuses can add damage or produce extra self-healing records. The final ordinary damage payload also multiplies by each defender type-1 status factor. A neutral setup with zero defense, no applicable equipment or status effects and no model-category bonus preserves the entire product. Ordinary resolution emits sound 0x2D at the **candidate** and can emit a separate critical-reaction sound.

The ordinary hit contains caster and candidate IDs, final binary32 amount, original critical byte and candidate position in the fields listed above. Its impulse is the prepared impulse, +0x44 is 1, and subtype +0x45 is zero. Both durations start at zero. Under the shared knockdown rules the first duration can become 1500 for affiliation 0 or 3000 otherwise and vertical impulse becomes 10. With the zero input chance, neutral actors do not trigger this, though a candidate with +0x130 below -3000 and affiliation other than 7 can still consume the knockdown random draw. Particular actor actions can override the chance. The last byte +0x46 is 1 for caster action 0x1D and zero otherwise. Kind 2 selects the resolver behavior and is not the hit subtype.

An ordinary hit appends once, then applies once on the authoritative world. Positive ordinary damage reduces HP only if defender and resolved caster pass 0x50520. That permits affiliations 0, 1, 3 and 6, plus affiliation 7 except models 0x8C through 0x8E. Subject to the other application gates, HP is reduced by the final amount, and a lethal result is clamped to zero and takes the shared death path. Zero damage can still produce a hit record. The host’s ordinary impulse application excludes affiliation 7 and may retain a stronger existing impulse. HP change, submitted damage and displayed number can differ.

Shared defensive outcomes are reachable. A miss yields subtype 3 and a full impulse. An eligible block yields subtype 1, spends the defender’s guard resource according to its current action and defense, and uses half the impulse. A positive type-6 absorb pool yields subtype 5 with half impulse before any ordinary residual hit. Its record carries the incoming amount, and only a positive excess above the summed pool continues to ordinary damage. Each of these special records clears critical and byte +0x44, records the candidate’s coordinates and its own subtype, and has zero duration fields and final byte. The authoritative application routine returns immediately on subtype 1, bypasses the HP branch for subtype 3, and reduces the applicable type-6 status pool for subtype 5. These conditions explain why the area alone does not fix the number of records for an equipped or defending actor. This contract leaves the complete shared combat/status and post-death systems to their respective native routines.

## Network receipt and combat numbers

The ordinary guest world does not run 0x2A45E0. A self-heal exception inside that routine would permit its second insert and application on a non-authoritative world only when both source ID and target actor are local. The normal caller at 0x2DA6C0 never enters it on the guest. Similarly the generic damage resolver’s guest admission requires local source and a different target. Neither internal condition makes a mirrored bubble run a second area sweep.

Server forwarding at 0xF33B0 uses 0x74AE0 to append the complete hit range in order to every peer queue, before looking up the peer actor for particle and sound range filtering. The type-7 event message sent at 0xF5520 carries the ordered hit list. There is no source-ID, target-ID, distance or duplicate filter on this broadcast hit range. The guest’s type-7 reader at 0x3E450 places the received hits into its incoming list. Receipt, local HP processing and local text presentation have different tests.

In the incoming-hit pass at 0xA5A50, a resolved target equal to the local player takes the local handling for **each** record. For a living local player, ordinary subtype zero subtracts the signed amount and caps at current maximum. Thus two received negative entries can heal twice on that recipient’s client, with an independent cap each time. The server’s direct burst application was once. An observer does not mutate another actor’s HP in this pass. Against a nonlocal target, a positive hit from a nonlocal source may set its hit flash. Incoming local-player handling has separate subtype-1 and subtype-5 branches, and excludes subtype 6 from ordinary HP processing. Later the same frame copies incoming hits into the world presentation list only when their source ID differs from the local actor ID. A local-source echo is thus skipped for this copy even if the local target already processed its HP effect.

The text producer 0xA0750 walks world hit entries individually. It requires the target to resolve and either source or target to be the local actor. Subtypes 4 and 6 suppress this number. Ordinary subtype zero requires absolute amount at least 0.1. The displayed number uses the absolute **record** amount. At one or above it truncates to an integer. Below one it truncates to tenths. Heals use green RGBA (0.4, 1, 0.5, 1). Incoming ordinary damage to the local player uses (1, 0.3, 0.3, 1). Outgoing ordinary damage uses (1, 1, 0.25, 1) with +0x44 set and white when that byte is clear. The base size field is 20 and critical sets it to 60. A qualifying heal can therefore produce two equal labels even if the server applied one capped HP change. A bystander can receive both records without seeing either number. The blocked, missed and absorbed subtypes produce the corresponding words, and a nonzero first duration can also emit a separate K.O. label.

## Offline arithmetic example

Use a stock neutral bubble, empty qualifying caster equipment, no stat modifiers and a creation strength roll of 0. The stat is 5, so the bubble stores 3.75 at +0x184. Use 16 ms for every update, a starting resource of 1, zero +0x18C, no posture, action or status changes, no external hits and no intervening writes. Step 563 starts with +0x188 equal to 0.10078756511211395. Its first subtraction yields 0.09998756647109985, killing the bubble at 9008 ms. Its second subtraction leaves 0.09918756783008575. The following authority step performs the first-death sweep.

Use a local caster ID 41, an eligible friendly NPC ID 42 with 99.75 of 100 HP, and an eligible hostile NPC ID 43 with 50 HP. Both accept ordinary HP changes. Neither is guarding or protected. The caster has a neutral action and count zero, the hostile has zero defense and no statuses, and there is no category bonus. Place the bubble at (0,0,0), the friend at (0,65536,0) and the hostile at (65536,0,0). These are raw coordinates. For this example take the friend’s target roll as 1 and the hostile’s as 4. These are the per-target roll inputs, independent of draws by any other actor.

- The friend contributes two hits from 41 to 42, amount -0.3719533681869507, critical 0, at (0,65536,0), with zero impulse. The direct HP application raises 99.75 to 100 once. A local caster presenting these authoritative entries gets two green labels reading 0.3.
- The hostile contributes one ordinary hit from 41 to 43, amount 0.7439067363739014, critical 1, at (65536,0,0), with impulse (10,0,2.5) and final bytes (1,0,0). Hostile HP becomes 49.2560920715332. The caster’s text reads 0.7 at the larger size.
- If the same friendly records are received for a living local guest initially at 50 of 100 HP, the first write yields 50.371952056884766 and the second yields 50.74390411376953. A local-source echo and remote-target observation follow the distinct rules above.

The example traces recorded and displayed values under named inputs. It does not establish a fixed lifetime independent of step schedule, interference or statuses, or a measured end-to-end network outcome.
