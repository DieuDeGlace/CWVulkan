Descriptor construction and field widths

For a supplied signed 32-bit block X/Y point, 0x35CE0 floors each by 1024, calls 0x36160 for a one-byte flag, stores that flag at T+4, and calls 0x362A0 for a signed 32-bit center pair stored at T+0x10/+0x14. At overflowing inputs this is the actual 32-bit comparison, not a widened Euclidean-distance promise.

Inside that bound, 0x2B99D0 is queried with V, the 1024-block cell pair and the flag; its integer kind is stored at T+0. A zero kind clears the float pair at T+0x18/+0x1C and the 32-bit variation at +8. For a nonzero kind, T+0xC receives integer radius 250 or 500 according to the flag. 0x364E0 receives V, the center pair and the original block X/Y. The selected kind-5 return of 0x2B99D0 occurs only in its nonzero-flag branch. 0x36160 derives its byte from the half-cell coordinates, a coordinate hash with bias 0x39A13D and a conditional 0x36210 query. Final descriptor/coordinate exclusion

Only after all preceding comparisons pass does the search derive Q.x and Q.y by signed floor division of P.x and P.y by 1024. For negative operands it uses the equivalent native construction of incrementing the negative operand by one, dividing toward zero by 1024, then subtracting one; at exact negative multiples this still gives the proper floor. When T's byte at +4 is nonzero, each Q component is additionally rounded down to the nearest even integer. Native division toward zero is corrected for negatives before doubling. For example, block coordinates -1 and -1024 both belong to cell -1, while -1025 belongs to -2; down-to-even maps cell -1 to -2, cell -2 to -2 and cell -3 to -4.

At call site 0x2D1522, 0x115DF0 constructs L using the saved R pair from 0x2D126F, not Q and not the pair returned by a later conditional center query. L stores V at +0 and the signed 32-bit R pair at +8/+0xC, and initializes the two signed 32-bit fields at +0x80/+0x84 to INT32_MAX. The final exclusion reads precisely L+0x80/+0x84 as a pair K. It does not compare Q with L's header R pair or with every record in L's collections.

The traced initial collection-building loop in 0x115DF0 scans 1024-block cells covering the region-related bounds. Where its 32-bit products do not wrap, X runs from 8 times R.x minus 8 through 8 times R.x plus 16 inclusive, and Y has the analogous bounds, X outer/Y inner: 25 by 25 cells. The (0,0) cell is skipped. Each cell is queried through 0x36160 then 0x2B99D0 with the flag. Nonzero kinds are normalized to even grid coordinates when flagged, centered through 0x362A0, and queried through 0x2ADA20 at those block centers. A record is admitted only if the returned regional pair equals the input R pair component by component. This is a bounded local descriptor scan inside an otherwise unbounded outer candidate search.

The descriptor then performs additional registry/role processing. In the selected assignment path for K it collects the remaining kind-5 records' 8-byte grid pairs in registry traversal order and requires more than one pair. It gathers remaining kind-0x16 records separately and requires that collection to be nonempty. It selects and removes a kind-0x16 registry record, updates associated role bookkeeping, and chooses a kind-5 pair using a coordinate-derived carried counter transformed by left-XOR-13, right-XOR-17 and left-XOR-5 steps and remainder by the pair count. The counter originates from R coordinates and has already been advanced by earlier registry work; it is not an independent imported rand sample or a fresh isolated index depending only on the final count. The chosen pair is copied to L+0x80 and then +0x84 at native sites 0x116C76 and 0x116C81.

The direct assignments identified for this pair in 0x115DF0 are the initial INT32_MAX stores and that guarded selected-pair copy. The full comparator, all preceding record transformations, and all later temporary-descriptor business meanings are not inferred here. They matter to which K is produced, and the final search consumes the actual constructed K. If the sentinel pair remains, its INT32_MAX components cannot equal a Q component obtained by the search's signed 32-bit block division by 1024 and optional even rounding. In that sentinel case this final exclusion does not reject.

The search compares K.x with Q.x first. A difference immediately takes the cleanup-and-return path. Only equality reaches the K.y/Q.y comparison. A difference there also cleans up and returns the original caller's output-pair pointer. Equality in both components cleans up and retries from the increment at 0x2D1210. 0x82450 destroys L's owned temporary collections on both the successful and excluded-candidate routes before return or retry. The candidate pair C remains the already-written home pair; Q, R and K are separate units and are not substituted into it.

Boundary, random and evidence limits

All candidate/home/block integer operands described above retain their actual native widths. Home-to-block shifts/additions, coarse-cell candidate arithmetic, descriptor integer distance calculations and many cell/site products are 32-bit. Fixed-coordinate accumulation, signed fixed differences and the selected truncation of float warp offsets are 64-bit. Negative division corrections are local to the stated operations. In particular, the floor conversions for S/R, the truncating fixed conversion inside q, and final floor/even rounding are different reached operations and are not interchangeable. No assertion that ordinary arithmetic keeps working as unbounded integers across signed-coordinate wrap is made.
