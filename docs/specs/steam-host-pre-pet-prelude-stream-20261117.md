# Steam authority prelude before the pet pass

## Image and scope

This contract concerns the original Steam image `cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe` at image base `0x140000000`, SHA-256 `7af18cb28c8bf848f355287428c6390b2ea2a682ca466190f5eb9859c5d6f244`. RVAs and scalar constant values were checked against the matching native instruction export and PE. Actor offsets are relative to the full actor pointer. Actor ID at `+0x08` and position components at `+0x10`, `+0x18` and `+0x20` are 64-bit. The position scale is 65,536 fixed-point units per voxel. Category and action are the bytes at actor `+0x60` and `+0x68`. Model is the 32-bit value at actor `+0x64`. HP and the action or death timer are binary32 at actor `+0x180` and `+0x6C`. The comparisons below describe ordinary finite actor state and valid initialized containers.

In World tick `0x2DA6C0`, the byte test at `0x2DA8CE` gates the three calls at `0x2DA8DF`, `0x2DA8E7` and `0x2DA8F3` on World `+0x358` being zero. They execute in that order. The current contract begins after the first call returns and follows the latter two calls, to `0x2A56C0` and `0x2A45E0`. The latter receives the same binary32 tick increment as the preceding resident call. The pet pass `0x2E0DB0` is called immediately afterward at `0x2DA8FB` and also runs when the mode test skips the three calls.

## CRT stream

The original PE import address table identifies `0x42CD28` as `rand` and `0x42CD38` as `srand`, both in the Windows CRT utility API set `api-ms-win-crt-utility-l1-1-0.dll`. The scoped resolved call graph reaches no `srand` site, including the nested combat, death and item branches. The `0x2A56C0` graph reaches zero resolved `rand` sites. The `0x2A45E0` graph has two local draw sites, `0x2A4CCC` and `0x2A4FDB`. Further draws are possible synchronously inside a target's hit, death or item work. The resolved paths take their incoming stream from earlier execution and pass its resulting position to the pet update. They do not reset the stream on entry, per actor or per candidate. Dynamic call limitations appear at the end.

Each site in the draw sequences below denotes one call to the imported `rand`, including rolls forced to some outcome by later tests. Integer residues are residues of the raw nonnegative draw. A normalized draw is the raw draw converted to binary32 and divided by the binary32 constant 32,767 at `0x42E5F0`. When describing a scaled draw, the native pitch and angle sites multiply the converted raw draw by the scale before dividing by 32,767, then add the offset if any. Values and order in this contract depend on the stream inherited from earlier execution. The admitted executable imports the CRT functions and does not supply the provider's state recurrence, seed history or absolute draw ordinal on tick entry.

## Range removal at 0x2A56C0

The routine makes a list of all category-zero actors from World `+0x40` in forward list order. HP, Z and the local-actor pointer play no part in this collection. A second forward pass considers each nonzero-category actor against that list. It keeps the actor if any anchor passes both absolute X and absolute Y differences at most 320 voxels and horizontal squared distance at most 102,400 voxel units. The native comparison sites are `0x2A582E`, `0x2A584A` and `0x2A5875`. Subtract the signed 64-bit positions before converting to binary64 and applying the factor at `0x43EF58`. Both edges are inclusive. Z is ignored. An empty anchor list selects every nonzero-category actor for removal.

After marking finishes, each selected actor is handled in marking order. A matching pointer is unlinked from the World `+0x40` list, the count at `+0x48` is decremented and its list node freed. The actor ID is erased from the World `+0x450` index at `0x2A5949`, then deleting virtual slot zero is called with argument 1 at `0x2A5959`. That call follows the index erase even if the list search found no matching node. Scratch lists are discarded after the pass. The surviving list and index are the starting state of `0x2A45E0`.

## Dead actors at 0x2A45E0

### Entry, block sweep and sound

The outer pass follows the current World `+0x40` list forward. An actor enters the work only when its HP is at most zero and its category is nonzero. The routine adds or finds its ID in the ordered set at World `+0x20` at `0x2A4749`. Insertion success is not a gate for the effect or target scan. For timing discussed below, the first-visit effect requires an exact zero timer and model `0x11D`, `0x90` or `0x119` (tests `0x2A4752` through `0x2A4782`). It appends an effect of ID `0x14` at the actor's position to World `+0xB0` at `0x2A4836`. The secondary selector is 2 for model `0x11D` and 1 for the other two. This append consumes no draw.

Only model `0x90` performs the block sweep. It visits integer offsets from minus 8 through plus 8 in X, then Y, then Z with Z varying fastest. It processes only offsets with squared length strictly below 64. There are 2,103 in-range offsets. The samples are relative to the original fixed-point origin of the actor. Each in-range sample looks up the block at the signed voxel coordinates, with the coordinates rounded down. The read at `0x2A4A05` enters `0x00E9A0` with all three voxel coordinates. That path uses the live chunk index at World `+0x460` and the returned six-byte block, including its fallback values for missing data. The byte at block `+5` must have at least one bit of mask `0x11` set (test `0x2A4A0A`).

For each qualifying block in sweep order, append a particle record to World `+0xB0` at `0x2A4ABB`, clear or replace the block locally through `0x2A59F0` at `0x2A4AFE`, then enqueue a block change at World `+0xC0` at `0x2A4C14`. The particle is at the sample plus 32,768 fixed-point units on each axis. Its three color bytes are converted using the factor 1/128. Block access, replacement and record insertion use no resolved CRT draw. The success flag records whether at least one sample took this path.

After the entire sweep, a set success flag consumes one draw at `0x2A4CCC`. Its pitch is 0.5 plus 0.4 times the draw divided by 32,767, with native binary32 operations. The sound ID is 1 and the other scalar is 1.0. The sound is queued at the source actor position in World `+0xA0`. The following call at `0x2A4DB2` to `0x2E0710` examines resident entries in the horizontal region between the source coordinates minus 8 and plus 8. The rectangle arguments use signed fixed-point division truncated toward zero and the callee clamps them to nonnegative bounds before dividing by the 64-voxel chunk width. The callee can remove entries from nearby resident chunk records after checking their backing blocks. This path adds no resolved draw. A zero-success sweep skips both the sound draw and that rectangle call. Sweeping and its optional sound always precede the actor target scan.

### Target filter and the first hit draw

Every qualifying source model scans the then-current World actor list forward. The per-target filter is evaluated again against live state on each visit. It rejects the source pointer itself, then requires compatibility from `0x050550` at `0x2A4E03`. It rejects target HP at most zero, target binary32 `+0x12C` unequal to zero, and any target whose signed ID already exists in the source's ordered set at `+0x9B8` (through `0x2A4E91`).

The compatibility predicate requires distinct pointers and no `0x00400000` bit in either actor's 32-bit `+0x80`. If either category is 7, it then returns true. Otherwise both actors must avoid category 8, model `0x12F` and class byte `0xA1` at `+0x14C`. With those exclusions satisfied, it returns true when one actor is category 1 and the other is a different category, when both are category 1 and their `+0x80` bit `0x8000` differs, or when either low byte at `+0x128` has mask `0x20` set.

The binary32 horizontal radius is the target's binary32 `+0x84` multiplied by 0.7 and increased by 8. Both absolute XY differences after binary64 fixed-point conversion must be at most this radius. The binary64 sum of their squares must be at most the binary32 square of the radius promoted to binary64. Absolute vertical separation must be strictly below the sum of half of target `+0x8C`, source `+0x8C` and 8, computed in binary32. These tests occupy `0x2A4E97` through `0x2A4F77`. To form the impulse, the code converts the target-minus-source XY difference to binary32 voxels and normalizes that pair only when its squared length exceeds 0.01. It multiplies each horizontal component by 10 and uses 2.5 for the vertical component.

At `0x2A4FDB`, a candidate surviving those tests consumes one draw and is critical when the draw is divisible by 4. A critical doubles the source's binary32 `+0x184` magnitude by addition. The target ID is then inserted into source `+0x9B8` at `0x2A5049`. The draw and insertion both precede the late rejection of target category 8 and the owner/model checks. In particular, category 8 can reach the draw through the category-7 compatibility arm. Doubled or ordinary magnitude is sent to the hit handler for models `0x90` and `0x119` at `0x2A5274`, with the dying source actor as attacker and damage selector 1.

For `0x11D`, multiply that magnitude by source binary32 `+0x188`. The result must be strictly positive and source owner ID `+0x1A0` must resolve to an actor in World `+0x450`. If the owner and target satisfy `0x050550` at `0x2A50A2`, reach the same hit handler with that owner as attacker and damage selector 2. If that check fails, a still-living target outside category 7 instead receives a signed-negative amount with the source owner ID, critical flag and target position. The sign bit is toggled at `0x2A50FD`. At `0x2A5146` this packet is appended to World `+0x90`. In authority mode it is appended a second time at `0x2A51C1` and applied once at `0x2A5202` by `0x29DCF0`. This is the healing arm of the packet applicator and cannot produce a new lethal HP transition for ordinary positive maximum-HP state. It does not call the hit constructor. Failure of the positive amount, owner lookup or late target gates does not refund the candidate draw.

### Aging, revival and deferred removal

After a source actor's effect and target work, add the given tick increment to binary32 `+0x6C` at `0x2A5313`. This addition also runs for qualifying dead actors that skipped the zero-timer effects. A category-6 actor with nonzero owner ID follows the revival arm. Once the updated timer is at least 20,000, if the owner ID resolves to an actor with HP at least zero, it restores HP using `0x05FAC0` at `0x2A53E8`, copies the owner's three position components, clears actor `+0x9E0` and clears its `+0xB88` tree. For other actors the removal threshold is an updated timer of at least 1,000. It stages those actors in forward encounter order and at that time erases their IDs from every current actor's `+0xB88` and `+0xB98` trees. The sites are `0x2A549C`, `0x2A54EE` and `0x2A54FD`.

Only after completion of the outer walk are those staged actors removed from World `+0x40` and `+0x450`. List unlinking decrements `+0x48` and frees the list node. The index erase is `0x2A55F2` and the deleting virtual call is `0x2A5602`, again through slot zero with argument 1. The resolved timer and removal helpers use no CRT draw. A target killed by an earlier source is already dead in live state. If its outer-list visit is still ahead, it can get its own timer-zero effects on that same invocation. If its outer-list visit already passed, it gets no second visit from this invocation. Deferred removal is separate from that live-state observation.

## The reached hit handler at 0x29EB60

At `0x2A5274`, the attacker is always a nonnull actor. The call supplies the critical flag, a separate byte set to 1, base secondary-effect chance zero, the dying source's position, the impulse above, damage selector 1 or 2, ability selector zero, an empty optional argument, and final byte 1. These constants distinguish the reached branches from other callers of the same handler. All the handler's draws precede a return to the current target loop. A lethal packet can call the item branches before that return.

1. The handler can reject without any draw of its own. It rejects a target at HP at most zero, category 8 or model `0x114`. Its alternate-mode ownership rejection is inactive in the scoped authority route. When the attacker is the actor in World `+0x448` and the target-model classifier `0x065750` returns 6, it deterministically scales the magnitude by 1 plus `0x0510F0` evaluated for the attacker. It then rejects attacker actions `0x64` and `0x4B` if the target byte at `+0x5C` has bit 0 clear (`0x29ECCA` to `0x29ECDA`).
2. With an eligible attacker action `0x1D`, consume `0x29ED13` to set an effect scalar to 0.1 plus 0.1 times the draw divided by 32,767. This is before the protective checks below and may be the first of several draws. The effect record is appended to World `+0xB0` at `0x29ED7B`.
3. The next branch checks the target's `+0x128` low-byte bit `0x80`, `+0x5C` bit `0x20`, or action `0x7D` with timer strictly below 5,000. If any condition holds, consume `0x29EE1D`, queue sound ID `0x18` with pitch 1 plus 0.3 times the draw divided by 32,767, and submit the mode-3 hit packet. The packet reaches `0x29DCF0` through `0x2A02A0`, where mode 3 skips HP application. The hit handler leaves without the succeeding draw sites.
4. Otherwise the final argument of 1 enables the mana branch. It qualifies when `0x05ED30` is true and target binary32 `+0x184` is positive, or when target action is `0x48`, `0x49` or `0x82` and its timer is within the lower-inclusive, upper-exclusive window returned by `0x04EAD0` and `0x0660D0`. The lower bound is the return from `0x04EAD0` with its action argument selecting the live action. The upper bound adds the result of `0x0660D0` for that action. The `0x05ED30` predicate first excludes positive `+0x134`. Its qualifying cases are actions `0x09` and `0x6E`, action `0x5F` with timer below the value from `0x04F220`, and actions `0x3C` and `0x40` with both actor bytes `+0x14C` and `+0x14D` equal to 1. A nonqualifying `0x5F` does not qualify through another arm. On this mana path, the resource change and mode-1 packet run before one sound draw at `0x29F22D`. It uses the same sound ID and pitch rule as the mode-3 branch. The mode-1 applicator returns without HP damage, and the hit handler leaves without further draws.
5. If neither branch above terminates the hit, sum the binary32 amounts from each type-6 status record in the target list at `+0x980` in forward order. Each list record's type and amount are at node `+0x10` and `+0x14`. A positive sum causes a mode-5 packet to be queued before draw `0x29F440`. That draw sets sound ID `0x5E` pitch to 1 plus 0.3 times the draw divided by 32,767. The hit handler then calls `0x29DCF0` at `0x29F4D3`. The mode-5 packet reduces type-6 amounts and can clear depleted entries' amount and following four bytes. Subtract the pre-application sum from the incoming hit magnitude. Continue the regular hit only if the remainder is strictly positive. A sum at most zero skips the shield mode packet and sound draw, and continues with the unsplit magnitude.
6. The regular hit computes its magnitude and impulse using live actor and equipment state. At `0x29F4F9` the defense value comes from `0x064D90` because the supplied damage selector is nonzero. Subtract the defense value multiplied by one minus the attacker resource fraction capped at one, then floor the result at zero. The resource fraction divides the attacker's signed `+0x70` by `0x05F9E0` for that attacker. At `0x064D90`, armor records at `+0x3B8`, `+0x458`, `+0x4F8` and `+0x598` count when their types are respectively 4, 6, 5 and 7. For category-zero actors the class fit predicate `0x1094D0` also gates each record. Its item values from `0x10A1E0` are summed and modified by `+0x80` flags and statuses. The original deterministic helper calls and additives for equipment, response and outgoing events use no resolved CRT draw.
7. On this regular finish, the selector is 1 or 2. It emits a fixed-pitch regular impact at that selector, then uses the following optional draws in this order. If the initial critical flag is set and the target category is other than 7, consume `0x29FF4A` to make a pitch of 1 plus 0.1 times the draw divided by 32,767. This call is before the model-to-critical-sound table, so a model without a table result still spends it. Afterward, if the target category is other than 7 and target binary32 `+0x130` is strictly below minus 3,000, consume `0x2A0172` and compare the normalized draw strictly below the secondary-effect chance. A zero chance still spends the draw. On success `0x05FC20` supplies the duration, 1,500 for category zero and 3,000 otherwise, and a fixed-pitch sound with ID `0x17` is queued. The regular packet is then appended to World `+0x90` and applied at `0x2A0287`.

The secondary-effect chance at `0x2A0172` starts with the call's zero. Before the target and attacker flag adjustments, attacker actions `0x0D`, `0x3B`, `0x45`, `0x46` and `0x67` set it to one. The target `+0x80` mask `0x40000` multiplies it by 0.15. The attacker lacking that flag and having nonzero category multiplies it by 0.1. Attacker action `0x64` or `0x4B` or target action `0x58` overrides the resulting chance to one. It then scales by 0.9. The presence of any target type-1 status record forces it to zero. The ordering of the tests and normalizations is visible at `0x2A00AA` through `0x2A0187`. These gates read the current target fields, including in the regular remainder after a mode-5 application.

The hit handler also contains two `rand` sites outside the scoped argument domain. The generic selector-zero weapon sound draws at `0x29FB2F`. The null-attacker path draws at `0x29FCE6`. Neither is available from `0x2A5274`. The direct healing path at `0x2A5202` bypasses the entire handler, including both of those sites.

## Packet application and access to the death draws

The packet applicator `0x29DCF0` contains no CRT draw or seed call of its own. It resolves target ID via World `+0x450` and requires an existing positive-HP target. With World `+0x448` null, it rejects a category-zero target. Mode byte `+0x45` of 1 returns at the intake gate. The attacker ID is separately resolved. Positive amounts can affect acting state before damage, and nonnegative amounts from a resolved attacker update cross-actor combat references. The `0x2000` bit in target `+0x80` adds a gate that requires a positive `+0xB88` contribution from an ID resolving to a category-zero actor and a resolved attacker of category zero or six. The qualification is in `0x29DFFA` through `0x29E0C7`.

Mode 3 skips application. Mode 5 with positive amount reduces type-6 statuses without subtracting HP. The negative amount of the direct `0x11D` arm increases HP and caps it using `0x05FAC0`. For a regular positive packet, HP subtraction requires `0x050520` to allow the target and also a resolved attacker, if present. This helper accepts categories 0, 1, 3 and 6 and accepts category 7 except models `0x8C` through `0x8E`. Other categories fail that helper. If status multipliers, shield remainder, armor, packet gates and the resulting exact HP do not give a death, there are no loot draws. Damage subtraction sites are `0x29E32B` through `0x29E35C` and HP is tested for death at `0x29E486`.

When the applicator reaches HP at most zero from a live target, it writes binary32 zero HP, queues a fixed death sound (ID `0x2D` for model `0x11D`, otherwise `0x1B`), and resets the target's timer to zero at `0x29E558`. It calls `0x2A6860` at `0x29E562`. After that call returns, a set `0x100` bit in the 16-bit field at target `+0x128` calls `0x2A7CA0` at `0x29E5A6`. The latter's signed integer argument is the lesser of 4 and the signed 32-bit value at actor `+0x144` incremented by one. All of this completes synchronously before the hit handler returns. At `0x29E5AB` onward the applicator can also mark a linked actor dead without recursively taking the loot route for that linked actor at this call. Subsequent bookkeeping is draw-free on resolved edges.

## Death draws at 0x2A6860

This routine returns immediately for actor category 0, category 3 or bit `0x800` in actor `+0x80`. The separate `0x2A7CA0` gate at its caller is evaluated even if this routine returns early. For a remaining actor, the row order below is execution order. Each placement call in a row runs fully, with its own draws, before the next row or repeat. An item's type byte is at its start, subtype is the 32-bit value at item `+4`, and item variation word is 32 bits at item `+8`.

Each placement scatter triple uses successive draws for Y, X and angle. The horizontal offset on each axis is the signed integer truncation of 65,536 times the binary32 result of one minus twice that raw draw divided by 32,767. The angle is 360 times its draw divided by 32,767. The base Z is copied for the placement helper. The helper can subsequently replace the angle and choose final Z.

## Additional death draws at 0x2A7CA0

This routine first obtains a deterministic world location tag through `0x2ADA20` at `0x2A7D2A`. It visits the World actor list forward. Every category-zero actor whose three-dimensional squared distance from the death point is strictly below 10,000 voxel units consumes `0x2A7DF8`. This test uses the three binary32 scaled fixed-point differences and imposes no HP filter. Even calls `0x2D0680` at `0x2A7E35` and odd calls `0x2D15D0` at `0x2A7E27`. In both cases the generator takes the death helper's input level and that recipient candidate's class byte `+0x14C` as selector, with the generator mode byte zero where used. When generation returns, scatter at `0x2A7EC1`, `0x2A7ECE`, `0x2A7F42` and place at `0x2A7F8A`.

After scanning the list, it always draws at `0x2A7FC6`. The count is signed truncation of 10 times `0x061000` of the passed level, times one plus the normalized draw. For positive count, repeat that many times and scatter at `0x2A803A`, `0x2A8047`, `0x2A80B6` before placing at `0x2A80F2`. These are type-`0x0C` records. Finally, for each integer from 1 through the passed level, draw at `0x2A8117`. On residue 0 modulo 8, create type `0x0B` with subtype equal to the iteration plus `0x1F` and level byte equal to the iteration. Scatter at `0x2A816D`, `0x2A817A`, `0x2A81EE` and place at `0x2A822A`. No category-zero actor in range means the per-actor draws are empty while the count draw and final per-level sequence still run.

## Common placement draws at 0x2A5B70

Every placement call first takes draw `0x2A5CB3`, which sets a duration from 500 plus the residue over 300. It happens even when the placement later returns without an item. If there is a nonzero-category source and the item type is in the `0x454083FC` bit set for types below 31, a deterministic region tag can replace the item tag. This uses `0x00EAB0` at `0x2A5D5E`, then either the source's stored horizontal origin at `+0x1B8` and `+0x1C0` or an origin reconstructed through `0x036160` and `0x0362A0` from the source position, according to the returned block byte `+5` bit `0x20`. It passes that origin through `0x2ADA20` at `0x2A5E35`.

Recipient selection is reached for types in mask `0x0440D3FC` up to type `0x1A`, or for type 1 subtype `0x0D`, unless a nonnull category-zero source caused it to be bypassed. For a category-1 source it first builds candidates from the source's `+0xB88` IDs in tree order. An ID must resolve to a category-zero actor and pass item/class fit `0x1094D0`. For other sources, including a null source, it first collects eligible category-zero actors with that fit in World list order and binary32 squared distance strictly below 65,536 from the proposed position. If either first list is empty, use all category-zero actors in World list order. An empty fallback exits the placement without a second draw.

When the list is nonempty, `0x2A6448` selects one candidate by residue over the list count. The chosen actor's class at `+0x14C` is tested by `0x1094D0` again at `0x2A6464`. On success the recipient tagging takes place. It also enables the level-increase sequence for item types 3 through 9 and `0x1A`. Draw at `0x2A6485`, then `0x2A64AB`, then `0x2A64D1`, then `0x2A64F7`. In this sequence a site is reached only when every preceding site divided by 20 with residue zero. Each such success increments the level byte. The final level byte is capped at 4. No redraw follows a failed selection or class check.

The path next looks up the placement chunk from the floored horizontal voxel position divided into 64-voxel chunks. If the World `+0x460` lookup returns a real entry with a nonnull chunk, `0x2A66E9` consumes one further draw and replaces the caller's scatter angle by 360 times this draw divided by 32,767. Block checks underneath the proposed position choose the final Z and append the resident item. A missing chunk skips this draw. The caller's original scatter angle had already consumed its draw before the lookup. Recipient selection and level checks precede chunk lookup and can therefore consume draws even for a position with no resident chunk.

## Deterministic arithmetic in the same graph

Position caches and chunk maps use deterministic key hashing. For example `0x2E0710` and the chunk lookup `0x012700` hash the two 32-bit chunk coordinates byte by byte in little-endian X then Y order using the 64-bit FNV-1a offset basis `0xCBF29CE484222325` and prime `0x100000001B3`. The hash is masked to choose a bucket. It does not consume the CRT stream.

The world location-tag paths beginning at `0x2A5D5E` and `0x2A7D2A` can reach `0x036160`, `0x0362A0`, `0x2ADA20`, `0x2AD4D0`, `0x2B9080`, `0x2B9210` and `0x2B99D0`. Their coordinate mixing uses coordinate buckets, independent salts and 32-bit wraparound. In this family a coordinate word is XORed with itself shifted left 13 before the polynomial. The polynomial multiplies the square of that word by `0xEC4D`, adds `0x0131071F`, multiplies by the word and adds `0xD208DD0D`. The low 31 bits are used. Coordinate selectors commonly divide that result by `0x53E2` before the final coordinate or range selection. The position tag also uses deterministic spatial noise via `0x02E0C0`. These helpers add no resolved draws and do not set the incoming CRT seed.

Mana, defense and maximum-HP helpers also read item state. The variations at `0x108D50`, `0x1097B0` and `0x10A1E0` add respective constants `0x18008`, `0x2673` and `0x1A6D` to the 32-bit item word at `+8`. Within unsigned 32-bit arithmetic they successively XOR with the value shifted left 13, right 17 and left 5, then take the residue over 100,000. These are pure operations that can use a previously generated variation word without making a new draw. As a bounded arithmetic check, variation word zero produces respective residues 90,728, 66,964 and 18,731. Variation word one produces 61,097, 36,691 and 48,424. Other item scaling and actor flags come after or around these local calculations.

The `0x2A50FD` sign-bit change uses the constant mask starting at `0x4ACB30`. It is ordinary binary32 amount encoding. The double absolute-distance mask at `0x4ACB10`, the modulo-reduction reciprocal constants and the stack security cookie are also unrelated to stream progression.

## Limits of static resolution

The game-object call targets at `0x2A5959` and `0x2A5602` depend on the actual actor's deleting virtual slot. Their target bodies and any stream effects through them are unresolved here. The known list and index operations preceding those calls add no draw. Combat logging in `0x007080` uses stream-buffer virtual slots at `0x0070FB` and `0x00722B` on its runtime-backed logging paths. The concrete stream-buffer objects are outside this proof. The associated arithmetic and draw order give the resolved game path.

Tile lookup and item placement can enter runtime local-static initialization through `0x00E770`. The computed synchronization call at `0x392ED7` and tail call at `0x392E6F` have semantic targets recoverable from the import-lookup setup at `0x392BF0`, which stores the encoded addresses of `SleepConditionVariableCS` and `WakeAllConditionVariable`. The selected native synchronization branch and runtime address depend on initialization and platform state. Ordinary event fallback calls and fatal length or stack-cookie paths are separate from the draw sequences.

A static image cannot choose which actors are resident, which terrain blocks carry the qualifying flags, the current timers and HP, the applicable item lists, runtime item and chunk availability, or the inherited CRT state on this invocation. The contract specifies what those inputs gate and which resolved calls consume the resulting draws. Indirect dispatch, CRT provider internals, and the resident and pet calls outside the two assigned sites are outside the claimed stream count.
