Row Y begins at 197 and advances by 17 while less than 316, producing seven rows with indices zero through six. These are widget-local coordinate units before the drawing path's reached scale/translation, not a claim of physical display pixels. The draw makes a background-square call and then a smaller color-square call through UI-server virtual +0x30, using sizes 17 and 15 and an inner position offset of one.

Each row starts from its column's base four-float record. Rows zero, one and two multiply RGB by the single-precision result of row index times 0.25 followed by addition of 0.2; alpha is unchanged. Row three leaves the base record unchanged. Rows four, five and six subtract 3.0 from the single-precision row index, multiply by 0.25, and for each RGBA component add that fraction of the difference toward the original all-one record at RVA 0x42E8C0. The constants were checked in the image, including 0.2 as bits 0x3E4CCCCD, 0.25 as 0x3E800000, 3.0 as 0x40400000 and 255.0 as 0x437F0000.

The palette obtains local cursor components through 0x32B180 in two selected calls, taking X from the first and Y from the second. A square is selected when each integer component is at least its lower X/Y coordinate and strictly less than that coordinate plus 17. For a hit, native conversion order is blue, green, then red: each selected float is multiplied by 255.0 and converted toward zero to a signed 64-bit integer. The low red/green bytes are combined in a two-byte store to S+0x257/+0x258, then the low blue byte is stored to S+0x259.

There is no mouse-button-held or press-event condition in this selected palette hit/store block. The actual draw itself can change working color from the current cursor position.
