# Steam ground pickup cadence and success music

## Image and scope

The supported image is the installed Windows x64 Steam `cubeworld.exe`, 5,939,072 bytes, SHA-256 `a1efbbd41eeb8df08395c4d6511194d416cb70a14054b2a2000b9f5cdbcfa65b`. The archived original matches. The examined unpacked image is 5,757,952 bytes, SHA-256 `7af18cb28c8bf848f355287428c6390b2ea2a682ca466190f5eb9859c5d6f244`, with preferred base `0x140000000`. All addresses below are RVAs in that image. The evidence is original Steam instructions, call relations, strings and image constants. Infinite-Cubes was used for leads, with the contracts below checked in the Steam evidence.

G denotes the Game object, W the current Game world at G+0x08, and C its local creature at W+0x448. The authoritative host has its own world and creature records. Offsets are byte offsets. Positions relevant to pickup are signed 64-bit fixed-point coordinates with 65,536 units per block. Timings below use milliseconds.

## Success music in the Game update

G+0x2050 is a signed 32-bit success-music countdown. Game construction initializes it to zero at RVA 0x7ef39 in 0x7ea80. Direct references to the Game field establish five event writes of 60,000 and one recurring countdown store. Each event write replaces the prior value. In execution order within a normal Game update, the five sites are as follows.

| Event | Routine and write RVA | Admission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New completion of the sampled place | 0xa5a50 at 0xa7471 | The first completion sample is false and the second is true. |
| Direct interaction with a personal reward chest | 0x98180 at 0x9a205 | An actual eligible open and passage of the presentation-pose gate. The update calls 0x8ae50 at 0xa9b2c, which calls 0x98180 at 0x8df69. |
| Speaker dialogue gift | 0xb40d0 at 0xb51d9 | Active first-read page has a nonzero gift count and the local creature passes its item-admission test. |
| Main speech panel gift | 0xb40d0 at 0xb591d | Active first-read page has a nonzero gift count and the local creature passes its item-admission test. |
| Local item pickup receipt | 0xa5a50 at 0xaad54 | Receipt reaches the presentation dialogue and passes the presentation-pose gate. Multiple receipts are visited in their list order. |

The speech pass at 0xaa1bc visits the speaker dialogues before the main speech panel, then returns before the pickup receipt loop beginning at 0xaa620. The success countdown sees all admitted writes above in that update. Multiple admitted writes in the update yield one countdown subtraction from the latest 60,000.

The Game update takes a binary64 elapsed time in milliseconds from 0x134420 and caps it at 1,000 at 0xa5afc–0xa5b0a. Late in 0xa5a50, it subtracts the capped binary64 elapsed time from the signed integer at G+0x2050, truncates the result toward zero to a signed 32-bit integer, and clamps a negative result to zero before storing it at 0xac0dd. The separate G+0x204c timer receives the same treatment immediately afterward. Success eligibility uses the reloaded G+0x2050 at 0xac106, strictly greater than zero. The full capped update time applies even to a write made earlier in the same update. The time is subtracted once per Game update across any number of binary32 world substeps. Fractional milliseconds are discarded at the integer store each time.

Selection of `success.ogg` at 0xac116 follows the location, spirit-world and boss choices. A visible menu at G+0x118, G+0x170 or G+0x130 subsequently selects `maintheme.ogg` at 0xac17d. Selection is a candidate for the music system. The track-change path at 0xac307 uses the candidate to update the shared music path at G+0x2028 when the audio status and name comparison admit it. That path and the volume state at G+0x2048 are shared with the music worker under the critical section at G+0x1d38, as seen in 0x83fb0. The 500 ms transition threshold and 10,000 ms positive-success threshold in this logic are distinct from the 60,000 ms event writes.

## Mission completion and discovery

At 0xa7167–0xa7224, the update derives the place key from the local creature's horizontal position before the world simulation. Each axis uses the integral block coordinate truncated toward zero, then signed floor division by 1,024. When the large-place test at 0x36160 admits it, each resulting signed 32-bit axis is rounded down to its even cell origin. The update preserves both key axes for the second sample, including when the creature moves during simulation.

The shared membership test at 0x5e430 returns false if the creature lacks player data. Otherwise it searches for the exact signed coordinate pair in the completion collections at player-data offsets +0xc8 and +0xd8, in that order, and either match is sufficient. Set changes associated with mission state are visible in 0x131620, reached through 0x2e1750 at the end of each 0x2da6c0 world substep. The Game conditionally runs that world step between the two samples. For a matching active world seed and name, the capped time is split into 16 ms substeps with a final remaining step of at most 16 ms. The update performs its maximum-health adjustment after the substeps. When the first sample was false, 0xa7464 checks the same place key against the current local creature. A true second result writes the timer at 0xa7471 before the mission-kind-dependent dialogue decisions.

The arrival/location notice, a revealed map marker, and the mission-completion membership test are separate observations. The transition above depends only on a false-to-true result for the sampled pair. A pickup or dialogue reward applied later in the Game update falls after both samples. A completion-set change made by a later world substep can satisfy the transition if the first sample of that later update was still false. Evidence for the sampling boundary and membership is stronger than a mapping of all mission kinds or location discoveries to their set changes.

## Receipt presentation, reward chests, and gifts

The pose gate common to these paths admits an idle current ability byte at C+0x68, or an elapsed ability time at C+0x6c strictly greater than the signed integer attack duration from 0x4f220 converted to binary32. Equality fails the gate. The current ability and elapsed-time fields are one byte and binary32 respectively. The presented action item is at C+0x1d8.

**Pickup receipt.** The ordered receipt list at W+0x110 carries a 64-bit recipient identity, item, unsigned 16-bit quantity, three 64-bit fixed-point position components, and flags. In a receipt list node these fields begin at +0x10, +0x18, +0xb8, +0xc0 and +0xd8 respectively. The host receipt builder widens the 8-bit ground flags by sign extension to 32 bits, and the Game's per-player test uses the low byte. The Game resolves the recipient and transforms the item for that creature through 0x10a720. The grant and success path require the recipient to be local. With flag mask 0x02 set, the Game tests the player's set at player-data +0x88 using all three position components floored to blocks and records the key when absent. A matching key skips the receipt's local grant, presentation and notification. An unresolved recipient skips the receipt entirely. A resolved remote recipient can reach ordinary notification without running the local grant and success path. These gates are at 0xaa628–0xaa721 and 0xaad68–0xaadae.

The local grant occurs before the presentation logic. Category 0x0c with variant byte 0x0d at item offset +0x1c adds receipt quantity to a signed 32-bit regional balance keyed by the creature's current region. A nonzero gold-bag value from 0x109070 adds that value to the signed 32-bit gold balance at C+0xaac. Otherwise the item and quantity go to the inventory grant at 0x4e810. For normal category 0x0c, variant 0x0b coins, this grant increases the signed 32-bit inventory coin balance at inventory +0xbc by receipt quantity. ManaCube category 0x0e has its own inventory counter. Standard coins and mana cubes follow ordinary notification and bypass the success presentation.

After the local grant and inventory refresh, the original category 0x1e marks the pickup's horizontal tile in the player's marker set and enters presentation dialogue. The tile uses floor division by 16 blocks. Other receipts enter that dialogue only for resolved category 0x17, 0x18 or 0x1d. The dialogue is generated first with one `rand` draw reduced modulo 10,000. The pose gate then determines whether the timer write at 0xaad54 occurs. A blocked pose gate leaves the previously applied grant and generated dialogue in effect and bypasses this write.

For an admitted category 0x18, item subtype at +0x04 is 32-bit. Subtype 1 is Hang Glider and selects ability 0x78. Subtype 2 is Boat. It selects ability 0x79 only with mask 0x02 set in the creature flags at C+0x5c, the in-water flag. A dry boat receipt jumps straight to the success-timer write, leaving the current ability, elapsed time and action item untouched. Other admitted items select the presentation ability 0x7c. When a pose is selected, the receipt assigns its ability byte, copies the resolved action item, and zeros its binary32 elapsed time before writing 60,000. RVAs 0xaac96–0xaad54 and the item-name registration references 0x15592e and 0x155988 establish this split.

**Personal reward chest.** The direct use case at 0x98180 checks a selected static object of type 0x16, an active byte at object +0x28, a previously unseen horizontal tile in the local player's marker set at player-data +0xa8, and a nonempty reward at the selected object index. The chest's active byte is maintained from the local marker set at 0x2e3510. On a fresh use press at G+0x1a7b admitted by the interaction handler, it records the tile, obtains the reward, transforms a generated reward (category 0x1e), and generates the receipt dialogue with one `rand` draw reduced modulo 10,000. When the pose gate passes, it sets ability 0x7c, zeros the elapsed field, copies the item, and writes the timer at 0x9a205. The reward is granted afterward even when the pose gate fails. A nonzero gold-bag value goes to C+0xaac. Otherwise it adds one item and performs ordinary notification. The chest uses the presentation pose for a boat reward too. The other static-object interaction that queues a host open request is a separate route. A resulting dropped item reaches the ordinary pickup receipt path if collected.

**Dialogue gifts.** The Game's speech pass at 0xb40d0 checks a nonempty dialogue with a valid selected page and binary32 elapsed read time exactly zero. The page stride is 0x1b0. Gift count and item are at page +0xd4 and +0xd8, with a signed 32-bit count. In the speaker path the optional take operation is applied before the gift check. In the main speech panel the take operation is inside the nonzero gift-count branch and follows the gift attempt, including an item-admission failure. In both paths an admitted gift is added to local inventory and notified, then sets 60,000. A gift of exactly one category 0x18 item additionally generates a received-item page using one `rand` draw reduced modulo 10,000. Its optional 0x7c presentation uses the pose gate after the timer write. The gift itself sets the countdown even when presentation cannot start. Boat and glider subtype checks belong to the pickup receipt path. The Goddess examination at 0x98180 creates a page with gift count one, category 0x18 and subtype 4 for the main speech panel. Its timer writer is the page read at 0xb591d, which can run later in the same Game pass if that page is eligible.

## Automatic nearby coin requests

**Game producer.** The ordinary update calls the input and targeting pass 0x8ae50 at 0xa9b2c, after the local world substeps. The call is gated by the loaded-world field at W+0x210, local ability exclusions 0x9c and 0x9d, and visibility tests on the three menu nodes. Input processing itself has additional early exits. When the ground scan runs, it visits loaded 64-block zones covering the player's horizontal position plus and minus eight blocks, inclusive, in ascending zone X then ascending zone Y. A zone's ground slots are visited from index zero upward, at stride 0xe0. The fixed coordinate-to-zone conversion at 0x16e50 floors negative positions correctly.

For a normal category 0x0c coin (the Coin key in 0x1510f0 at 0x1557f2), the availability test at 0x2b8140 requires a nonempty category, nonzero signed 32-bit quantity at ground +0xd4, and no reservation or a matching 64-bit owner seed at ground +0xd8. The same automatic branch covers category 0x0e (ManaCube). It uses the player's current position, converts each fixed-point delta to binary32 blocks, and sums the three squares in binary32. The inclusive cutoff is 16.0 square blocks. The branch requires ground flags at +0xc4 to have mask 0x02 clear and the binary32 landing delay at ground +0xc8 to equal zero exactly. This scan uses no action-button edge and no camera target. It admits every eligible slot encountered in the pass. Admission uses the landing delay at +0xc8. The zone phase also maintains a separate signed 32-bit timer at +0xcc.

For each eligible slot the pass prepares one interaction type 7 with the copied item and a key of three signed 32-bit values, zone X, zone Y and slot index. They are at offsets +0xa0, +0xa4 and +0xa8 of the interaction body. The type is one byte at +0xc4. The scan searches the creature's pending queue at C+0xab8 for the same type, same three key values and equal item under 0x4df60. This compares item identity fields including category, subtype, seed, region, rarity and the active enhancement entries. Ground quantity is outside the deduplication key. The first match suppresses append. An otherwise new request is added to the tail, and the scan continues to later slots. Evidence includes 0x8d47a–0x8dd0f, particularly 0x8d5a7, 0x8d6d5 and 0x8d701.

**Client send and host receive.** The Game reaches the client network pump at 0xaaebf, after the scan and pickup receipt pass. In 0x3f4f0, the regular send interval must be strictly greater than 32 ms since the last send interval, and the normal connection gates must pass. One eligible send pass takes at most one interaction from the front of the pending queue. It removes that entry immediately and sends one interaction message, message type 9 and 200-byte interaction body, through 0x3f460. The remaining queue stays in order for later eligible passes. A later scan can enqueue the same still-visible slot again after its earlier request has left the queue. The deduplication covers pending local entries only. This applies to normal in-process and remote delivery. In-process delivery queues the message at 0xf32c0 for the host receive phase. The host drains available input messages before its world steps at 0xf5520, with 0xf4490 decoding each type-9 message and appending its entry to the matching creature's authoritative queue. The local-host worker in 0x83e60 normally sleeps to a 32 ms cadence. Client send and host receipt are separate phases with independent timing.

**Authoritative pickup.** Each 0x2da6c0 substep first runs the zone phase at callsite 0x2da73c. The substep later visits creatures in world list order. For each living creature, the interaction pass at 0x2db6ba runs 0x2c3710. The client-mode world passes over this operation at 0x2c379b. On the authoritative side, it traverses the entire queued list from front to back and clears the list at the end at 0x2c51b0. More than one interaction can be consumed within one substep when the host already has multiple entries for that creature. Each type-7 request checks the current zone, signed slot index in bounds, current three-dimensional binary32 squared distance at most 16.0, and item equality with the current slot. These tests happen again after any earlier entries' changes. The producer checks the landing delay. The host evaluates the request against the current slot using the tests above.

For an accepted ordinary coin request, the host appends a pickup sound event at the creature position, sound 0x30 (`pickup.wav`) with volume 1.0 and one `rand` draw for playback speed, scaled by 0.1, divided by 32,767, then offset by 1.0. It then appends the result with the creature ID, copied item, low 16 bits of the ground quantity, ground position and widened flags. With mask 0x02 clear, it marks the zone's ground items changed and resets the collected slot. Subsequent requests use their original slot indices and see the collected slot as empty. The Game's later receipt pass performs the inventory grant. RVAs 0x2c3c68, 0x2c3d2f, 0x2c3d4e, 0x2c3dc4, 0x2c3ebc, 0x2c3f21 and 0x2c3fb6 establish this order. A repeat request for an emptied slot fails the identity comparison and emits neither event.

**Ground delay.** For ordinary spawned drops through 0x2a5b70, one `rand` draw reduced modulo 300 and offset by 500 gives a binary32 delay of 500–799 ms. In the zone phase at 0x2e3510, ground records in the visited zones with positive delay have the binary32 substep delta subtracted, with values at or below zero set to zero at 0x2e498f. This phase also decreases the separate positive signed 32-bit timer at +0xcc using binary32 subtraction with truncation and a zero floor before processing the delay. When the delay reaches zero, the authoritative side queues the category 0x0c drop sound 0x3d (`drop-coin.wav`) at the ground position. This landing event uses its own single randomized speed draw and precedes the interaction pass of that substep. The local Game ground scan sees the delay after its world substeps, so it can request a coin whose delay crossed zero during them. A drop created in the authoritative interaction phase is after that substep's delay phase and can first age in a later substep that visits its zone.

## Evidence limits

The direct field-reference audit bounds the constructor, countdown, and five event sites above for this image. An exhaustive proof for computed aliases or other images was outside scope. The audited mission predicate and sequence establish the exact sampled membership transition. They leave the catalog of mission-kind prerequisites, marker effects and art variants open. The host loop establishes per-creature request order and the local phase boundaries. Actual inter-thread scheduling, packet arrival frame, unloaded-zone timing and competition among different players for one slot require runtime observation. Other pickup variants and other audio transitions fall outside this review.
