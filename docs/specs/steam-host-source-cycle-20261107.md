# Native Steam host source-loading cycle and retention

This contract concerns the ordinary host-owned source-loading worker at RVA 0xF2340, its directly reached source retirement and factory calls, the separately reached logical-resident admission and retention calls, and the minimum directly proved World+0x358 history needed to distinguish World instances. It applies to the original x64 Steam image binary/cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe, image base 0x140000000, PE timestamp 0x5D93AB0D, SHA256 7AF18CB28C8BF848F355287428C6390B2EA2A682CA466190F5EB9859C5D6F244. Original evidence was read from individually identified files under decompiled/functions and the original decompiled/assembly.txt listing; literal and function-pointer identities were checked against that image. This is original-derived behavioral prose, not runtime capture evidence. Earlier held contracts remain unchanged.

Game construction at RVA 0x7EA80 keeps the supplied viewed/client World pointer at Game+0x8 and constructs a separate host subobject at Game+0x6E8: the address is selected at RVA 0x7EB24 and its constructor at RVA 0xF17A0 is called at RVA 0x7EB36. That host constructor calls the World constructor at RVA 0x29A130 on Host+0x8 at RVA 0xF17E1. The host-owned embedded World therefore lies at Game+0x6F0; it must not be conflated with the World reached through Game+0x8.

The host constructor writes one to its byte at Host+0x550 at RVA 0xF17E7 and launches a worker through RVA 0xF0150. The original thread entry at RVA 0x3C7D0 invokes the supplied functor. Its concrete vtable entry at image RVA 0x43FD18 points to the thunk at RVA 0xF3070, which reaches RVA 0xF3220; that callback calls RVA 0xF2340 at RVA 0xF3292. An eight-byte wrapper carries the host pointer. These links establish a host-owned worker executing this loading loop. They do not turn the batch limit of four into a thread count. Host destruction at RVA 0xF1F20 clears byte +0x550 at RVA 0xF1F45 and joins the stored worker at RVA 0xF1F78 through RVA 0x9AF20 before later host teardown. The latter helper directly waits for thread completion and clears its stored thread identity.

At the start of each admitted loading cycle, RVA 0xF2340 enters the host World's critical section at World+0x3C8 through RVA 0x2ABB70, called at RVA 0xF23D0. It walks the live resident list at World+0x40 in its existing traversal order and copies a two-component centre for every resident whose byte +0x60 is zero. The byte test is at RVA 0xF2414. This contract uses that literal eligibility predicate rather than assuming that every possible object satisfying it belongs to one untraced gameplay category.

The centre sampling has two directly distinct cases. A resident whose byte +0x68 equals 0x9C supplies the two signed 32-bit components at resident+0x1B0 and +0x1B4, read at RVAs 0xF2429 and 0xF2432. Otherwise the signed 64-bit fixed-position components at resident+0x10 and +0x18 are divided by 65,536 with truncation toward zero, then narrowed to signed 32-bit block components; the native conversion is at RVAs 0xF2462–0xF2485. In particular, this initial conversion of a negative fractional position is not a floor toward negative infinity. The purpose of the 0x9C alternative fields is not established here. The copied centres, rather than live position rereads, drive the remaining cycle.

The worker releases World+0x3C8 at RVA 0xF24DE and samples a 32-bit millisecond time at RVA 0xF24E3, storing it at RVA 0xF24E9. It then creates a new local unique coordinate tree, through RVA 0x86EA0 at RVA 0xF250D, and a new initially empty candidate vector. The unique-tree insertion helper at RVA 0x745E0 compares signed 32-bit X first and Y second. Walking this tree in order therefore visits unique signed coordinate pairs in that order. It is a union across all copied centres, not a separate independently processed loading batch for each centre.

For each centre, the demand scan includes both endpoints of the zone bounds obtained by subtracting and adding 270 blocks to each signed 32-bit centre component and dividing by 64 with floor toward negative infinity. Its reference centre zone is obtained by the same floor division of the sampled block component by 64. A scanned coordinate must differ from that centre zone by fewer than six zones on each axis, and the sum of the squared zone offsets, evaluated in double precision, must be at most 25. The axis admissions are visible at RVAs 0xF2668 and 0xF2679; the radius literal is the double at image RVA 0x43FD20. Admitted keys are inserted at RVA 0xF26C5. The ordinary mathematical region stated here excludes signed 32-bit overflow cases in centre addition, subtraction or subsequent coordinate arithmetic, whose useful domain is not established by this audit.

The values 77, 78 and 79 describe independently calculated sizes of this one-centre wanted-coordinate set. They are not numbered scheduler phases. For a block component's floor-division remainder from 0 through 63, remainders 0 through 13 give relative scan bounds from minus five through plus four; remainders 14 through 49 give minus four through plus four; remainders 50 through 63 give minus four through plus five. The complete integer radius-five disk has 81 coordinates. The middle remainder range excludes both radius-five axial endpoints for that axis, whereas either outer remainder range excludes one. Thus both component remainders in 14 through 49 give 77 coordinates, exactly one in that range gives 78, and neither gives 79. For example, remainder pairs 14/14, 14/0 and 0/0 give 77, 78 and 79 respectively. These are arithmetic derivations from the proved admissions, not observed native loading counts. Multiple eligible centres contribute their union, so these single-centre numbers do not specify a multi-resident total.

Before looking for missing generation candidates, the worker calls required-set retirement at RVA 0x2D2670, from RVA 0xF2718. That helper scans the loaded source map at World+0x460 under World+0x3F0, entered at RVA 0x2D26CB. Its membership test uses the loaded source object's own signed 32-bit zone components at source+0x10 and +0x14, read at RVAs 0x2D2704 and 0x2D270F. Every scanned source outside the supplied required-coordinate set is appended to a local retirement list. There is no four-source retirement cap in this body, and this admission does not inspect render preparation, mesh activity or logical-resident presence. The helper is distinct from the other source-retirement routine at RVA 0x2D2410; the host worker directly calls 0x2D2670 here.

The retirement scan releases World+0x3F0 at RVA 0x2D284F. For each saved map-node iterator it obtains the source pointer before reacquiring the guards, enters World+0x3F0 at RVA 0x2D286F, then World+0x3C8 at RVA 0x2D287C, erases that loaded-map node through RVA 0x2ABD20 at RVA 0x2D2892, and calls the selected-state retention helper at RVA 0x2D3E20 from RVA 0x2D289D. It releases World+0x3C8 at RVA 0x2D28A9 and World+0x3F0 at RVA 0x2D28B6. Only afterwards does it invoke the source object's deleting virtual entry, with argument one, at RVA 0x2D28CC. Retained selected state and continued residency of the source object are therefore separate facts. The helper frees its local retirement list before returning.

This retirement path reads source coordinates through the loaded pointer without a preceding null-source admission on the scan. Its stated ordinary behavior presumes nonnull loaded entries. It also preserves raw map-node iterators across the unlocked interval between selection and erasure. The observed guard order and these exact scopes do not prove safety against every alternate caller, concurrent map mutation or destruction route. No task-completion barrier or extra lifetime ownership is inferred from a critical-section name.

After retirement returns, RVA 0xF2340 walks the local required tree in order and queries the loaded source map. An existing nonnull source excludes the key from generation candidates. A missing entry or an entry containing a null source enters candidate scoring. A loaded hit therefore consumes no place in the cap-four generation batch and does not request generation merely because the key remains required. The candidate records are 12 bytes: two signed 32-bit zone components followed by a signed 32-bit priority key. Scoring starts with the negative sentinel minus one, admits a centre inside the zone's closed 64-block square with priority zero, otherwise considers the reached centre-dependent native distance cases and retains the minimum admitted key. This audit does not replace the separately bounded priority-arithmetic or exact tie-order contract with a simplified distance interpretation.

An empty candidate vector takes the five-millisecond sleep call at RVA 0xF2C2B. A nonempty vector is sorted through RVA 0xF0790, called at RVA 0xF2C7A. Its reached comparisons, including RVAs 0xF0070, 0xF0450 and 0xF02C0, compare the signed 32-bit priority member in ascending order. The driver has partition and heap paths as well as a small-range insertion path; this is not a general promise of stable equal-key ordering. Candidate construction initially follows the required tree's coordinate order, but this contract assigns no universal coordinate tie break after sorting.

If the sorted vector contains more than four records, the worker truncates its used range to the first four through RVA 0xF4E60, called at RVA 0xF2C8F. It then visits that remaining vector from its beginning in 12-byte increments and calls the source factory at RVA 0x2AE0C0 from RVA 0xF2CBE. Each call returns before the next record is visited. These are serial factory requests on this worker, not four submitted tasks. The caller does not test the returned source pointer and does not resample resident positions or the run-control byte between those individual calls. An admitted batch may therefore finish its selected calls after another thread has cleared Host+0x550. Four requests do not guarantee four newly installed sources: the factory itself can find an already installed nonnull source and return it.

The factory at RVA 0x2AE0C0 first performs a loaded-map lookup and takes its existing-nonnull fast-return branch at RVA 0x2AE129. On its reached missing/null route it constructs the source through RVA 0x2F9400, called at RVA 0x2AE16A, and runs source generation through RVA 0x2FF6F0, called at RVA 0x2AE179. The selected retained-state restoration branch follows generation. Only afterwards does it acquire World+0x3F0 and then World+0x3C8, at RVAs 0x2AE321 and 0x2AE32E, obtain the map node through RVA 0x299640 at RVA 0x2AE34A, and store the source pointer at RVA 0x2AE354. It releases World+0x3C8 at RVA 0x2AE35F and World+0x3F0 at RVA 0x2AE36C. This directly inspected body does not publish an early in-progress null placeholder before generation. That observation is not a proof that no transitive generation helper can expose any pointer elsewhere.

Both the empty and nonempty candidate paths then take the zero-millisecond sleep call at RVA 0xF2CCE. There is no fixed per-cycle period established by these calls. The empty path requests a five-millisecond sleep before this yield; a nonempty path finishes its selected serial factory calls before the yield. Generation duration, scheduling and reached persistence work contribute to elapsed time. The code does not wait for all required coordinates to be loaded before ending a cycle.

A separate periodic state-snapshot path follows the yield. The unsigned 32-bit elapsed-time comparison at RVAs 0xF2CDE–0xF2CE3 requires strictly more than 60,000 milliseconds between the cycle's earlier time sample and the retained prior sample. If Host+0x550 is still nonzero, the worker enters World+0x3C8 at RVA 0xF2D04, copies the selected World state comprising the two 32-bit fields at +0x8/+0xC and the containers at +0x10/+0x20/+0x30, and releases that guard at RVA 0xF2D5E before calling RVA 0x2D38E0 at RVA 0xF2D6F. The remembered time becomes the earlier cycle sample, not a fresh post-generation or post-persistence clock read. RVA 0x2D38E0 has its own nonnull World+0x210 gate and reaches the state persistence interface; successful durable storage and an exact one-minute wall-clock cadence are not established here.

The local required tree is destroyed at RVAs 0xF2E18 and 0xF2E2A, the candidate allocation is released through RVA 0xF3C70 at RVA 0xF2E53, and the copied centre list is cleared and freed through RVA 0xF2E95. The worker reads Host+0x550 again at RVA 0xF2E9E and starts another cycle only while admitted. The required set, ordered candidates and centre snapshot do not persist as a queued plan into the next cycle. The loaded source map and other state held by still-resident source objects do persist until their own mutation or retirement. A previously unselected missing key is reconsidered only through the next fresh demand and candidate construction. This is not evidence of a separate failure/retry object, cancellation protocol or graceful recovery from allocation or generation failure.

If no eligible centre was copied, the local required set remains empty. Under the ordinary nonnull-map assumption, required-set retirement selects every currently loaded source; the generation vector remains empty and the five-millisecond sleep path is taken. The worker's exit path is different: it enters World+0x3C8, takes the selected state snapshot through RVA 0xF1C90 at RVA 0xF2EFF, releases the guard at RVA 0xF2F0D, calls RVA 0x2D38E0 at RVA 0xF2F1E and destroys that snapshot. It does not call required-set retirement as part of that exit sequence.

Logical-resident admission is reached separately from the host simulation/update routine at RVA 0xF5520. That routine enters the host World's World+0x3C8 at RVA 0xF68CD, reaches ordinary World updates at RVA 0x2DA6C0 through RVAs 0xF6917 and 0xF692D, and releases the guard at RVA 0xF69F8. The first call site can repeat for reached catch-up work. In the World update, the byte World+0x358 is tested at RVA 0x2DA8CE; its zero branch calls logical admission at RVA 0x2E17D0 from RVA 0x2DA8DF, then logical retention at RVA 0x2A56C0 from RVA 0x2DA8E7. RVA 0x2E17D0 also has its own zero-byte gate.

That logical admission routine first selects the existing live residents whose byte +0x60 is zero. Its source selection uses those residents' physical signed 64-bit fixed-position fields +0x10/+0x18, without the loader's byte-0x9C alternate-centre case. It scans the inclusive zone bounds for physical position minus and plus 256 blocks on each horizontal axis, with the reached negative floor corrections. In the ordinary nonoverflow domain this gives a nine-by-nine square of potential zone keys per centre, without the loader's radius-five disk test. Only an existing nonnull source-map hit contributes to its unique local source-pointer set. Missing or null keys are skipped by this selection; it neither calls the source factory nor adds missing keys to a generation queue.

For each selected loaded source, logical admission calls RVA 0x31C540 at RVA 0x2E1D7C and visits that source's zone-owned resident-template collection at source+0x460. This source-local collection is distinct from the loaded source map at World+0x460. Actual live admission also checks existing World+0x450 resident identities, the World+0x20 collection, centre-dependent proximity and further record-specific prerequisites. A reached admitted branch constructs a live resident at RVA 0x2E23E3 and inserts it through RVA 0x29D0D0 at RVA 0x2E283A; the insertion helper appends to World+0x40 and records the identity in World+0x450. Loaded source residency thus supplies data for admission but does not itself mean that every source template becomes a live resident. The full per-template gameplay policy is outside this audit.

There is one directly proved cross-call source-local refresh gate relevant to the distinction. The source constructor initializes the 32-bit field source+0x4F8 to minus one at RVA 0x2F9732. RVA 0x31C540 compares that field with the owning World's 32-bit field +0x8 at RVA 0x31C589; the equal case branches to return at RVA 0x31C590, and inequality stores the World value at RVA 0x31C596 before doing its reached refresh work. No day, seed or loader-completion label is assigned to those fields here. This retained source-local comparison is separate from a loading cycle's temporary wanted set and from the existence checks used for live admission.

The immediately following logical retention routine at RVA 0x2A56C0 uses yet another region. It retains live residents whose byte +0x60 is nonzero when they lie within a horizontal radius of 320 blocks of at least one byte-zero centre resident. The reached double-precision tests include component magnitudes at most 320 and squared horizontal distance at most 102,400; the image literals are at RVAs 0x472220 and 0x4724A8, with the fixed-unit scale at image RVA 0x43EF58. This inspected predicate has no vertical component. Residents outside every such centre region are unlinked from World+0x40, removed from World+0x450 through RVA 0x2AC530 and virtually deleted. These are live-resident retention decisions, not the source-map required-set test.

Consequently the 77/78/79 one-centre source-demand counts do not form a readiness barrier for logical admission or a promise of that many admitted live residents. The logical route can process whichever sources in its own square are already loaded while other loader-required keys are missing. Conversely, the loader's retirement predicate contains no exception merely because a live resident or a logical-admission source-pointer selection exists. The loader releases World+0x3C8 after centre sampling and does not hold it continuously through demand construction, map candidate reads or generation. The directly observed guards serialize the reached protected operations, but this bounded audit establishes no global relative schedule between the loading cycle and every host update, no all-demand-complete handshake, and no general safety proof for untraced simultaneous source writers or alternate callers.

The directly identified constant byte writes to World+0x358 are its zero initialization in the World constructor at RVA 0x29A276, the one write in connection setup at RVA 0x3DB2D, and zero writes in disconnect at RVAs 0x91249 and 0x91309. Connection-owner construction at RVA 0x3AA10 stores its Game backpointer at owner+0xA50 at RVA 0x3AB74; setup at RVA 0x3D820 follows that backpointer and Game+0x8 for the one write. Setup compares the supplied identity with the global identity at image RVA 0x54DDC8. The equal-identity case can dispatch directly through RVA 0xF32C0 when owner+0xA10 is nonnull; the unequal-identity case reaches the outgoing interface call. Both converge before the byte-one write. The setup entry at RVA 0x3DBF0 itself supplies that same global identity, so the one write is not restricted to the unequal-identity route.

Disconnect at RVA 0x911E0 reaches these clears only under its nonzero Game byte +0x21E8 branch. One clear follows closing and clearing a nonnull Game+0x21F8 socket. The later clear follows the reached thread joins and occurs while World+0x3C8 is held. These writes target the World through Game+0x8. They do not target the separate embedded host World merely because both participate in a locally hosted session. The host World's directly proved initial byte remains the constructor's zero unless a further writer is established. This is a list of directly identified writes, not proof that every aliasing writer, alternate World setup or later mutation has been exhausted.

In source creation, the byte's directly proved effect is the branch at RVAs 0x2AE17E–0x2AE186: zero permits the reached retained-state lookup and selected restoration from World+0x10 after generation; nonzero jumps to installation at RVA 0x2AE31A and skips that branch. In ordinary World update it separately admits the local logical-resident work described above when zero. These uses and the separate World identities support distinguishing the host-owned restoration route from the viewed/client connection route. They do not justify a universal field name such as remote-only, fully loaded or source-ready.
