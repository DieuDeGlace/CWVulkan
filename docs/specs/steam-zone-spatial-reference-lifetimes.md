# Native Zone spatial reference lifetimes

This bounded contract is FINAL / STABLE / HELD following author readback. Its evidence is the original AMD64 image cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe, 5,757,952 bytes, SHA-256 7AF18CB28C8BF848F355287428C6390B2EA2A682CA466190F5EB9859C5D6F244, and its original function, assembly, call and function-range evidence. Addresses below are RVAs relative to image base 0x140000000. No replacement implementation, SDK, tests, captures, implementation logs or other project prose supplied evidence. The scope is the late generator calls at 0x31d470 and 0x31d700 and the storage paths inspected beneath them, not a complete generator escape census.

The established result is that these two routines rebuild or update Zone-owned value storage. The inspected paths contain no worker submission, external owner registration, reference-count acquisition or storage of their Zone argument or a child-object address into an external queue. Their allocations remain attached to the Zone or are temporary buffers released within the reached operation. This is a bounded finding about the described paths and ordinary completion, not a proof about every call reachable from generator 0x2ff6f0, arbitrary runtime allocation callbacks or every concurrent consumer.

The generator calls 0x31d470 at 0x318f63 and 0x31d700 at 0x318f6c, in that order, loading the same saved Zone address into RCX for each. Both routines consume that single Windows x64 pointer argument. No second input object, asynchronous completion handle or owning return object is established. Their incidental register contents on return are not an ownership transfer. The original full generator decompilation is incomplete; these call boundaries are established from assembly and calls.csv rather than treating the incomplete body as evidence of absence elsewhere.

Routine 0x31d470 first resets the logical end of the Zone vector whose begin is at +0xc0 and end is at +0xc8. It appends selected records from the Zone vector beginning at +0x60 through helper 0x129b50. The source and destination record stride is 0xe0. Helpers 0x9860 and 0x35400 copy fields and embedded bytes into destination storage; vector growth through 0x356c0, 0x35500 and 0x352d0 constructs another owned allocation and replaces the vector storage. The source-record address is not stored as the destination record. This establishes copied records and possible invalidation of the former vector allocation. It does not assign a complete semantic type to every copied record field.

The same routine rebuilds a circular doubly linked list rooted at Zone+0x4b8, with an eight-byte count at +0x4c0. It detaches and frees all old ordinary nodes, restores the existing sentinel's forward and backward links to itself and resets the count before adding new entries. Helper 0xa160 allocates 0x28 bytes per node; links occupy +0 and +8. Helper 0x7520 copies three eight-byte quantities into node+0x10, +0x18 and +0x20. It does not retain the source address. The two insertion sources are selected 0xe0-byte records from Zone+0x30, at record+0xa0, and selected records from Zone+0x60, at record+8. The source selection and geometry algorithms are outside this contract.

An observed later reader is World routine 0x2e3510. It obtains a Zone from the World's spatial map, loads that Zone's +0x4b8 sentinel and traverses the nodes directly. It treats the three payload quantities as signed 64-bit position components: the first two undergo spatial coordinate conversion, and all three participate in position differences against a Creature's three position components. This supports numeric position values rather than borrowed object addresses in this list. The inspected reader fragment does not remove its list nodes. No claim is made that it is the only reader. List nodes persist until another rebuild or Zone cleanup; they do not become independently owned by this observed reader.

Routine 0x31d700 uses a different Zone-owned collection: a unique ordered tree with its header pointer at Zone+0xe8 and eight-byte count at +0xf0. Constructor 0x2f9400 initializes this collection with sentinel helper 0x9f20. Sentinel and ordinary nodes occupy 0x28 bytes. The links are at +0, +8 and +0x10; the color and sentinel bytes are at +0x18 and +0x19. A key is three consecutive 32-bit coordinate values at node+0x1c, +0x20 and +0x24. Insertion 0x1152c0 compares them lexicographically using signed comparisons and suppresses equal keys. Its reached helpers 0x113a10, 0x112f30 and 0xa3a0 allocate a node and copy twelve key bytes. The temporary coordinate triple's address is not retained.

At entry, 0x31d700 traverses the existing coordinate keys and uses them to remove flag 0x40 from the corresponding six-byte cell value where present. It then visits the current Zone+0x60 records, obtains dimensions through 0x13a960 and 0x13a520, and updates affected cells and coordinate keys. The dimension helper writes three float values into caller storage; it does not register the source record. The existing coordinate tree is not emptied by this routine. Coordinate values can therefore remain in it across calls, even though the prior cell flag is cleared before current affected cells are processed. These are retained integer values, not a deferred list of Zone or child pointers.

The cell read helper is 0xeb90. It selects a column inside the Zone's inline array beginning at +0x518, with element stride 0x58, and copies six bytes into caller-provided storage, using 0xe770 for stored elements. The matching write path is 0x31c1c0 followed by 0xa36e0. It writes six supplied bytes into the selected column's vector; the supplied stack-cell address is not saved. Growth through 0x6d030, 0x6cb30 and 0x6cca0 creates or replaces vector storage through ordinary allocation and six-byte copy helpers, and frees the temporary buffer. The column's begin/end/capacity slots are at column+0x40, +0x48 and +0x50. These are heap allocations owned through an inline Zone column, not externally published child objects.

One reached branch of 0x31c1c0 calls 0x2a3140 with the Zone's World pointer from +8, scalar coordinates and scalar column values. The inspected descendants 0x2ab410 and 0x2d3150 calculate a value into caller storage and call scalar noise routine 0x2e0c0. World field reads occur in this path, but no Zone or child pointer is forwarded to those value routines and no World queue/cache insertion was identified there. This edge is not a worker dispatch. The read helper 0xe770 also has runtime static-initialization guards and writes fallback-value globals. Those effects do not retain Zone addresses, but they mean the reached code is not described here as globally side-effect-free. Allocation, deallocation and allocation/length failure paths are additional runtime effects; partial mutation on failure is not rolled back by a contract established here.

Normal cleanup is visible in Zone destructor 0x2f9940. It frees the +0x4b8 list's ordinary nodes and sentinel, clears the +0xe8 tree through 0xfca0 and frees its sentinel through 0x36bc40. Whole-tree clearing reaches 0xb9a0 and recursive node cleanup 0x87a90; these release tree-node storage, not objects named by coordinate values. The destructor also invokes column cleanup 0x6cc40 across the 0x1000 inline elements of stride 0x58. That cleanup frees the column's six-byte vector allocation through 0x6cfb0. This proves ordinary storage cleanup, not that destruction can race safely with readers or that no other generator edge escaped before these routines ran.

Neither spatial routine establishes its own general World critical-section interval. An observed frame path in 0xa5a50 acquires World+0x3c8 through 0x2abb70 at 0xa6464, calls both spatial routines at 0xa83bd and 0xa83c9, and releases through 0x2b9890 at 0xadb23. A separate observed consumer path runs 0x2e30b0, which invokes 0x31d700 on already retained Zone addresses. In hosted routine 0xf5520, 0x2e30b0 runs inside an outer hosted-object critical section at +0x5f8 and an inner World+0x3c8 interval. These helpers directly enter and leave the native critical section at the stated World offset. These observed caller intervals do not establish protection for every caller, a universal lock order or safety for concurrent unpublished temporaries. The generator's two late calls alone provide no additional guard guarantee.

Refresh retention is a distinct edge. Routine 0x31c540 explicitly supplies its Zone pointer to 0x73f40 for insertion into World+0x1a0. No corresponding insertion occurs in 0x31d470, 0x31d700 or the storage/value paths described above. Excluding that refresh registration from these two late spatial calls does not exclude it from another route or establish that the full generator is escape-free. These findings remove the inspected spatial paths as an identified external-retention mechanism; they do not independently authorize disposal of every generated unpublished Zone. Other generator callees, outstanding outside readers, complete exception cleanup and full worker/publication interactions remain unresolved.
