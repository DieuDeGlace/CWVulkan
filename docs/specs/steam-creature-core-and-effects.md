# Original Creature core scalars and effects

This bounded contract supports the original AMD64 PE32+ image cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe, 5,757,952 bytes, preferred image base 0x140000000, SHA-256 7AF18CB28C8BF848F355287428C6390B2EA2A682CA466190F5EB9859C5D6F244. Addresses are RVAs. Evidence is restricted to the original binary, native function sources, assembly and call records. No game, probe or build was run. All offsets below are relative to the Creature base unless an effect payload or node is explicitly named. Descriptive roles below do not import SDK names or establish a complete field taxonomy.

## Construction and scalar widths

Constructor 0x4ce80 invokes 0x4d600 with Creature+0x10, then constructs additional owning collections. Consequently offsets within that nested initializer must be increased by 0x10 before treating them as Creature offsets. Wide initialization stores are evidence that bytes were initialized, not evidence that the entire store is one logical field.

At +0x60, one byte is initialized to 3. Native control, hit and update consumers, including 0xbac0, 0x29dcf0, 0x2bb8e0 and 0x2de4c0, compare this byte against category values such as zero, five, six, seven and eight. It participates in authority/control and relationship-dependent branches. A complete enumeration or one universal faction interpretation is not established. Bytes +0x61 through +0x63 are not initialized by the examined constructor path and are not assigned a field meaning here.

At +0x64, four bytes are initialized to zero. Native update routines compare the four-byte value against kind codes, including 0x90 and 0x11d. It is a distinct kind selector, not part of the +0x60 byte. This audit does not identify every kind or supply its names.

At +0x68, one byte is initialized to zero. It is an action/state selector used by 0x4ead0, 0x4f220, 0xbac0, 0x2bb8e0 and 0x2de4c0. These routines select timing thresholds, transitions and movement behavior using the byte. Bytes +0x69 through +0x6b have no initialization or independent field meaning established by this constructor audit.

The eight-byte zero store beginning at +0x6c covers two independently consumed four-byte fields. The value at +0x6c is floating point: update 0x2de4c0 adds its elapsed-time argument in applicable states and compares it with action-dependent thresholds from 0x4f220; state transitions can reset it to zero. It is an action-time accumulator, not an eight-byte time value or a normalized charge fraction. The value at +0x70 is a signed four-byte counter: 0x2dba40 resets or increments it, and 0x2bb8e0 converts it to float for a ratio involving 0x5f9e0. Its complete action-specific meaning remains unproved.

The four-byte region at +0x74 is initialized to zero. Examined consumers in 0x2dba40 test the byte at +0x74, while other paths reset four bytes there. This establishes a byte-tested flag and initialization of +0x75 through +0x77; it does not establish those other three bytes as additional flags, nor prove that the logical field is a four-byte integer.

At +0x128 there is a two-byte flags field initialized to zero. Physics 0x2bb8e0 reads and modifies it as an unsigned word, including masks 0x0004, 0x0040, 0x2012 and 0x2002. Some tests read only its low byte. The high byte is therefore part of the established word, not an unrelated adjacent field inferred from a byte access. Complete bit meanings are outside this audit. Bytes +0x12a and +0x12b are not initialized by the examined constructor path and are unresolved.

At +0x12c, a four-byte floating-point value is initialized to zero. Control routine 0xbac0 can set it to 600.0 while changing the Creature's motion; physics tests it together with the action selector, and upkeep 0x2de4c0 copies it to another field, subtracts elapsed time and clamps a negative result to zero. This establishes a timed motion/action window. It does not establish an amount of charge or a resource total at this offset.

At +0x130, a four-byte float is initialized to -3000.0. Hit application 0x29dcf0 can raise it using a positive signed duration from the incoming hit record. While it remains positive, physics 0x2bb8e0 suppresses a motion contribution, and upkeep 0x2de4c0 resets action/time state. Upkeep subtracts elapsed time; this timer can be negative and is not universally clamped to zero. A stun-like action-suppression role is supported, but a complete set of causes and exemptions is not specified.

The adjacent +0x134, +0x138 and +0x13c values are separate four-byte floats, each initialized to zero. Upkeep subtracts elapsed time and clamps negative results to zero. Hit application 0x29dcf0 can raise +0x134 from another incoming duration; upkeep also sets it to 800.0 in one action termination branch. Physics tests +0x138 and +0x13c for nonzero and applies different movement factors; 0x2dba40 can raise +0x138 from a hit-related quantity. These facts establish distinct countdowns and movement-related consumers, not named fear/chill/wind categories or interchangeable timers.

At +0x140, a separate four-byte float is initialized to zero. In 0x2de4c0 a negative value approaches zero using elapsed time and a scaling constant, and an overshoot is clamped to zero. Its full purpose and the meaning of positive values remain unresolved. It must not be identified solely from its position after the countdown fields.

At +0x144, a signed four-byte level is initialized to 1; the following four-byte counter at +0x148 is initialized to zero by the same eight-byte initialization store. The level meaning has direct UI evidence: frame 0xa5a50 compares the local Creature's earlier and later +0x144 values and displays LEVEL UP! after an increase. Routine 0x666a0 reads both values as signed four-byte integers and adds +0x148 to a quantity derived from the level. This establishes a progression-related counter at +0x148; its complete award/reset policy and any universal experience interpretation are outside scope.

At +0x14c and +0x14d are independently consumed bytes, both initialized to zero. Physics and upkeep jointly test the pair to choose action/resource behavior, including pairs 4/1 and 2/0. No complete enumeration, class name or specialization taxonomy is established here. Bytes +0x14e and +0x14f are not initialized by the examined constructor path and remain unresolved.

At +0x150 and +0x154 are separate four-byte spatial/region values, initially zero under an eight-byte store. World update 0x2da6c0 derives a spatial lookup from the local Creature's position and copies two four-byte results from 0x2ada20 into these fields. Subsequent spatial processing uses the pair. Exact region units, all assignment paths and a universal zone interpretation are not established by this audit.

At +0x158 is a four-byte float initialized to zero. Physics 0x2bb8e0 accumulates into it during particular actions, can reset it on action interruption, and caps it against Creature+0x188. Control 0xbac0 compares it against amounts derived from +0x188 before advancing charged-action states, including a transition from action 0x19 to 0x17. A charge-related accumulator role is established. This does not identify it with every resource at nearby offsets or make it a general current-mana field. Bytes +0x15c through +0x15f are also zeroed by construction, but this bounded audit establishes no independent typed field or semantic role for them. They are not named a float solely because +0x158 is one.

## Effect collection and element record

Constructor 0x4ce80 constructs the effect-list header inline at +0x980. Header+0x0 is an eight-byte sentinel pointer, and header+0x8, Creature+0x988, is an eight-byte count initialized to zero. Allocator 0x4e260 allocates 0x28 bytes for the sentinel and for ordinary nodes. Each node has an eight-byte next pointer at node+0x0 and previous pointer at node+0x8. The empty sentinel links to itself. Ordinary payload begins at node+0x10 and occupies 0x18 bytes. Sentinel payload is not initialized as an effect.

This is a separately allocated doubly linked list. The 0x28 allocation size is not a stride between consecutive nodes. Traversal follows links to the sentinel. The 0x18 payload is internally contiguous, but there is no established begin/end/capacity array representation at +0x980.

Within the payload, +0x0 is a one-byte kind selector. Payload+0x4 is a four-byte float used as an effect-specific magnitude or factor. Payload+0x8 is a four-byte float decremented by upkeep's elapsed-time argument. Payload+0x10 is an eight-byte identifier: producers such as 0x29d3c0 supply an actor identifier, and upkeep 0x2de4c0 can resolve it through the World for periodic application. Payload bytes +0x1 through +0x3 and +0xc through +0xf are copied by the record-copy machinery, but no independent scalar meanings or guaranteed initialization of those bytes are established here. They must not be promoted to fields from the copy width alone.

Record copier 0x4ccb0 copies three eight-byte chunks into a fresh node payload. Its copy includes the unresolved bytes. Producers such as 0x29d3c0 explicitly set the kind byte, float magnitude, float time and identifier without establishing all copied bytes as zero. The record does not own an actor pointer merely because its identifier can later resolve to an actor.

Insertion routine 0x65390 receives the Creature in RCX, a readable 0x18-byte source record in RDX, and a byte mode in R8B under Windows x64 calling conventions. There is no separately established success/status return. It copies into owned storage rather than retaining the source-record address. A nonzero mode allows replacement; with zero mode replacement is also enabled for kind values 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 10 and 11. Replacement matches both the kind byte and the eight-byte identifier at payload+0x10, chooses the first forward match and copies the complete payload in place. That leaves the existing node position and count unchanged. When no replacement occurs it appends before the sentinel and increments the eight-byte count. Allocation/length failures are not converted into a documented failure code; the routine includes native allocation and length-error paths.

The collection is not sorted by kind or identifier. Appends preserve insertion order, while replacement preserves the replaced node's position. Different identifiers can have the same kind. Further, direct append helper 0x2d03e0 constructs a node without the replacement search, so uniqueness cannot be inferred even for an eligible kind/identifier pair. Lookup 0x5e3a0 searches forward by kind only and returns a pointer to the first matching payload, or null; it does not combine all effects of that kind or return a detached copy.

Magnitude and time semantics vary by kind. For example, 0x64d90 reads one kind's magnitude as a multiplicative factor, while upkeep 0x2de4c0 uses another kind's magnitude for periodic hit processing. Upkeep decrements the time float but has distinct handling for kinds 0x1e and 0x1f, whose removal condition also involves magnitude. Therefore a nonpositive time alone is not a universal definition of a dead record. A full effect taxonomy and recreation of update algorithms are outside scope.

## Reset, removal and lifetime

Upkeep 0x2de4c0 can clear the entire effect list when the Creature is not alive through 0x3c9c0. That clear resets sentinel links and count and frees ordinary nodes while retaining the sentinel. For individual expiry/removal, upkeep first records selected node addresses, then unlinks each selected node, decrements +0x988 and deallocates it through 0x36bc40 with a single 0x28-byte element. This is actual removal, not iterator advancement.

Broader reset routines 0x65fb0 and 0x64a90 also clear effects while retaining the sentinel. They reset different additional scalar regions; neither should be treated as a constructor-equivalent initialization of every byte in the requested ranges. In particular, constructor defaults and unresolved gaps above do not become universal reset guarantees. Routine 0x64a90 invokes nested reset 0x64c00 at Creature+0x10, so its internal offsets have the same +0x10 adjustment as the nested constructor.

Creature destructor 0x4d8b0 passes Creature+0x980 to list destructor 0xcc140, which frees ordinary nodes and the sentinel. The deleting wrapper at 0x4e0a0 invokes that destructor and can free the Creature itself when its flag's low bit is set. Effect payloads have no separate nested destructor on these examined paths. A payload pointer returned by lookup is borrowed: replacement can change its contents in place, removal or clear invalidates it, and Creature destruction invalidates the entire header and list. Node addresses and the count are not durable effect identifiers.

## Observed protection and limits

Constructor, lookup, insertion, direct append, reset and destructor routines examined here do not themselves acquire a Creature-local effect-list lock. Their ordinary scalar/link/count writes are not a lock-free publication contract. A read also depends on the Creature remaining alive, independently of the effect-list representation.

One established caller scope is frame 0xa5a50: it acquires the inline critical section at the World reached through Game+0x8, World+0x3c8, before its received-effect application at call site 0xa8445 to 0x65390 and releases that World section later in the frame. Helpers 0x2abb70 and 0x2b9890 enter and leave that section. Another established scope is hosted update 0xf5520: it holds its enclosing hosted-object section at +0x5f8, then enters its embedded World's +0x3c8 section before 0x29d980 and 0x2da6c0. World update 0x2da6c0 reaches physics 0x2bb8e0, upkeep 0x2de4c0 and hit processing 0x2dba40. Thus the inspected hosted route runs those consumers within that caller-held World section.

These are bounded native caller facts. They do not prove that every scalar/effect writer uses the same section, establish a complete lifetime/shutdown exclusion scheme, or prove a universal transitive lock order. The audit does not prescribe an engine lock, snapshot policy, resource naming scheme or effect taxonomy. Unresolved field meanings, constructor gaps, copied record bytes and unexamined writer paths remain unresolved.
