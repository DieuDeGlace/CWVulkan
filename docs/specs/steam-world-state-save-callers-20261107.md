# Steam World-state save callers and capture order

This is a bounded original-Steam audit of the directly established callers of World-state save wrapper RVA 0x2D38E0 and the reached character-save, menu-display and application-shutdown routes. The supported original x64 image is binary/cubeworld.original.exe.unpacked.exe under C:/Users/DieuDeGlace/Documents/decompiled cube world steam, base 0x140000000, timestamp 0x5D93AB0D, SHA256 7AF18CB28C8BF848F355287428C6390B2EA2A682CA466190F5EB9859C5D6F244. The image hash was checked. All addresses below are original image RVAs.

Reference filenames were selected before scoped inspection. Principal originals are decompiled/functions/1402d38e0.c, 1400f2340.c, 1400a27b0.c, 140149760.c, 14014ab50.c, 140147750.c, 14009ea10.c, 14009fdf0.c, 1400911e0.c, 140084f70.c, 1400842d0.c, 1400818d0.c, 140086630.c, 14007ea80.c, 140076160.c, 1400762d0.c, 1400890c0.c, 140089160.c, 14009af20.c, 1401355a0.c, 1402d2930.c, 1402d3e20.c, 14029b290.c, 1400f17a0.c and 1400f1f20.c. Scoped original decompiled/assembly.txt references and original image vtables establish the call and deleting/thread-entry identities. No replacement implementation or older mixed-history material is evidence.

The inspected original listing positively establishes two direct calls to 0x2D38E0: the host-loader periodic call at 0xF2D6F and its exit call at 0xF2F1E, both inside 0xF2340. No additional direct call or tail-call reference to that wrapper was established by the scoped original reference check. This is not a universal absence claim: unrecovered code, indirect function pointers, alternate callbacks and other save mechanisms remain outside that result.

Wrapper 0x2D38E0 itself tests World+0x210 for nonnull. When admitted, it serializes the supplied state value through 0x14AB50 at 0x2D3943 and submits the resulting buffer under the state key through the backend at World+0x3B8. Its directly inspected body does not walk the loaded-source map or call loaded-source recording helper 0x2D3E20. Thus this wrapper's body does not establish that a caller has first recorded every current loaded source.

The periodic host-loader route copies the embedded Host World's day/time and retained-value collections while holding World+0x3C8, leaves that guard and invokes the wrapper at 0xF2D6F. Its ordinary exit route similarly captures through 0xF1C90 at 0xF2EFF, leaves the guard and invokes the wrapper at 0xF2F1E. The state argument comes from the captured value, including the map already retained at World+0x10. Neither of these directly inspected capture bodies first iterates all currently loaded sources through 0x2D3E20. They can include values recorded earlier; this contract does not equate their capture with an automatic all-loaded-source writeback.

Character save has a distinct route at 0xA27B0. It first enters the World+0x3C8 guard for the World reached through Game+0x8. It reads the signed 32-bit selected index at Game+0x2264; a negative value causes a return after the routine's matching guard leave, without the reached character output. On the nonnegative route it constructs a temporary character, copies selected current-owner fields and collections from World+0x448, and constructs the temporary secondary record at character+0x1528. The scope here is that copy/save boundary, not a complete character-format specification.

Before releasing the World guard, 0xA27B0 also copies day/time from World+0x8/+0xC and copies collections at World+0x10, +0x20 and +0x30. The retained-source-map copy is called at 0xA2970, followed by the other two copies at 0xA2984 and 0xA2998. The routine's own World-guard leave call is at 0xA29A2. A caller that entered the guard before invoking this routine, as 0x9EA10 does, has its own later leave; this call is not asserted to end that outer scope. The directly inspected character-save body contains no loaded-source-map traversal and no call to 0x2D3E20 before these copies.

The output that follows must be distinguished from that temporary World copy. Routine 0x147750 constructs a writer around a new output buffer. At 0xA29CF the save routine calls 0x149760 with that writer and the cloned character pointer. The native arguments at that call and the inspected serializer body identify character data, including the character's secondary collections. The inspected path does not pass the temporary World state to 0x14AB50 or to 0x2D38E0.

The character clone is destroyed after serialization. The reached output is submitted through the backend at Game+0x2268 under a key formed from the selected numeric index, with Game+0x1C98 held around the reached submission. The submit call is at 0xA2AAD. The temporary World collections copied earlier are later destroyed, with cleanup calls at 0xA2B55, 0xA2B79 and 0xA2B9D. Their presence on the stack is therefore not proof that this route writes a World-state payload. This audit leaves indirect backend behavior and opaque codec details open; it does not infer a World-state save merely from the character-save operation.

Four direct character-save call sites were established. The source-generation worker 0x84F70 calls 0xA27B0 periodically at 0x85671 and on its admitted exit route at 0x8572D. The character-creation/appending route 0x9EA10 calls it at 0x9ECBF after assigning the new selected index. The menu-display route 0x9FDF0 calls it at 0x9FE02. No broader caller census is inferred from these selected original references.

In the reached periodic 0x84F70 branch, the unsigned millisecond difference must be strictly greater than 60,000, the viewed World pointer field+0x210 must be nonnull, and the current UI state values reached through Game+0x130, +0x118 and +0x170 must all be zero. A further Game byte+0x2068 test admits the call only while that byte is nonzero. The exit branch is reached after that worker observes Game+0x2068 zero; it calls character save only when those same three UI values are zero. That exit admission does not contain the periodic branch's World+0x210 test. No general pause or menu meaning is assigned to the other UI state values.

The source-generation worker's admitted character-save call is not an explicit flush of loaded source objects. Neither its periodic save branch nor its exit save branch invokes 0x2D2930 or iterates loaded sources into 0x2D3E20 immediately before the character capture. Their construction/generation work and previously recorded retained values remain separate facts.

The direct 0x9EA10 route copies the current character identity/input information, creates the new selected index, invokes character save under its reached World guard and later appends a character entry. It has no reached explicit all-loaded-source recording phase before that save in its inspected body. The callback registration reference to this route is not used to invent a universal save-button policy.

The inspected menu-display routine 0x9FDF0 saves first at 0x9FE02, then invokes disconnect routine 0x911E0 at 0x9FE0A, rebuilds the displayed character entries and activates the UI root reached through Game+0x118 while clearing selected gameplay/display fields. This describes the reached menu-return behavior without requiring a broader GUI audit. The inspected disconnect body closes and clears the reached network connection, joins its admitted receive/send thread records, and writes zero to the viewed World's byte+0x358. It does not itself clear Game+0x2068 or perform the viewed loaded-source bulk teardown. The menu route consequently does not supply a proved all-loaded-source-recording-before-character-save sequence.

A separately identified callback dispatch at 0x292320 routes its selected action value to 0x9FDF0 through 0x29234C. Another reached action routes to 0x9F660, whose original body only writes one to Game byte+0x1D68 and returns. The application loop 0x1355A0 tests that byte at 0x136447 and 0x1364BE and posts a quit request when it is nonzero. No save/capture or source-recording call is part of the inspected 0x9F660 body. This identifies a concrete shutdown-request route, not every possible window/application exit trigger.

The application-loop construction and cleanup establish the relevant viewed-World identity on the inspected successful path. Routine 0x1355A0 allocates and constructs a World through 0x29A130, then passes that World to the Game constructor 0x7EA80. The Game constructor stores it at Game+0x8. When the reached message-loop termination path is taken, it deletes that Game at 0x137BC1, performs the intervening renderer/map-resource deletion calls, and later deletes the same separately allocated viewed World at 0x137C66. Those intervening resource destructor bodies are not a complete alternative save audit.

Original Game vtable 0x436068 resolves its deleting entry to 0x86630, which reaches Game destructor 0x818D0. That destructor disconnects through 0x911E0 and writes zero to Game+0x2068 at 0x8191C. It then joins the admitted worker records. The source-generation worker record at Game+0x1FA8 is joined at 0x819BE, and the preparation-worker record at Game+0x1FB8 is joined at 0x819F0, after earlier reached joins.

These worker identities were resolved through constructor and thread-entry evidence, rather than diagnostic names. The Game constructor creates the preparation worker through 0x76160 and vtable 0x437EA8, whose entry reaches thunk 0x87BE0 and wrapper 0x890C0, then 0x842D0. It creates the source-generation worker through 0x762D0 and vtable 0x437EB8, whose entry reaches thunk 0x87BF0 and wrapper 0x89160, then 0x84F70. Both thread records carry the same Game pointer; their reached World operations follow that Game's World pointer. Successful joins establish completion by their respective returns, not an ordering between the workers' earlier exit bodies.

The preparation worker's reached exit call at 0x84F2F invokes 0x2D2930 on the viewed World. Under World+0x3F0, that routine snapshots the coordinate/source-pointer map and empties the actual loaded-source map. It then records each nonnull snapshot source through 0x2D3E20 at 0x2D2A1B while holding World+0x3C8 for that individual source, leaves the guard and destroys the source at 0x2D2A3E. The retained helper's selected-field admissions, including source bytes+0xF9/+0xFA for the two arrays, remain applicable; this is not an unconditional copy of every source byte.

That bulk recording and the source-generation worker's exit character capture belong to separate thread bodies. The common stop-byte write and the order of join calls do not prove that all preparation-worker source recording finishes before the generation worker's capture. The shared World guard admits each source-record operation and the character-copy operation separately; the bulk routine clears the loaded map before its per-source recording loop. An empty loaded map at an intermediate point is therefore not evidence that the whole retained-value update has completed.

No explicit character capture or 0x2D38E0 call follows those worker joins in the inspected Game destructor body. The separate viewed World destructor, reached later by the application loop, records any nonnull sources still present in its loaded map at 0x29B303 before deleting each source at 0x29B31A. Whether that map remains empty after the worker's earlier teardown is not generalized across untraced writers. Its destructor contains no newly established World-state save-wrapper call after that recording phase.

The Game destructor also later destroys its embedded Host at 0x8222A. That owner uses its own embedded World, constructed inside the Host at Host+0x8, whereas the viewed World was separately allocated and passed to Game+0x8 on the proved construction path. The ordinary Host destructor 0xF1F20 stops and joins its own loader before reaching that embedded World destructor. Its loader's final capture/submission therefore still precedes its embedded World destructor's still-loaded-source recording at 0x29B303. The viewed worker's recording cannot be substituted as an earlier recording phase for that different embedded World.

No additional mandatory same-instance route that first records all current loaded sources and then captures or submits World state was established in this bounded audit. This leaves such a route unresolved rather than proving that none exists. A worker capture could happen after some or all recording in a particular execution, and earlier periodic/retirement work may already have supplied matching values; neither possibility creates a required after-recording capture order. Direct calls, in-memory captures, character output and state-wrapper submission must also remain distinct from eventual disk durability.

All earlier held contracts remain immutable. Indirect/global save calls, uninspected resource callbacks, complete backend/codec behavior, every exit trigger, failure/exception paths and a universal save or synchronization policy remain outside the proved scope. No replacement implementation, history, tests, SDK/dependency source, reconstruction, owner outputs/captures or implementation results were used.
