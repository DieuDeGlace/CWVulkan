# User-authored cloud layer add-on

FINAL / STABLE / HELD. This report covers the additions in commits 83ac80b and f43b42f of the user-designated renderer. The latter commit supplies the final constants where both revisions define one. Evidence consists of added renderer lines and added cloud declarations in their matching header changes. Unchanged and removed lines were excluded.

## Candidate area and fog

The cloud fog distance is the greater of the current view distance and 900 blocks. The first revision used 480 blocks. The horizontal candidate radius is the cloud fog distance plus a visibility margin. The search extends by the ceiling of that radius divided by 200 in either direction on each lattice axis, including both ends. Within that window, the add-on rejects anchors whose horizontal distance from the camera exceeds the candidate radius. Equality passes this check. A subsequent sphere-visibility test receives the drifted three-dimensional anchor, a distinct cloud bounding radius, and the candidate distance. The numeric values of the margin and sphere radius are absent from the permitted additions. The 900-block minimum applies to fog before the margin is added. Evidence is in the renderer additions of both commits.

## Drift and sample locations

The layer starts with a double-precision elapsed-time accumulator of zero and adds the supplied step to it. The speed is 0.0012 blocks per millisecond, equivalent to 1.2 blocks per second. At draw time the add-on converts the accumulated displacement in blocks to a fixed-point coordinate. It adds that displacement to the lattice anchor's X. Y stays at the undrifted anchor and Z comes from the cell altitude. For example, a 2,000-millisecond advance represents 2.4 blocks of positive X displacement before fixed-point quantization. The additions establish conversion after multiplication and before adding the displacement to the fixed anchor. The raw fixed-unit multiplier, rounding rule, source of the frame step, and pause or restart behavior are outside the permitted lines. Evidence is in the 83ac80b renderer and cloud timer declaration and the f43b42f position construction.

The camera search subtracts the same fixed displacement from camera X. The X center index comes from the resulting block coordinate divided by 200 with floor division. A cell's undrifted X is 200 times its signed column index. The undrifted Y is 200 times its row index plus a stagger of 100 blocks in positive odd columns and minus 100 in negative odd columns. Even columns have zero stagger. The search derives a separate center row for each column by subtracting the stagger from the camera's Y block coordinate and using floor division by 200. Horizontal rejection therefore measures distance to the moving anchor. Evidence is in the 83ac80b renderer additions.

The admission test samples biome humidity and two-dimensional noise at the undrifted block X and Y. Add 0.2 to the humidity, then admit the cell if that value is at least one plus the sampled noise. Equality qualifies. The 0.2 is an additive allowance in the humidity comparison, so it can admit a cell that misses the earlier humidity threshold by up to 0.2. It does not specify a fixed percentage of sky coverage. For an admitted cell the terrain query uses those same undrifted coordinates, requests height with place shaping skipped, and clamps negative terrain height to zero. The altitude adds 200 blocks and 30 times a separate noise sample taken at 17 times the column index and 19 times the row index. The light/dark choice samples biome and area information at the undrifted block anchor, then compares the resulting undead influence strictly against a threshold. The threshold value is outside these additions. Evidence is in f43b42f and the lattice additions of 83ac80b.

## Cache and visibility gates

The cache key is the signed column and row pair. A new entry records the admission decision, including rejected cells. An admitted entry also records its clamped terrain height. When shading is first requested, the entry records the dark-model decision and marks that result known. Entries contain no drifted position or final altitude. Drift continues between cache refreshes, and requerying a given key uses its original world sample coordinates. The entire cache clears when the elapsed layer time since its last clear is strictly greater than 2,000 milliseconds or the world seed differs. Exactly 2,000 milliseconds alone preserves it. A clear records the new time and seed. Cells are repopulated as encountered, so an individual entry can live less than two seconds. Evidence is in the f43b42f renderer and cloud member additions.

The added call to draw clouds requires a clear submerged flag. That call passes the sun direction and restores the scene fog distance afterward. The added scene fog setup caps the underwater distance at the lesser of the view distance and 48 blocks in the later revision. The additions show the cloud lighting inputs and sun direction, but contain no daylight threshold, time-of-day comparison or enclosing daytime condition. Whether an unchanged outer branch suppresses clouds by night, and precisely how the submerged flag is computed, remain unestablished within this evidence. Evidence is in the 83ac80b and f43b42f renderer additions.
